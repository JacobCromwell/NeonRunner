# Owner reference art

Concept images supplied by the owner to guide the code-built models. They are **references only**:
never shipped, never loaded by the game. The `.gdignore` file keeps Godot from importing this folder,
so the images stay out of every export.

| File | What it guides | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `player_echo.jpg` | The player character (GDD §11) | Leave out the sword and the symbols on the coat's lower half (graffiti and the skull emblem). |
| `cyborg_viewing_devices.jpg` | The cyborg gangsters (GDD §9.2) | **Variant 1, "Static TV Head" Infiltrator, is the base model.** Variant 2 ("Wide-Aspect VR" Runner) is the Corporate variant and Variant 3 ("Broadcast Brute" Enforcer) the Gangland variant. Recolour every glow to the game's colour rules (see the brief). |
| `cyborg_casino_enforcer.jpg` | The Marketplace cyborg variant (GDD §9.2), and the starting point for the Golden Zone variant | Same unit as the base; recolour the screen to cold white. |
| `casino_zone.webp` | The Casino zone's skin (GDD §5, owner, October 8, 2026): walls, floor, ceiling and background | A covered casino street under a vaulted glass-and-iron roof: brass pipes along the walls, stacked balconies, neon casino signs ("Gasket's House of Chance", "The Brass Lotus"), banners hanging from the roof. Recolour every glow to the game's colour rules: the image's pink, cyan, green and orange neon may only be dim background elements, never bright glows near the track. |
| `beach_zone.jpg` | The Beach zone's skin (owner, October 9, 2026; task D10): the sand and boardwalk floor, bamboo tiki bars and surf shops for walls, the sea and sky, and the pools of water that are its gaps | Inspiration only. The colour rule wins over the picture: the pool water and the neon signs don't glow in hazard colours (no turquoise, pink, yellow or green glow), the pools' steel frames sit flush with the floor, and signs carry no words. |
| `chairman.webp` | The Chairman, Hostile Takeover's man (owner, October 17, 2026; GDD §10): the merger screens' and the Marketplace intro card's face (`chairman_face.gdshaderinc`), his figure in the locomotive's window, and his figure on the rig in the Casino's boss intro | Inspiration only, simplified (the owner): no sidearm, no pouches or straps. His lights are the Corporate brand's blue, never the picture's cyan. |
| `casino_patrons.webp` | The casino patrons of the Casino's boss intro (owner, October 17, 2026; task F2i): the wealthy woman in red (and the two women beside the Chairman, built like her), the "Lady Luck" gambler, the grimy patron, the elite high-roller; the cyber-mob enforcer is the Casino's existing cyborg look | Inspiration only, simplified (`CasinoPeople`). Nothing glows: the picture's cyan eyepieces and wrist screens are dark glass. |
