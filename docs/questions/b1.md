# Questions from B1 (campaign restructure)

Placeholders the six-zone, 15-level campaign needed. Each is marked `DESIGN-TBD` in code or data.

1. **Where each new thing appears within its level** (GDD §5, §6). The schedule places only City 1's
   cyborgs ("late in the level").
   - Placeholder: every level introduces its new features at a start of its own (`feature_starts` in
     `data/levels/*.tres`); the first pattern picked from there uses the feature, so it appears right
     after its hint.
   - City 1's cyborgs come at 60% of the level. Other introductions come at 10%, and a level that brings
     two or three things staggers them (GDD §6: one new thing at a time):
     - City 3: pulsing fences 10%, window cyborgs 35%, the hover truck 60%.
     - Gangland 1: screeches 10%, ramps 40%. Gangland 2: Octodogs 10%, speed pads 45%.
     - Gangland 3: fence generators 10%, heli drones 40%.
     - Marketplace 2: wall fences 10%, vent screeches 40%. Corporate 1: the Buzz Overdrive 10%, partial
       wall fences 50%.
   - Is staggering right, and are these the right places?

2. **Sewer screeches outside street zones** (GDD §5, §9.5). Manholes need a street.
   - Placeholder: the Marketplace, Corporate and Golden zones get wall-vent screeches only
     (`screech_vents`), from Marketplace 2's shopfront vents on. The Dead Zone's rubble street gets
     manholes and vents (`screech`).
   - Marketplace 1 has no screeches, since the table puts the shopfront vents in Marketplace 2. This is
     the one level where an earlier enemy doesn't appear.
   - How often: Marketplace 2 picks vent screeches 2.5× as often as the City's rare rate
     (`feature_weights`, as often as Gangland's vents). Corporate and the Golden Zone keep the rare rate.
     Is that the intent?

3. **The Buzz Overdrive's two zones** (GDD §9.9: "it appears in only two zones").
   - Placeholder: Corporate and the Dead Zone. It's left out of the Golden Zone.

4. **Partial wall fences** (GDD §9.1: "from the Corporate zone").

6. **Dead Zone 2, "a quiet, eerie remix"** (GDD §5).
