# B2: dangerous floor under ceilings

Questions raised while building GDD §3's changed ceiling rule (September 26, 2026: the floor under a
ceiling may be dangerous, the landing zone is always safe, the ceiling is never required and is the
easier route). Each has a placeholder marked `DESIGN-TBD` in code or data.

1. **Gauntlets under ceilings: which, how often, from when** (GDD §3, §6). The floor under a pattern's
   own ceiling is now a gauntlet in six new patterns, each a 4–4.5 s ceiling over rows about 1 s apart:
   `ceiling_over_fences` (three rows of full fences with one lane free, from difficulty 0.3),
   `ceiling_over_holes` (three rows of holes with one lane free, 0.35), `ceiling_over_gauntlet` (a
   gapped fence across every lane, holes, then two fence rows, 0.5), and with enemies
   `ceiling_over_cyborgs` (two cyborgs and a fence row, 0.35), `ceiling_over_manholes` (two manholes,
   0.3) and `ceiling_over_generator` (a generator and two fence rows it powers, 0.4). Placeholder: pick
   weights 0.15–0.35 against the plain ceiling's 1.2, so most ceilings stay plain; City 2 introduces
   ceilings with a plain one (difficulty about 0.18 there) and may show a gauntlet in its second half.
   Data in `data/patterns/prototype_patterns.json`, `cyborg.json`, `screech.json`, `generator.json`.
   Is that the right mix, and should City 2 already show one, or only from City 3?
2. **What "safe to land on" covers** (GDD §3). Placeholder (`CeilingZones`): 1.2 s at run speed from the
   ceiling's end (`hull_landing_seconds`, 21.6 m, as before) with no hole or fence in any lane and no
   floor enemy's reach in it (the screech's reach now starts 30 m before its manhole, where it springs
   out: `data/enemies/screech.tres`). A cyborg further ahead may still fire at a player who is dropping
   off a ceiling, as before B2. Should enemies also hold fire while the player drops and lands?
3. **A pad the player can step on** (GDD §3). Placeholder (`CeilingZones`): the pad's lane is free of
   holes, fences and ramps from a full jump before the pad (about 12 m) until the lift reaches the hull
   (about 8 m after the pad), and no floor enemy's reach, in any lane, touches the pad. The pad may
   still be in a lane a hazard row forces the player out of just before it; a floor runner can always
   pass it by (switch away or jump it). Enough, or should a pad's approach be clear in every lane?
4. **Ceilings the rules add now lie over the floor** (GDD §3, §9.6, §9.7; replaces "From the full build"
   item 90's clearing). The drone's pad schedule (a 3 s ceiling every 8–10 s after a level's first wave)
   and a Bad Dream chase's pads used to clear the floor under each ceiling, which emptied about half of
   every drone stretch; they now clear only the landing zone and the pad's spot, so drone and host
   levels keep much more of their floor content (the pad is the escape from it). Placeholder: yes, that
   density is intended. Is it, or should those stretches get lighter patterns?
5. **Octodogs under a ceiling** (GDD §3, §9.4). Placeholder: a dog may run and charge a floor runner
   under a ceiling; its charges keep 6 m from any pad and 25 m after a ceiling's end (as before), and it
   never winds up at a player riding the ceiling (it paces ahead, then gives up after its slack).
6. **Credits under a ceiling** (GDD §7: high-value credits in risky spots). Placeholder: unchanged. Trails

   taking the pad); the rich credits at hole edges and fences appear under ceilings like anywhere. Should
