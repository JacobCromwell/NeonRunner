# Questions from B7 (in-run pickups)

1. **A pickup of an item the player already holds** (GDD §8: breakable items are single charges;
   §10: armor, shield and grapple pickups): does it add a second charge, pay something instead, or do
   nothing? It matters most for the standard armor rule, whose final-phase pickup comes whether or not
   the player still has armor. Placeholder: the player takes it without gaining a charge (the HUD's
   icon still flashes): at most `PickupTuning.max_charges` (1) of each item, like the loadout's one
   charge per attempt and a boss's granted items (`data/tuning/pickups.tres`, marked DESIGN-TBD in
   `scripts/core/pickup_tuning.gd`). If the cap goes above 1, picked-up charges break before the
   player's own, so the stock is spent last (`Loadout.costs_stock`, DESIGN-TBD).

2. **Where a pickup appears** (GDD §10 says when the armor pickups come, not where): placeholder
   (`data/tuning/pickups.tres`, `PickupField`): 42 m ahead of the player (about 2.3 s at run speed), in
   the player's lane or the nearest fair lane at most 2 lanes away, with 12 m of floor before it and
   6 m after it free of gaps, fences, pads, ramps, ceilings and enemies, and never where a boss's
   floor warning shows an attack coming. When nothing in reach is fair, it waits until something is,
   so a due armor pickup can come a little later than the rule's 10–15 or 15–17 s. Are those the
   right distances, and is waiting (rather than, say, clearing a spot) the right answer?

3. **A missed pickup** (GDD §10, "at most once per phase" (proposed)): a pickup the player runs past
   is gone, and the armor rule's cap still counts it, so a player who misses the pickup after a break
   gets no other until the next phase (or the final phase's). Is that intended, or should a missed
   armor pickup come back once?

4. **Picked-up items and the stock** (GDD §8: breakable items break when used, then are bought
   again): placeholder: a picked-up charge belongs to the fight, like a granted item, so breaking it
   never takes one from the player's stock, and a pickup never adds to the stock
   (`Loadout.picked_up`, `Loadout.costs_stock`).

5. **The pickups' look and sound** (GDD §11; CLAUDE.md readability rules): placeholder (`Pickup`,
   `scripts/run/pickup.gdshader`, marked DESIGN-TBD): the HUD's round badge for the item standing
   upright at chest height, 1.3 m across: the item's white icon (the one the HUD and the shop show) on
   a dark disc in a white ring, with a soft halo and a faint glow on the floor under it; it bobs and
   sways and never spins; the same in every zone. Its whites carry no hazard hue, but white is also the
   100-credit gem's colour: the badge's size, shape and icon tell them apart. A soft chime when one
   appears (`pickup_appear`), a latch and chime when it's taken (`pickup`), and a first-encounter hint
   per item (`data/hints/hints.json`). Does it fit the art direction?
