# Questions from B8 (runner-style boss framework)

1. **Boss numbers** (GDD §10): each boss's health, payout in credits, defeat score, score per weak
   point, and the time bonus are open. Placeholder: `BossDef` defaults, all DESIGN-TBD
   (`scripts/campaign/boss_def.gd`): health 300 laser tier 1 shots, 500 credits, 5,000 points for the
   win and 500 per weak point, and 50 points for every second under 200 s. No boss slot sets its own
   yet (the test boss does, in `data/bosses/test_boss.tres`).

2. **Par times** (GDD §10, proposed: two and three stars for beating par times set per boss in data):
   the times themselves are open, and so is whether they differ between 3 and 5–6 lanes. Placeholder:
   150 s for two stars and 100 s for three (`BossDef.two_star_seconds`, `three_star_seconds`), the
   same on every lane count. The results screen lists them under the stars.

3. **Credits on a boss's track** (GDD §7, §10): a fight has no time limit, so credits along its track
   would pay players for stalling. Placeholder: the arena's laps carry no credits
   (`BossArena._generate`); beating the boss pays its payout instead, and dying keeps the usual 20% of
   nothing. The House's jackpot fountain (GDD §10) will need credits placed during the fight, which the
   credit field can't do yet (see the architecture notes).

4. **How long a checkpoint lasts** (GDD §10: the final fight has a checkpoint halfway): it holds for
   every retry of that run (death → summary → shop → retry, and Restart fight in the pause menu), but
   starting the fight again from the map, or quitting the game, starts it from the beginning.
   Placeholder: `RunContext.boss_resume`, copied by `RunContext.retry()`, never saved.

5. **Time and score after a checkpoint:** a win after resuming at a checkpoint counts the fight time
   and score from the attempt that reached the checkpoint plus this one's, so par times, the time bonus
   and the leaderboard compare whole fights. Placeholder: `BossEncounter` carries them
   (`boss_resume.time`, `score`), and the weapon damage dealt, so the weapon cap holds over the fight.

6. **Granted items** (GDD §8: bosses may grant power-ups before the fight): placeholder
   (`Loadout.grant`): a granted breakable item is one charge, whether or not the player brings their
   own, and using it never takes one from the player's stock; a granted permanent item is at least
   tier 1 (`"weapon:2"` for a tier). Granted items ignore the shop's equip toggle. The revive can't be
   granted (it's used from stock on the death screen). Is that the intended generosity?

7. **The standard armor rule's timing** (GDD §10): placeholder (`BossEncounter`, `armor_pickup_due`):
   the delay after a break is picked in [min, max] from a seeded stream (15–17 s; the Floating Head
   10–15 s), a break counts against the cap of the phase it happened in even if its pickup comes in the
   next phase, and the final-phase pickup also comes when a retry resumes in the final phase at a
   checkpoint. The pickups themselves are task B7's.

8. **Weapons against bosses** (GDD §10, the Floating Head: weapons chip slowly, so even the best
   weapon saves at most one stomp over the whole fight): in a fight with no time limit, only a cap can
   promise that. Placeholder: `BossDef.weapon_share_cap`, the most of a boss's health weapons can take
   over the whole fight (the Floating Head 0.34, the test boss 0.34, 1 = no cap elsewhere). Auto-fire
   stops aiming at the boss once the cap is reached. Splash damage never hurts a boss's body.

9. **Carried damage between phases:** a hit that takes more than what's left of a phase carries into
   the next one (so chip damage can save a stomp), but a single hit never takes the boss past the end
   of the next phase: every phase gets played. Placeholder: `BossEncounter.damage`.

10. **Harder difficulty tiers and bosses** (GDD §6): placeholder: a tier's run speed multiplier and
    difficulty bonus apply to the boss's arena (`Campaign.configure_boss`), and the boss's own pattern
    doesn't change. What should a harder tier do to a boss fight?

11. **Phase data for the designed bosses** (GDD §10): the boss slots now carry the phases the design
    gives: three phases each (one stomp each for the Floating Head and The House, one EMP each for Sleep
    Taker, three clamps in Hostile Takeover's last phase, the Sewer Swarm's two clusters then the rest,
    then three stomps on the Host), and two stages for the final villain with the checkpoint at the
    second. Placeholders: the Sewer Swarm's second phase as three clusters (five in all), one big hit in
    Hostile Takeover's first two phases, and every phase's pace and intro length.

12. **After the win:** the runner keeps running for two seconds while the boss's defeat plays out
    (the Floating Head crashes into the street ahead and the runner runs through the wreck), safe from
    anything still in the air; then the results, the shop, and the next step (the outro, or in the web
    demo the outro and then the store-link screen). Is a shop wanted between a boss and its zone's
    outro? Placeholder: yes, like every level.

13. **The first boss's hint:** "A boss! Only its glowing red weak points and your weapons can hurt
    it." (`data/hints/hints.json`, shown once per profile when a boss first appears). Sleep Taker, which
    weapons can't hurt, will need a hint of its own (`"enemy:dead_zone_boss"`).

14. **The boss HUD and results** (OPEN_QUESTIONS §5): placeholder: a bar top centre in the
    enemy-health red with the boss's name, its phase ("PHASE 2 · 2/3"), a marker at each phase's end,
    and a grey fill while it can't be hurt; a "Checkpoint!" hint; the results screen's fight stats
    (time, phase reached, weak points hit, kills, hits blocked, time bonus) and the par times.

15. **Boss leaderboards** (GDD §10): placeholder: one board per boss and difficulty tier,
    `boss/<boss id>/<tier>` (`RunContext.leaderboard_id`), like the levels' boards; none in the web demo.
