# C3: the Resonator

Numbers are exports on `ResonatorTuning` (`scripts/enemies/resonator_tuning.gd`, values in
`data/enemies/resonator.tres`, F6 "Enemy: Resonator") unless another file is named. Review it with
`tools/showcase/resonator_showcase.tscn` (`--view=model`, `--view=play`, `--view=close`, with `--lanes=3`,
`--skin=golden`, `--double`, `--wall`, `--reduced`), or `./play.sh --features=resonator --skin=golden`.

1. **The look** (GDD §9.10, proposed: a floating, slender golden spire ringed by 2–3 slowly turning halos
   around a red glowing core; sci-fi broadcast tech, never church-like). Placeholder (`ResonatorModel`,
   `scripts/enemies/resonator_model.gd`): a faceted, double-ended mast in the zone's gold
   (`CultEmblem.GOLD_COLOR`, unlit reflective metal) with ivory enamel collars and three small gold vanes at
   each end (the emblem's three-fold symmetry); a transmitter tip up, an emitter tip down at the floor; its
   middle opens into a cage of three gold ribs around a red crystal core, the only part that always glows.
   Three halos, each three gold arcs with ivory end nodes, tumble slowly at rest. No bell, chain, cross,
   candle or steeple shape. 5.2 m tall at `model_scale` 1.1, halos 3.6 m across; 4 draw calls, about 1,500
   triangles. Right look?
2. **The warning** (GDD §9.10: its halos spin up and line up while the chime plays). Placeholder: 1.3 s
   (`warning_seconds`). The halos spin up, and each swings into line facing the runner on one of the
   chime's three notes (inner to outer, at 0, 0.42 and 0.84 s), so they end up as a red target; the core,
   the halos' inner trims and the emitter collar glow red, brighter as the pulse nears. The wave leaves at
   the warning's end and reaches the runner about 1.1 s later, so about 2.4 s pass from the first note to
   the wave. With Reduced flashing the glow holds steady instead of throbbing, and the wave's stripes hold
   still. Right length and look?
3. **Distance and size** (GDD §9.10: it hovers far ahead). Placeholder: `hover_ahead` 34 m (within the
   weapons' 70 m), core 3.1 m above the floor (`hover_height`: too high to stomp, the spire's top under the
   ceiling), `model_scale` 1.1. On the Golden skin's bright facades the warning's red target is small at
   34 m, about 40 pixels across at 960x540, and at 3 lanes the halos overlap the walls; the chime carries
   the warning. Closer, or larger?
4. **The wave** (GDD §9.10: a red shockwave ring rolling along the floor toward the player across every
   lane). Placeholder: a straight, low crest across every lane rather than a ring (a ring 34 m out crosses
   the track almost straight); it spreads out from under the Resonator as it leaves, blended over the floor
   in enemy-fire red (so it stays red on the Golden Zone's cream and gold), with stripes rolling over it
   and a fading wake. It rolls at 11 m/s early and 14 m/s late (`wave_speed_early/_late`), closing on the
   runner at 29-32 m/s. Its hitbox is 0.45 m high and 0.5 m deep (`wave_height`, `wave_depth`; the visible
   crest 1.35 times taller): a jump started 0.1 to 0.6 s before it reaches the runner clears it, and a
   slide doesn't. It reaches 0.12 m past the outer lanes' centres (`wave_outer_reach`) and stops short of a
   wall runner's body, so the strip of floor at the walls' feet stays uncovered, as a wall rider is safe.
   Right shape, speed and height?
5. **Pulses per visit and scaling** (GDD §9.10: it leaves after a few pulses; later in the zone it pulses
   faster or sends double waves). Placeholder: both. 3 pulses early to 4 late (`pulses_early/_late`), a rest
   of 2.2 s to 1.2 s between one pulse's wave passing and the next warning (`pulse_rest_early/_late`),
   waves 11 to 14 m/s, and 0% to 50% of pulses doubled (`double_share_early/_late`), the second wave 0.9 s
   behind the first (`double_gap`: time to land and jump again, and inside the 1 s hit invulnerability, so
   armor or a shield that blocks the first wave covers the second). The early numbers apply at the
   level's `enemy_scaling` 0.85 and the late ones at 1.0 (`scaling_from`, `scaling_to`): the Golden Zone's
   own span (Golden 1 at about 0.86, Golden 3 at 1). A level's first pulse is always a single wave (one new
   thing at a time). Right counts and ramp?
6. **Toughness** (OPEN_QUESTIONS: "the Resonator's shots to kill"). Placeholder: 15 health, 15 laser tier 1
   shots like the Gilded Sentinels (`health_early/_late` in `data/enemies/resonator.tres`), score 400.
   Auto-fire targets its core. It has no contact hitbox (like the heli drone): it stays ahead, and nothing
   of it reaches the player but its waves. It can't be stomped (it hovers too high) and the claws don't
   touch it. Shot down, it breaks apart and a wave still rolling fizzles out, since its source is gone.
   Right?
7. **Protection.** GDD §9.10 says armor and the shield block a wave. Placeholder: the wave is an enemy
   attack in the shared damage rules, so armor and the shield block one, and the dash passes through it
   like any enemy attack (`dash_kills` off: a wave isn't the Resonator's body). Right for the dash?
8. **A big attack** (GDD §9, "Big attacks take turns"; OPEN_QUESTIONS, From build phase 2, 98 proposed the
   pulse is big).
   Placeholder: from its warning until its last wave has passed the player, a pulse is a big attack. It
   asks the director just before each warning; while another type's big attack is on it hovers and moves
   the rest of its visit on. After 8 s spent waiting for other attacks (`turn_wait_max`; waiting for
   clear floor doesn't count) it drops its remaining pulses and leaves, but never before its first. Over
   Golden 1-3 at 3, 5 and 6 lanes (`tools/measure/big_attacks.gd`): with turns on, no overlap, and 10 of 44
   pulses waited first, 0.8 s on average (2.3 s at most); with them off, 35 s of the 57 s of overlaps
   involved a pulse (with a drone's barrage, a Bad Dream's chase or a hover truck). Right?
9. **Never a wave on a gap or a fence** (GDD §9.10, proposed). Placeholder: the generator plans each pulse
   where, from 0.6 s before its wave meets the runner until 0.6 s after (a double's second wave included;
   `clear_before_seconds`, `clear_after_seconds`), no lane holds a gap or a live fence, and there's no
   floor enemy, no anti-grav pad within 6 m, no ceiling landing and no speed pad from the warning on, so a
   jump always has solid floor to leave from and land on. Before each warning it checks again from where
   the player really is; if the floor isn't clear it waits and moves its pulses on. Is that the right
   stretch, and should a speed pad count?
10. **Where visits go.** Placeholders: the pattern (`data/patterns/resonator.json`) has weight 1.4 and
    length 135 m, which keeps the floor clear where a visit's first two waves meet the player. A pulse looks
    up to 240 m past its earliest point for clear floor (`pulse_slack`), a visit needs at least 2 pulses
    (`min_pulses`), and one that doesn't fit tries spots 20 and 40 m earlier and 40, 80 and 120 m later
    (`MOVE_OFFSETS` in `resonator_rules.gd`) before it's left out. One Resonator at a time: the next one
    arrives no sooner than 6 s (`visit_gap_seconds`) after the last one's visit could have ended (its last
    wave, plus 8 s of waits). No pulse is planned during an Octodog's run. Drones, hover trucks
    and Bad Dream chases aren't planned around, since a chase's 20-30 s would leave few places for a visit;
    they take turns with it at run time instead. A level that must have every feature gets one Resonator
    added where a visit fits if none is left (`guarantee_one`). Result per level: Golden 1 about 2.6
    visits of 3 pulses, Golden 2 and 3 about 1.8 visits of nearly 4. Right amount?
11. **The recency curve leaves it alone** (like OPEN_QUESTIONS, From build phase 2, 148). Placeholder: `resonator` 1 in the
    curve's `max_factor` (`data/tuning/feature_recency.tres`). Boosted 4 times in Golden 1, its rules
    dropped about 30% of its picks (one visit at a time) and left their stretches empty: Golden 1 lost 1.9
    enemies and 3.4 obstacle rows a level, and Golden 3 2.7 rows (`tools/measure/level_shape.gd`). Capped,
    the curve changes nothing in the Golden Zone until the Tithe Collector and the Gilded Sentinels are
    built. Should the Golden Zone's newest enemy get a boost after all, which would mean fewer, longer
    visits, or visits placed by its rules rather than by patterns?
12. **The chime and the other sounds** (GDD §9.10: the cult's three-note chime, pleasant like a
    public-address jingle; OPEN_QUESTIONS, From build phase 2, 109: the Golden music avoids bells and
    chimes so it never sounds like it). Placeholder (`tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_resonator.gd`): `resonator_chime`, soft
    mallets on tuned metal bars with a glassy shimmer, rising G5, C6, E6 (a bright major triad, far from the
    Golden music's F sharp minor; the same every time), 1.9 s; `resonator_pulse`, a deep thump and hum as
    the wave leaves, then its rush rolling in along the floor, peaking as it reaches the runner;
    `resonator_death`, the chime's notes bending out of tune, shattering glass and a small explosion. The
    chime and the pulse play at full volume wherever it is (not positional), since they're the warning and
    the attack; the death sound comes from where it is. Right notes and feel?
13. **One at a time on screen.** Placeholder: a Resonator that arrives to pace the player sends the last
    one away after its current pulse, so two never hover together. Right?
14. **The hint** (`data/hints/hints.json`, first encounter): "When the Resonator's chime plays, a red wave
    rolls along the floor. Jump it ({jump}), or be on a wall or the ceiling." Right wording?
