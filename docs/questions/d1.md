# D1, Gangland update: questions

Placeholders built for the owner's direction (Mad Max in a cyberpunk setting, browns and tans, lived in,
hints of corporate and military funding, ceilings under buildings and overpasses). Each is marked
`DESIGN-TBD` in code; the numbers are exports on `GanglandSkin` (`scripts/world/skins/gangland_skin.gd`,
F6-tunable), with defaults in the script (`data/skins/gangland_skin.tres` overrides none of them).
This replaces the scavenger barge of `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §D item 45.

1. **Ceilings: which structures, and how do they look?** (GDD §3, §5.) GDD §5 names "undersides of decaying
   or bombed-out buildings, overpasses and similar". The placeholder builds two structures, 45% buildings
   (`ceiling_building_share`), picked per ceiling by its start distance (`GanglandCeiling`):
   - an overpass: a tagged concrete fascia under a crash barrier and railing, a sign gantry with
     billboards and corporate ads, a dead lamp post, and a gang lookout of sandbags and military crates;
   - the upper storeys of a bombed-out building bridging the street, with lit and curtained windows,
     laundry across its face and clutter on its broken roof.

   Both share the running surface: a flat concrete slab, one precast beam per lane, with dark joints and
   small warm-white work lamps along every lane seam, and the orange end band. Nothing hangs below it.
   Are these the right structures, and is the lamp-lit seam a good lane read?
2. **Narrow ceilings in Gangland** (GDD §3, task B3). A ceiling's width now comes from the lanes it covers.
   Each side either runs into the building faces or, for a side in mid-street, ends in a plain edge face
   (`GanglandCeiling.build(..., anchored_left, anchored_right)`; the skin hook passes both anchored until
   B3 says which sides reach a wall). What should a narrow ceiling be in Gangland: a slab broken off a
   building, a pedestrian bridge, something else?
3. **Time of day and weather** (GDD §5, §11). The GDD gives the palette, not the hour. The placeholder is a
   dusty dusk: a brown sky, tan dust over the horizon, a veiled pale sun and smoke columns, with brown dust
   fog (the "Environment" exports). It keeps the scene dark enough for the hazards' glow to pop. Keep it,
   or a harsher daylight?
4. **Hints of funding: which forms?** (GDD §5.) The placeholders: side streets barricaded with stacked olive
   military supply crates (stencilled codes, 30%) or corporate containers (off-white doors with a logo,
   30%) instead of rusty sheets; olive military notice boards above the wall-run band; corporate ads among
   the posters (30%); sandbags as one of the fence mounts. The corporate brand colour and logo are
   placeholders (a generic mark in `scripts/world/meshes/shaders/kit_logo.gdshaderinc`, greys): should they
   match the Corporate zone's brand (task D4)?
5. **The cult's emblem, hidden in plain sight** (GDD §5, proposed). The owner's pick (`CultEmblemChoice`,
   the Convergent Triad) is drawn by `CultEmblem` in its unlit bronze, small beside the logo on some
   container doors, as the sponsor's mark in the corner of some corporate ads, and on some crates and
   notice boards (35% of each, `cult_emblem_share`). It never glows and fades out before it spans fewer
   than about 24 pixels (radiation-trefoil look-alike at tiny sizes), so a player sees it only up close,
   mostly on ads and crates facing the approach. Is that the right amount of "hidden"?
6. **Signs of life** (GDD §5). Placeholders: graffiti pieces and tags over the lower storeys, barricades and
   fascias (60% of 4.2 x 2.8 m slots; dusty blue, steel grey, violet grey and cream with dark outlines, no
   hazard hues); 12–32% of upper windows lit, half of those curtained; balconies with laundry on 55% of tall
   buildings; rooftop tanks, antennas, dishes, crates and washing lines; bulbs strung over 60% of side
   streets; washing lines across the street at 15.4–17.4 m where both facades stand tall enough. Too busy,
   or not enough?
7. **Holes as craters** (GDD §5; §D item 47). The asphalt is now scorched toward each hole (`scorch_amount`),
   and sand drifts along the street (`sand_amount`). The holes are still one lane wide and square-cut: the
   orange edge sits on the collision edge. Enough of a "crater" read?
8. **Motion on a still street** (§D item 46). The drifting particles are now dust-coloured flecks, paper
   scraps and pale speed streaks, as before.
