# D10b (the Beach's levels: open side walls and a provisional zone): open questions

The owner asked on October 9, 2026 for the Beach's side walls to "appear about 50% of the time that they are now
currently appearing for this zone", in "much longer sections where there aren't sidewalls", so the player sees
the surroundings (GDD §5, A seventh zone). D10b gives a level its own wall-gap tuning (`LevelConfig.wall_gap_tuning`),
the Beach's open walls (`data/tuning/beach_wall_gaps.tres`, the Open walls group of `WallGapTuning`), and a
provisional Beach zone with two levels outside the campaign, played with `--level=beach/1` and `beach/2`. Every
placeholder below is marked `DESIGN-TBD` in the code or listed here for its data file.

## The open walls (`data/tuning/beach_wall_gaps.tres`)

- **How much of each wall opens** (GDD §5: "about half as often as in other zones"). Placeholder: `coverage_target`
  0.52, so each wall stands on about 48% of its level, half of the 96-97% the shared tuning leaves elsewhere. A wall
  never opens through anything it must keep (a ramp's launch and longest wall run, signs, wall fences, wall
  enemies, ceilings reaching it, wider floor gaps, the run-up and the end-clear stretch), so a wall whose keep-outs
  leave less free stands more. Measured over both levels at 3, 5 and 6 lanes: on their own seeds every wall stands
  on 48-57% of its level; over their own seeds and eight others, 106 of 108 walls stand on 40-60% (median 51%), and
  two on 64% and 66%, walls with many ramps and window cyborgs (the shared tuning leaves 97-100% standing). Is about
  half right in play, and should a wall that busy have fewer wall pieces in the Beach, or is standing more there
  fine?
- **How much is open on both walls at once** (no wall on either side: the widest view, and nothing to run along).
  Placeholder: `both_open_max` 0.3, at most 30% of the level; measured 15-30% (median 29%). Where both walls
  would be open over more, the wall with more open stands again there. Nothing in the GDD; the owner wants to see
  the surroundings, but not lose the walls.
- **The shortest open stretch and the shortest stretch of wall that stands again.** Placeholder: 2 s each
  (`open_seconds_min`, `solid_seconds_min`; 48 m at 23.8 m/s): a free stretch shorter than that keeps its wall, so
  the walls never flicker. The open stretches run from 48 m to 859 m (median 117 m), and most of the open length
  (83%) lies in stretches of 100 m and more.
- **The longest open stretch.** Placeholder: no limit. On the levels' own seeds the longest runs 566 m (24 s)
  without the left wall, at the very start of Beach 1 at 5 lanes (both walls open for 23 s there); on other seeds
  up to 859 m (36 s). Should a long one be broken up by a stretch of wall?
- **A narrower clearance around what a wall holds.** Placeholder: `clear_seconds` 0.35 s (about 8 m) around
  signs, wall fences, wall enemies and ceilings, against the shared 0.5 s (12 m), so more of each wall can open:
  in a sweep of Beach-like levels, 4 walls of 48 stood on more than 60% with 0.5 s, 1 of 48 with 0.35 s. The
  margins that time a wall run (before a ramp and
  past its longest run, either side of a wall enemy) are the shared ones. Is 8 m of wall enough around a sign, a
  wall fence's emitters or a ceiling's end?

## The provisional zone and its levels (slot-dependent, for the recommended slot after Corporate)

- **Its place in the campaign.** The zone (`data/zones/beach.tres`) is in no campaign until the owner picks a slot
  (`docs/questions/d10.md`); `test_campaign` still pins six zones. Once it has one, the zone goes into
  `data/campaign/campaign.tres`, and the campaign then sets each level's difficulty, enemy scaling, recency curve
  and feature ages itself.
- **Its features.** Placeholder: Corporate 2's list for both levels (a remix: no new enemy, since the owner wants no
  new enemy assets), with nothing introduced (no `feature_starts`). It keeps the Tithe Collector (GDD §9.12 proposes
  it skips the Dead Zone, not this zone) and the wall-vent screeches (no manholes in sand, as in Corporate). Does
  the Beach break "every zone introduces at least one new enemy" (GDD §5, still to decide)?
- **Its mix.** Placeholder: no `feature_weights` (Corporate 2's heavier military presence, 1.5 on hover trucks,
  drones and Buzz Overdrives, stays Corporate 2's). Without it, one build in 54 over the levels' own seeds and eight
  others had no Enforcer Truck (its rules only bring one in where a bait's chase has room); every build on the
  levels' own seeds has one.
- **Difficulty and enemy scaling.** Placeholder: difficulty 0.63 and 0.65, enemy scaling 0.67 and 0.69, between
  Corporate 2's (0.61, 0.64) and Dead Zone 1's (0.67, 0.71) on today's 15-level curve. In the campaign the curve would
  run over 17 levels and give them 0.6 and 0.65.
- **Run speed.** Placeholder: 23.8 m/s on the zone, between Corporate's 23.4 and the Dead Zone's 24.2 (zones step
  0.8 m/s today).
- **Lengths and density.** Placeholder, like their neighbours: 145 and 150 s, `danger_density_increase` 0.31 and
  0.33 (Corporate 2 0.29, Dead Zone 1 0.37), two wider gaps, one cyborg in a charge's path, doodads 0.7, half the
  ceilings narrow, Corporate's credit settings.
- **Seeds.** Placeholder: 701 and 702 (the seventh zone's), checked at 3, 5 and 6 lanes.
- **Names.** Placeholder: the zone "Beach" ("Bamboo tiki bars and surf shops on a sandy lane down to the sea."),
  Beach 1 "Tiki Row", Beach 2 "Sunset Strip".
- **Music.** Placeholder: Corporate's track (`music = &"corporate"`, which plays the owner's Zone 4 song in levels):
  no new songs (GDD §11). A zone id the music library doesn't list would raise an error in every run.
- **Boss and cinematics.** None yet (GDD §10: one boss per zone; a new boss is a new enemy asset).
- **Skies.** Beach 1 has no sky of its own, so it plays in the zone's daylight; the sunset sky
  (`data/skies/beach_sunset.tres`, task D10) is for the last level and is wired into `beach_2.tres` with the skin.

## Playing it before it has a slot

- **`--level=beach/1` and `beach/2`** (debug builds) play the level as quick play: restarting on death, the next seed
  after a finish, no records or wallet, no level introduction or hints, the debug keys and F6 (whose "Wall gaps"
  group edits the Beach's own numbers). It takes `--lanes`, `--seed`, `--god`, `--nofall` and `--full-loadout`. Is
  that enough for the owner's review, or should it play with the full flow (introduction, results, shop)?
