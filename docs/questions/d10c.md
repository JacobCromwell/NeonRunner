# D10c (the Beach joins the campaign): open questions

The owner decided on October 9, 2026: "put the beach between the corporate and dead zone. Keep in mind that there
will be a boss battle for the beach, but it has not yet been created. Do not worry about any new enemies at this
time. Create level names that fit the theme." D10c makes the Beach the campaign's zone 5 (`data/campaign/campaign.tres`:
City, Gangland, Marketplace, Corporate, Beach, Dead Zone, Golden Zone), with its steps intro, Tiki Tides, Sunset
Strip, boss, outro, and keeps every other level and boss exactly as it was. Every placeholder below is marked
`DESIGN-TBD` in the code or listed here for its data file.

## The difficulty curve

- **Off the curve, or the curve re-spread over 17 levels?** (GDD §6: each level slightly harder than the last.)
  Placeholder: both Beach levels are off the campaign's difficulty curve (`LevelConfig.off_curve`, read by
  `Campaign.configure`): the curve still runs 0.1 → 0.9 over the other 15 levels, which keep exactly the difficulty,
  enemy scaling, run speed, feature ages and recency they had (every layout of every other level is byte-identical
  at 3, 5 and 6 lanes), and the Beach plays at its own numbers. Re-spreading the curve over all 17 levels would
  give each step 0.05 instead of 0.057 and move every level between City 1 and Golden 3 (Corporate 2 from 0.61 to
  0.55, Dead Zone 1 from 0.67 to 0.70, the Beach's two 0.60 and 0.65), with their enemy scaling, away from what the
  owner's playtests tuned. Should the curve be re-spread later, the Beach's levels included?
- **The Beach's own difficulty and enemy scaling.** Placeholder: difficulty 0.63 and 0.65, enemy scaling 0.67 and
  0.69 (`data/levels/beach_1.tres`, `beach_2.tres`; F6), strictly between Corporate 2's (0.61, 0.64) and Dead
  Zone 1's (0.67, 0.71), rising. The steps are smaller than the curve's: about 0.02 per level instead of 0.057.
  A harder difficulty tier adds its bonus as for any level. Is that the right feel for a remix between the two?
- **Feature ages and recency** (GDD §5, the recency curve). Placeholder: the Beach's levels count every level
  before them in the order they're played (their newest things, the Tithe Collector and the Enforcer Truck, get the
  most picks), and the levels after the Beach count only the levels on the curve, so the Dead Zone's and the
  Golden Zone's picks are unchanged (`Campaign.feature_ages`). Part of the question above.
- **The completion bonus.** Placeholder: the Beach's levels take Corporate 2's place on the curve
  (`CampaignStep.level_index`), so they pay Corporate 2's completion bonus (`GameRules.completion_bonus`), and the
  Dead Zone's and the Golden Zone's bonuses are unchanged. Should the Beach's levels pay more, by their place in the
  order they're played (levels 11 and 12)?

## Saves from before the Beach

- **Continue for a save that is past Corporate.** Placeholder (the existing rule, `App.step_done` and
  `App.next_unfinished_step`, unchanged): a save that had reached the Dead Zone or beyond keeps everything it had
  open (the Beach's outro counts as done once the Dead Zone's intro is), and its Continue offers the Beach's intro,
  the first step it hasn't done. A save that had finished the game keeps its harder tiers. Should Continue offer
  the Beach to such a save, or carry on where it was?

## The Beach's slots

- **The boss** (GDD §10, "to be designed"). Placeholder: `data/bosses/beach_boss.tres`, named "The Beach's boss",
  with no scene, phases or arena: the campaign shows its placeholder card and passes through it (CONTINUE counts
  it as done, with no stars) to the Beach's outro and the Dead Zone. It carries the standard armor rule (15-17 s,
  one pickup per phase) and the default rewards, unused until it's built (the economy tool counts no payout for an
  unbuilt boss). Its fight, its name and its payout are still to be designed.
- **The cinematics** (GDD §1). Placeholder: `data/cinematics/beach_intro.tres` ("The Beach") plays the arrival
  flyover every zone's intro plays, in the Beach's look, its card "ZONE 5 · Beach"; `beach_outro.tres` ("Last
  light") is a placeholder card ("a short scene after the Beach boss, as the sun goes down, heading for the Dead
  Zone"). Corporate's outro card now heads for the Beach instead of the Dead Zone. Their beats are still to come.
- **The music** (GDD §11; the owner, October 9, 2026: no new songs for now). Placeholder: the zone's track
  `beach` (`data/audio/music_library.tres`; `DESIGN-TBD` on `MusicLibrary.zone_tracks`) borrows the Marketplace's:
  its generated loop (`marketplace.wav`, 144 BPM, -11 dB) in the intro and the boss slot, the owner's Jackpot Plaza
  in its levels, and the Marketplace's level-complete riff. The Marketplace's is the most festive of the songs
  (the Beach is "a festive cyberpunk party beach", GDD §5), and Corporate's, which D10b borrowed, would have played
  in two zones in a row. No other zone's mapping changed. Which song should the Beach play?
- **The zone's line and speed.** Placeholder (from D10b): the tagline "Bamboo tiki bars and surf shops on a sandy
  lane down to the sea.", and 23.8 m/s, between Corporate's 23.4 and the Dead Zone's 24.2 (`data/zones/beach.tres`).
