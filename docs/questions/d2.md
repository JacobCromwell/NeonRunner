# D2, Marketplace skin: questions

Placeholders the Marketplace skin (`MarketplaceSkin`, Zone 3) needed. Each is marked `DESIGN-TBD` in
code or data; the skin's numbers and colours are exports on `scripts/world/skins/marketplace_skin.gd`
(defaults in the script, the resource is `data/skins/marketplace_skin.tres`).

1. **Enemy look in the Marketplace** (GDD §9.2: sleek citizens in city zones, scavengers in grimy
   and gang zones). The market is dustier than the City but commercial and happy, so it could go
   either way. Which look do Marketplace cyborgs, hover trucks, drones and screeches wear?
   - Placeholder: the sleek city look (`enemy_variant = &"city"` in `MarketplaceSkin._init()`).

2. **Time of day and light** (GDD §5 gives the palette and mood, not the light). Is a warm, dusty
   dusk right for a "bustling, happy market"?
   - Placeholder: dusk. A periwinkle-to-rose-to-orange sky with a pale early moon, a low sun gilding
     the upper floors of the left-hand buildings (`sun_color`, `sun_strength`, `sun_line`), the
     street in evening shade, and the shops' displays lit warm (Environment group).

3. **What lies under the stall roofs** (GDD §5: gaps between the stalls). How deep should the drop
   be, and what should it show?
   - Placeholder: the market floor 6.5 m below the roofs (`market_depth`; deeper than the 4 m at
     which a fall ends the run, so a fall never visibly lands). Everything under the roofs is in
     deep shade (`gap_inside_color`): a gap shows only the dark faces of the stalls and buildings
     dropping away and the orange edge, so it reads as a hole at a glance (the stalls' lit counters
     and lamps of the first pass made a gap look like a roof).

4. **The mix of ceilings** (GDD §5: undersides of buildings, bridges and overpasses, a few ships,
   floating ads). How often should each appear?
   - Placeholder: weights 3.5 building bridging the street, 2.5 overpass running along it, 1.5
     merchant ship, 2.5 floating ad (Ceilings group). A building bridging the street needs a ceiling
     across every lane, so a narrower ceiling (task B3) becomes an overpass instead.

5. **Shop windows at wall-run height, and the citizens in them** (GDD §5: citizens in the shops
   along the low part of the walls; §9.2: window cyborgs in windows at wall-run height). The skin
   gives every shopfront a row of real windows from 0.85 m to 2.8 m, with a lit display of goods
   behind each, and the player runs across them on a wall run. Is that the right height, and how
   should D3's citizens read apart from window cyborgs (a hazard that also looks out of a window)?
   - Placeholder: `gallery_bottom` 0.85 m (above the wall vents' zone, 0 to 0.7 m), `gallery_top`
     2.8 m, `shop_depth` 0.9 m. `MarketplaceSkin.shop_windows()` lists them for D3. No citizen is
     drawn yet. The skin doesn't know where window cyborgs are: a window cyborg draws its own dark
     window with a grey frame over the shopfront (about 1.5 to 3.1 m up), covering whatever shop
     window lies behind it, and it reads apart from the lit displays around it.

6. **Decorative signs and hazard signs** (task plan D2: decorative signs stay unframed and high).
   - Placeholder: decorative signs, neon, ad boards and casino bulbs never sit below 8 m
     (`decor_min_height`), and the wall-run band above the shop windows stays calm (closed
     shutters, no lights, no signs). Hazard signs are painted shop signs (a brand mark and lettering,
     lit a little) inside the shared yellow/black frame.

7. **Electric fence mounts** (GDD §9.1: other zones use their own mounts but keep the pink crackle).
   - Placeholder: steel poles with glowing insulator caps, standing in one or two stacked wooden
     market crates at the lane edges (`MarketProps`).

8. **Motion on a still floor** (GDD §5, proposed: motion effects for still streets). The stall roofs
   don't move like the City's trucks.
   - Placeholder: drifting dust, paper scraps and speed streaks (as in Gangland, paler and fainter),
     the frame pole across the start of every stall and its scalloped hem, and strings of pennants
     and festoon lights across the street every 34 m or so, high above the ceilings (Motion group).

9. **How often the cult's emblem hides in the market** (GDD §5, proposed: hidden in plain sight in
   logos and ads in every zone).
   - Placeholder: 40% of floating ads, rooftop ad boards and casino signs carry it as a small
     warm-white neon badge in the corner where the ad's text ends, and 40% of the painted shop blade
     signs carry it in unlit bronze at their top (`emblem_share`). Never smaller than 0.9 m
     (`emblem_min_size`: smaller, its three-fold silhouette could read like the radiation trefoil),
     never an ad's main mark, and never on hazard signs.

10. **Wall-run height marks on light walls** (OPEN_QUESTIONS §D, "From the full build", item 44
    asks whether to keep them). The City's and Gangland's marks are faint glowing lines on dark
    walls; the Marketplace's walls are light.
    - Placeholder: the same lines at 2 m and 4 m, as dark, unlit paint (`wall_mark_color`).
