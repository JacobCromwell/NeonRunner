# D4: the Corporate skin

Numbers and colours are exports on `CorporateSkin` (`scripts/world/skins/corporate_skin.gd`, in the F6
panel), unless another file is named.

1. **Time of day and weather** (GDD §5 gives the palette and mood, not the hour). Placeholder: a heavy
   overcast night, a low smog deck lit a cold grey from below by the city, no moon or stars, fog from 18 m
   to 190 m (Environment group). Right for "a more oppressive Blade Runner"?
2. **The brand's colour and mark** (GDD §5: one harsh brand colour, chosen away from the hazard colours).
   Placeholder: an electric ultramarine, `brand_color` (0.14, 0.27, 1.0), about 231°. The colour rule leaves
   only blue to violet free for glowing decoration, and the UI's accents own azure (about 209°) and violet
   (about 252°), so the brand takes the middle, over 20° from each, fully saturated where they are soft.
   Blue is also the dimmest hue, so the brand never outshines a hazard. Lit, it is `brand_paint_color` on
   banners and flags and the lighter `livery_color` on train roofs. The mark (`corp_logo()` in
   `scripts/world/meshes/shaders/kit_logo.gdshaderinc`) is a rounded square with its upper-right quarter
   split off as a separate piece, generic and soulless on purpose; ads carry it with wordless lettering.
   Does the owner approve the colour and the mark, and should the corporation have a name or wordmark?
3. **Gangland's corporate hints** now carry the same mark (the containers', crates' and ads' logo comes from
   `kit_logo.gdshaderinc`), but keep their off-white and grey. Should they take the brand's blue, as lit
   paint only? (`GanglandSkin.container_colors`.)
4. **Maglev or plaza, per level** (GDD §5: "roofs of maglev trains or plazas", proposed and agreed).
   Placeholder: the zone runs on the maglev trains' roofs (`data/zones/corporate.tres` uses
   `data/skins/corporate_skin.tres`). A plaza variant is built and tested,
   `data/skins/corporate_plaza_skin.tres` (`floor_style` PLAZA: an elevated deck of polished slabs, with
   the heavier military presence of item 9), meant for Corporate 2, Checkpoint Plaza, but it isn't hooked
   up: level data is a core file. Proposal: set `skin` in `data/levels/corporate_2.tres` to the plaza skin.
5. **The trains and what a gap shows** (GDD §5). Placeholder: express carriages on a per-lane grid
   (`carriage_length` 22 m, lined up across chunk cuts), a dark slit between parallel trains
   (`train_inset` 0.14 m), grey gangway bellows between carriages, runs of olive military freight cars
   (`military_car_share` 0.15), and the brand's mark painted on 30% of corporate roofs (`roof_logo_share`).
   A gap is the space between two carriages: the orange edge on the collision edge, and below it only deep
   shade (`gap_inside_color`) down to guideways at 5.6 m and a trench at 18 m, both below the 4 m at which
   a fall ends the run, so a fall never visibly lands. The plaza's gaps are holes in the deck with the same
   shade below. Right look?
6. **Motion cues on the still floor** (GDD §5 proposal, approved for every still-floor zone). Placeholder,
   per 40 m: 20 grit and drizzle flecks, 3 paper scraps and 16 speed streaks drifting toward the player
   (Motion group), and the carriage joints passing underfoot.
7. **The calm band** (GDD §9.1: from the Corporate zone, partial wall fences cover only the low or the high
   part of the wall). Placeholder: below `band_top` (7.2 m) every wall is flush sterile cladding (dark
   granite and gunmetal between steel pilasters, or a compound's blast walls) where nothing glows, lights up
   or sticks out, so a pink wall fence never competes with a band of light; decorative signs, screens and
   lights start at `decor_min_height` (8 m); floodlights on the cornice wash the faces above; the 2 m and
   4 m wall-run height marks are pale unlit lines (`wall_mark_color`). Right heights?
8. **Generic, soulless corporate art** (GDD §5). Placeholder: glass curtain walls and fin towers 44–170 m
   tall on 16 m lots, some stepped back or behind alleys, low buildings (16%) with a roof board or a steel
   sculpture of the mark; the brand's glowing sign near the top of 45% of towers, banners on outriggers on
   40%, surveillance cameras on 50%, cold light strips up 70% of corners (`sign_share`, `banner_share`,
   `camera_share`, `strip_share`); glass skybridges 24–33 m up in 60% of 180 m stretches where towers
   tall enough stand on both sides (`skybridge_share`). Ads are wordless: the mark, charts that only go
   up, a turning globe.
9. **The military presence** (GDD §5; the owner's review: heavier in Corporate 2). Placeholder: military
   compounds for 18% of buildings (blast walls with wire, an armoured block, a watchtower whose
   searchlight points at the sky ahead and away from the street, stacked supply containers stencilled with
   the mark, a radar dome); gunships hovering 40–52 m up in 55% of 300 m stretches, their searchlights
   raking tower faces; gunships flying low as ceilings (item 10); olive freight cars (item 5). The plaza
   variant raises compounds to 30%, hovering gunships to 80% and gunship ceilings to weight 2.0.
   Searchlights never touch the lanes (a light locking onto the lanes is the Floating Head's warning,
   GDD §10). Right forms and amounts?
10. **The mix of ceilings** (GDD §5: undersides of buildings, bridges and similar, occasionally a military
    ship). Placeholder weights: a glass skyway along the street 3.0, a tower bridging the street 2.5 (only a
    ceiling across every lane; a narrower one takes the viaduct's place), a concrete viaduct with a
    military checkpoint on its deck 2.5, a gunship flying low 1.0. Every underside is one flat plated
    surface with lamps along the lane seams and the orange band at its far end.
11. **How often the cult's emblem hides here** (GDD §5, proposed: in logos and ads in every zone).
    Placeholder: 35% of the ads on street screens and roof boards carry it as a small warm-white badge in a
    lower corner, and 35% of banners at their foot in unlit bronze (`emblem_share`); never smaller than
    0.9 m (`emblem_min_size`), at most 0.4 of a screen's height (so the 3-lane street's smaller screens
    carry none), never on a hazard sign, always above 8 m.
12. **How often the cult's feed plays here** (GDD §5, Cyborg Viewing Devices). Placeholder: 35% of the big
    screens (`feed_share`): the roof boards of low buildings, and the screens that 35% of flush towers hang
    out over the street 14–18 m up, facing the runner (`street_screen_share`); brightness 0.9 and 0.85.
13. **A boss arena over the street** (GDD §10: Hostile Takeover's gunship). Placeholder: an arena skin with
    `street_screen_share`, `banner_share`, `skybridge_share` and `hover_ship_share` at 0 leaves nothing
    over the lanes above the ceilings (tested). No arena skin file exists yet; the boss task can add one.
    Are these the right things to clear?
14. **Hazards in the zone's dress** (the GDD fixes only the pink crackle and the sign frame). Placeholder:
    fences strung between slim steel security pylons on round base plates (a blue paint ring) or pylons set
    in olive barrier blocks, with the shared glowing bars and caps; hazard signs are corporate screens inside
    the yellow and black frame (`scripts/world/skins/corporate/corporate_props.gd`).
