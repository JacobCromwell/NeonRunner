# D5: the Dead Zone skin

Numbers and colours are exports on `DeadZoneSkin` (`scripts/world/skins/dead_zone_skin.gd`, in the F6
panel), unless another file is named. Play it with `--quick --skin=dead_zone --nofall` or `--level=dead_zone/1`
(`dead_zone/2` is The Hush); `tools/showcase/skin_review.tscn -- --skin=dead_zone` shows every piece, and
`tools/showcase/bad_dream_showcase.tscn -- --skin=dead_zone` the Bad Dream's chase through it.

1. **Time of day** (GDD §5 gives the palette and mood, not the hour; the Sleep Taker's defeat brings "the
   first grey dawn light", GDD §10). Placeholder: a smoke-choked night. A low pall of smoke, dark overhead
   and paler toward the horizon, where an ash-grey haze hangs and the ruins stand against it in dark
   silhouette; columns of smoke over the far skyline, glowing faintly from below by distant fires; a
   veiled moon; no stars. Distance fades into that ash-grey haze (fog 10 m to 150 m), lighter than the
   ruins, so the black Bad Dream and the burned cyborgs read as dark silhouettes against it (Environment
   group). Right hour and mood?
2. **The rubble street and its holes** (GDD §5: a rubble street, proposed, owner agreed). Placeholder:
   broken road plates (staggered, cracked, some heaved, burn marks) under pale ash lying in streaks, worn
   pale lane lines mostly buried, ash piled in the gutters, and rubble scattered over it, drawn flat (no
   chunk on the running surface stands up like an obstacle); no round shapes anywhere on it (a manhole is
   a sewer screech's lair). A gap is a collapsed hole with the orange edge of every zone right on the
   collision edge (a lip on the plate, a strip along the top of its face) and a soft orange halo along
   the far edge; inside only deep shade (`gap_inside_color`, 0.042) dropping into a dark void 7 m down
   (`void_depth`, deeper than a fall that ends the run). The ash-grey street is the lightest surface in
   the zone, so a hole reads at a glance; the suite pins the hole's inside below 35% of the darkest
   street shade. Right look?
3. **Motion cues on the still street** (the owner's review: every still floor gets them; the brief named
   ash and drifting smoke). Placeholder, per 40 m: 70 flakes of ash drifting and slowly falling, 7 faint
   puffs of smoke drifting low over the street and rising, and 12 speed streaks (Motion group; the smoke
   puffs are a new kind in the shared drift shader), with the plate seams and rubble streaming past
   underfoot. Right equivalent?
4. **The ruins and the calm band** (GDD §5: a blackened, bombed-out husk of the city; the future still
   shows in the dominant shapes). Placeholder: the Neon City's own towers, gutted: their window grids
   (burnt-out rooms, shards of smoked glass, torn holes, soot streaming up from the windows), podiums,
   dead neon banners (the City's tubes, unlit), dead roof billboards and broken skybridges; tops sheared
   off, 40% burnt down to their steel frames at the top (`skeleton_share`), side streets between them
   choked with rubble. Every face is flush and closed up to 7.2 m (`band_top`): ash-dusted cladding with
   soot tongues licking up from the street, nothing that opens, lights up, sticks out, or looks like a
   window (a window cyborg's), a vent or a manhole (the Screech's lairs); faint pale lines at the 2 m
   and 4 m wall-run heights (`wall_height_marks`). Right look? More destruction (whole blocks gone, open
   sky between ruins) would need open lots, which the walls don't have in any zone yet.
5. **Embers** (GDD §5: fires are kept minimal; colour rule: fire only as non-glowing or dim background).
   Placeholder: 35% of towers (`smoulder_share`) still have rooms smouldering 16 m up or higher
   (`ember_min_height`): in 4% of the three-window stretches of their storeys up there (`ember_share`),
   most of the rooms glow a dull orange, kept below the bloom threshold and under a third of a gap
   edge's brightness, breathing slowly (still with Reduced flashing), on the faces along the street and
   the towers' near ends; plus the faint glow under the smoke on the horizon. None near the play field.
   Too many, too few, or none at all?
6. **Smoke rising from the ruins** (GDD §5: smoke). Placeholder: a column of grey smoke billowing slowly
   up from the broken top of 22% of the ruins 20 m tall or more (`plume_share`), never lower than about
   12 m. Right amount?
7. **The ceilings** (GDD §5: only the remains of the destroyed city, such as the undersides of dead
   buildings and crumbling, charred grey bridges). Placeholder: across every lane, a crumbling charred
   bridge (an elevated road along the street: cracked fascia with bites out of its top, broken barrier,
   bent railing, a burnt-out wreck and a toppled lamp post on its deck; weight 3.0) or the burnt-out
   upper storeys of a dead tower bridging the street (2.5). Over fewer lanes (B3's narrow ceilings): a
   floor slab broken off the tower it reaches, jutting over its lanes with rebar and cables, or, reaching
   neither wall, a collapsed span hanging on bent rods from the steel gantry that still crosses the
   street. Every underside is one flat slab of charred concrete with a steel strip and a pale line on
   each lane seam, and the orange band of every zone at its far end. Right mix and forms?
8. **The cult's emblem here** (GDD §5: hidden in logos and ads in every zone, proposed). Placeholder: in
   the brushed bronze of its unlit look (never glowing), scorched and half-gone: a ragged burn front
   across it, charred at the edge, on 40% of the dead roof billboards (in a corner, 1.4 m) and at the foot
   of the dead neon banners (1.44 m) (`emblem_share`), never smaller than 0.9 m (`emblem_min_size`; the
   kit also fades it out below about 24 pixels). Is a burnt remnant of the cult right here, and how much?
9. **The cult's feed on surviving screens** (GDD §5, "Cyborg Viewing Devices"; the brief asked for a
   proposal). Placeholder: the feed still plays in the dead city, on a few surviving screens, dim and high
   up: 20% of the low buildings' roof boards (`feed_share`) and a big screen hung out over the street from
   12% of the flush towers (`feed_tower_share`), 15 m up or more (`feed_screen_bottom`), at 60% and 65%
   brightness; about 16 over 3 km of walls at 5 lanes. The burned cyborgs' flickering screens (GDD §9.2)
   suggest the broadcast still reaches them. Should it play here at all, and this sparsely?
10. **The future in the ruins:** broken skybridges, a stub jutting from each wall 18 m up or more, broken
    off short of the middle, in half the 150 m stretches where towers stand tall on both sides
    (`skybridge_share`; a boss arena can set it to 0). Enough?
11. **Hazards in the zone's dress:** fences are the same pink field between charred steel posts set in
    rubble, a broken barrier or wreckage (GDD §10 calls the fence generators the Dead Zone's last working
    machines; the fence look itself stays the one of every zone); signs are dead billboards (a dark,
    cracked screen or a burnt poster, unlit) in the yellow/black frame. OK?
12. **The other enemies' look here** (GDD §9.2 gives only the cyborgs' Dead Zone variant: the base,
    burned out). Placeholder: `enemy_variant = &"burned"`; drones, hover trucks, Octodogs and screeches
    keep their clean look (they weather only on Gangland's `&"scavenger"`). Should they be burnt or
    weathered here too? (Each would need a line in its look code, in its own task.)
13. **The Hush** (Dead Zone 2, `LevelConfig.darkness` 0.7): every piece of the skin dims with it (the
    solid, drift and smoke shaders follow `scenery_light`); the street goes from about 80 to 56 in grey
    value on screen, the walls from about 40 to 20, while hazards, triggers and enemies stay as bright.
    Dark enough?
