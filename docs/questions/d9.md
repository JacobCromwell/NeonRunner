# D9: the cult's feed and emblem in the City and Gangland skins

What the feed shows is D2's placeholder (`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §D, "From build phase 2" item 52);
these questions are about where it plays in the first two zones, and where the emblem hides in the City.

1. **Where the feed plays in the Neon City, and how often** (GDD §5, "Cyborg Viewing Devices": the same
   feed on billboards and in shop windows). The City has no shop windows at the play field, so the feed
   plays high above it, beside the ordinary neon ads. Placeholder (`CitySkin`, group "Cult feed", marked
   `DESIGN-TBD`): **35% of the low buildings' roof billboards** show it instead of their neon glyph ad
   (`feed_share`), and **40% of the towers flush with the street** hang a big screen out over the street
   (`feed_tower_share`): 4.8 m wide (16:9, at most 70% of the street's half width on narrow tracks),
   its bottom 12-16 m up, on two arms, facing the oncoming traffic. Flat on the facades, big screens were
   seen almost edge-on from the game camera and didn't read; hung out, the feed's face reads from far down
   the street. With both walls together that makes about one hung screen every 170 m of street and one
   feed billboard every 210 m. The right places and amounts?
2. **Where the emblem hides in the Neon City** (GDD §5, proposed: in logos, ads and corporate art in every
   zone). The City's ads are neon glyph billboards and banners; it has no logos as such. Placeholder
   (`CitySkin`, group "Cult emblem", `DESIGN-TBD`): on **40% of the roof billboard ads**, a sponsor's badge
   1.4 m across in the lower corner where the glyphs end, and at the foot of **40% of the towers' tall
   neon banners** a brand mark 1.44 m across; both in the emblem's own warm-white neon, never on hazard
   signs, never below 0.9 m (`emblem_min_size`), and faded out below about 24 pixels on screen like the
   feed's and Gangland's (new kit pattern `MeshKit.PAT_CULT_MARK`). That is about one every 50 m of
   street; up close it reads plainly, but from the lanes it is usually too small or too edge-on to show,
   which keeps it hidden. The unlit bronze version
   isn't used in the City (all its ads glow). Right places and amount? Should it also be a brand on the
   hover trucks' containers or the ships (it passes for a logistics mark), though those are play surfaces?
3. **Where the feed plays in Gangland, and how often** (GDD §5). Placeholder (`GanglandSkin`, group
   "Cult feed", `DESIGN-TBD`): **35% of the overpasses' gantry billboards** are salvaged screens playing
   it among the paper posters, facing the approach and framed by their board (`feed_share`), and **30% of
   the tall ruins** have a TV glowing with it in one upper window, 9.9 m up or higher: a dark room lit
   cold by a small, dim set (`feed_window_share`, `feed_window_brightness`, `feed_window_glow`). The
   corporate ads among the posters on the barricades stay paper, since they sit in the wall-run band,
   which must stay calm. Should the bombed-out buildings bridging the street (the other kind of ceiling)
   show a TV in one of their windows too? They face the approach, so it would read well.
4. **A TV in a window next to window cyborgs** (GDD §9.2, "Readability"). The feed's loop shows a
   screen-head face, so a TV in a window can look a little like a window cyborg's cold-white face.
   Placeholder: TVs stay well above the boarded-up band (window cyborgs are always in it), small, dim and
   inside a dark room, and the face shows for 7 s of the 18 s loop. Is that distinct enough, or should the
   Gangland TVs leave the face out of their loop, or go?
