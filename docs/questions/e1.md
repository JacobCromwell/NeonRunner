# Task E1: questions

## From E1d (the propaganda and the defeat)

Numbers in `data/bosses/city_boss_tuning.tres` (groups Propaganda and Defeat; the fight's length in
Bombing run and Towers) and `data/bosses/city_boss.tres` (par times). Showcase:
`tools/showcase/floating_head_showcase.tscn -- --scenario=slogans|defeat|wreck --lanes=3|5|6`.

1. **The slogans' words** (GDD §10: "a few short slogans shown as text on its face screen, so only those
   slogans need translating"; the owner writes them). Placeholders, generic cult lines after GDD §5's
   "every path leads to him": "EVERY PATH / LEADS TO HIM", "STAY / ALIGNED", "DON'T RUN. / CONVERGE.",
   "HE SEES / YOU", "RETURN TO / THE FEED" (`slogans`). Each shows with a phrase of the voice, in the
   UI's display font (Orbitron) on a dark caption band across the lower part of its face screen, under
   the eyes (whose red glow is the lasers' warning) and above the mouth (the drop's warning); two lines of
   at most 12 characters so they read from the runner's distance. The game has no translation set-up yet
   (`docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, languages at launch): the slogans are a Label3D, which Godot translates
   like the UI's labels once translations are added. Their words, how many, and where on the face?
2. **The propaganda voice** (GDD §10: "a heavily distorted announcement voice that isn't meant to be
   understood"): four phrases of made-up syllables with an announcer's rise and fall, a deep voice
   shouting through a blown loudhailer (ring-modulated, band-limited, bit-crushed, with the street's
   echo), `head_voice_1-4` in `tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_bosses.gd`. It starts when its face powers on
   (the reveal), from the face screen, 1.5-3.5 s between phrases (`voice_gap_min`/`max`), at -7.5 dB
   (under its warnings, -2 to -3 dB). It never masks a warning: while one of its attacks warns or strikes,
   one of its cues still sounds, a cyborg it dropped is about or a pulsing fence is near, it ducks 18 dB
   at once and the slogan fades; a new phrase waits until they've been over for 0.5 s. So it's heard
   mostly between attacks (about 12 phrases in a 100 s fight). The right character and amount? Should it
   rather pause than duck?
3. **The defeat's look** (GDD §10: "its face glitches, the propaganda cuts out mid-shout, and it crashes
   into the street ahead; the runner runs through the wreck"). The last stomp's cry is the propaganda
   cutting out (a rising shout that stutters, dives like a stopping tape and dies in static,
   `head_voice_cut`; no shriek). Pinned, it shakes free as after any stomp; then it lurches up 30 m in
   front of the runner, its face tearing into static for 1.4 s (steady with Reduced flashing), loses
   power (its engines die, `head_power_down`; its face collapses to a line and goes dark, its lights
   fade) and plunges forward into the street at the first stretch ahead clear of holes and fences
   (50-100 m ahead as it starts to fall; with none, it limps on up to 6 s). It crashes (`head_crash`, a
   dust cloud, a heavy shake, no flash) and breaks up: its dead face (cracked glass, the face burnt in
   faintly) falls flat in the street before the wreck, and its stern half lies sunk between the trucks
   to where it's widest, torn open at both ends, so the lanes run through it like a tunnel (dark metal
   inside, cold white sparks, grey smoke; nothing glows in a hazard colour, nothing in it hurts). The
   runner runs over its face and through the wreck. Its bow breaks away out of sight (it vanishes in the
   dust). Does "runs through the wreck" read right as a tunnel? Should the bow stay, and should the face
   show something as it lies there (a last slogan, a frozen frame)?
4. **The fight's length** (GDD §10: 60-120 s; `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` §D, item 169). Placeholders: a
   16 s first bombing run (was 17), two 8 s later runs (was 9), marked towers every 240 m (was 300), two
   attacks before it takes aim at a tower (was 3), and the laser clipping every other tower on its own
   for a runner who doesn't bait them (`fallback_after` 1, was 2). A runner who never misses takes
   about 101 s at 3, 5 and 6 lanes. A struggling runner who never baits a tower and misses each phase's
   first window takes about 234 s at 3 and 5 lanes and 254 s at 6 (no time limit, no escalation). The
   right shape, or should a struggling run be shorter (the fallback sooner, the next window's tower
   nearer)?
5. **Par times** (GDD §10, proposed: one star for winning, two and three for beating par times):
   placeholders three stars under 110 s and two under 150 s (`three_star_seconds`,
   `two_star_seconds` in `data/bosses/city_boss.tres`), so a runner who never misses gets three and
   one who misses a window or two gets two.
6. **After the win**: the results come 2 s after the runner reaches its fallen face (as they run through
   the wreck); meanwhile the runner is safe (god mode, the framework's rule), so the arena's fences in
   the way are harmless, but a hole still ends the run early (the win counts). Should the street to the
   wreck be clear too (fewer holes and fences after the last tower), or its fences go dark as it
   crashes?
