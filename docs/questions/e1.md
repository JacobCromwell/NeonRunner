# Questions from E1 (the Floating Head)

## From E1b (the face-off: eye lasers, the cyborg drop, the marked towers and the pin)

1. **The eye lasers' warning** (GDD §10, proposed: "the eyes glow and whine, then twin beams sweep
   across the lanes"). Placeholder (`scripts/bosses/floating_head/floating_head_faceoff.gd`,
   `data/bosses/city_boss_tuning.tres`): for 1.0 s (`laser_charge_seconds`) the eyes glow red (red
   only in the eyes) and whine, while thin aiming beams show where it will fire. For a sweep, red
   aim lines cross the street at the runner's spot at the beams' heights, and the beams point at the
   wall it starts from (the one further from the runner). For a drag, a red spot sits on the floor
   of the runner's lane under its face and follows their lane until the warning ends; then the red
   lane warning shows as it fires. The beams are red with a pale core and throw sparks where they
   hit. Does this read? Is 1.0 s the right warning?

2. **High and low sweeps** (GDD §10, proposed: "jump a low sweep, slide under a high one").
   Placeholder: both beams of a low sweep cross the runner's spot at 0.35 m (`sweep_low_height`):
   jump them (a slide doesn't pass under). A high sweep's beams cross at 0.85 m and 1.9 m
   (`sweep_high_height`, `sweep_high_top`), the shape of a gapped fence: only a slide passes; a jump
   can't clear the upper beam or fit between them. With a single beam at 0.85 m, a well-timed jump
   would clear it too. A sweep crosses the street wall to wall at 13 m/s (`laser_sweep_speed`). Is
   the gapped-fence shape right for "high", or should a jump also get past it?

3. **The drag** (GDD §10, proposed: "switch lanes when a beam drags down a lane"). Placeholder: it
   aims at the runner's lane, lands under its face (about 25 m ahead) and burns down that lane to
   the runner's spot in 1.0 s (`drag_seconds`). It leaves a burning line that lasts 0.8 s, 70% of a
   lane wide and 1.4 m tall (`burn_seconds`, `burn_width_share`, `burn_height`), keeping clear of a
   wall runner beside it. It fires only while a lane beside the runner is free to switch into (the
   bombs' escape rule); otherwise it powers down without firing.

4. **The face-off's rhythm and pace** (the owner found early playtests not very challenging).
   Placeholder: one attack list per phase (`faceoff_patterns`: "low,drag,high,drop,drag",
   "high,drag,low,drop,low,drag", "drag,high,low,drop,drag,high,drop"), taken in order. When the
   next attack can't start fairly (a hole or fence where a sweep would cross, no free lane for a
   drag, a cyborg's burst under way), the first one in line that can goes instead. There are 0.8 s
   between attacks (`attack_gap`) and 0.9 s to move between its stations (`move_seconds`), all divided
   by the phase's pace (1, 1.15, 1.3). On the real arena that comes to an attack about every 3 s,
   plus a tower about every 20 s once the runner baits them. These numbers are the ones to tighten.
   Should later phases also shorten the warning itself, or only speed up the rhythm?

5. **The cyborg drop** (GDD §10: "its mouth opens and drops 1–2 cyborgs onto the trucks ahead, who
   then fight like normal cyborgs"). Placeholder: it pulls back to 50 m ahead with its belly 2.6 m up
   (`drop_ahead`, `drop_height`) and opens its jaw with a grinding sound for 0.8 s (`mouth_seconds`),
   while red target circles mark where the cyborgs will land (pickups keep off them). Then it drops
   1 cyborg in the first phase and 2 in the later ones (`cyborgs_per_drop`), 0.4 s apart
   (`drop_interval`), each falling 0.6 s (`drop_fall_seconds`). They land on clear roof (the cyborg's
   own obstacle margin), each in its own lane with at least one lane free, never near a pickup. They
   are normal cyborgs from the enemy director, so the panic variant comes up 1 time in 3, as
   anywhere. Its lasers wait while one of them is still ahead of the runner, for up to 4 s
   (`drop_hold_max`). Is the red target circle right for a landing spot? Should a dropped cyborg
   never be the panic variant?

6. **A dropped cyborg's burst during an eye laser**. Placeholder: never. The lasers share the
   cyborgs' "airspace" (one big attack at a time, GDD §9): a laser attack doesn't start during a
   burst, and no burst starts during a laser attack or while it lines up a tower's drag. Should they
   ever overlap, say in the last phase, for more challenge?

7. **The marked towers** (GDD §10: "marked, cracked towers stand ahead at the roadside").
   Placeholder (`scripts/bosses/floating_head/floating_head_tower.gd`): a 40 m tower of pale concrete
   flush with the dark building fronts (`tower_height`, `tower_width`). Its head (the top 30%) juts
   2.8 m out over the street, above the ship's highest flight, because a tower flush with the facades
   can hardly be seen looking along the street. It has white painted bands, big white targets (a
   ring and a cross: the mark) on its head, dark cracks at its base, and cold white warning lights up
   its edges and under its head. It has no hitbox: it's scenery until it falls. There is one every 300 m on alternating sides, the first 240 m into each lap
   (`tower_spacing`, `tower_first`). The track stays clear of holes and fences from 90 m before a
   tower to 50 m after it (`tower_clear_before`, `tower_clear_after`). What should the mark look like
   (never in a hazard colour)? How often should towers come?

8. **Baiting a tower** (GDD §10: the player "baits the eye laser into a marked tower by leading the
   beam to that side and dodging at the last moment"). Placeholder: for each tower it pulls back to
   48 m ahead (`tower_ahead`) and times a drag so the warning ends as the tower passes its face. If
   the runner is in the outer lane on the tower's side at that moment (the aiming spot follows them
   there), the drag lands beside the tower and clips it: a bait, worth 500 points (`bait_score`,
   "Tower!"). The runner then dodges the drag as usual. A face-off shows 3 attacks before any tower
   attempt (`towers_after`); towers that pass sooner are just scenery. Is "be in the outer lane on
   the tower's side when the eyes finish charging" the right rule? Should a tower attempt look
   different from a normal drag? For now it shows only by where the ship waits, pulled back beside
   the marked tower.

9. **The fallback** (GDD §10: "the laser eventually clips a tower on its own"). Placeholder: once 2
   towers in a phase have gone by unbaited (`fallback_after`), the next tower's drag strikes the
   tower first, then swings into the runner's lane. It gives no bonus. With 300 m between towers,
   that is about 60-70 s into a face-off where the runner never baits. Should it come sooner?

10. **The pinned pose** (GDD §10: the tower "pins it low across the trucks"; task E1c needs three
    ways onto its head: running up the fallen tower like a ramp, a wall jump, and dropping from a
    ceiling's underside). Placeholder (`FloatingHead.begin_pin`, the Pinned group in the tuning):
    - The tower topples forward onto the ship over 1.1 s (`tower_fall_seconds`) as the ship brakes
      to a stop under it.
    - The tower lands on the crown 9 m behind the face (`pin_rest_offset`, behind the weak points)
      and slams the ship down.
    - The ship sinks between the trucks until the tops of its weak points' sockets are 2.5 m up
      (`pin_top_height`; the crown's top surface is about 2.5 m too), rolled 5° toward the tower
      (`pin_roll_degrees`), and lies still on the track.
    - Its face is mostly hidden below the roofs.
    - For scale: a wall jump from a fresh wall entry peaks about 2.9 m up, the hover truck's roof is
      2.2 m, and a ceiling is 6 m.
    - Its weak points and crown top stay off until E1c.
    - The fallen tower lies diagonally, from its stump at the roadside across the crown.

    The other options were tilting it nose-down with its face showing, or shrinking it. Does
    sinking read well? Is it all right that the face is hidden while it's pinned? Should the tower
    fall so E1c can use it as a straight ramp?

11. **Until E1c: it shakes free** (a placeholder, noted for E1c). The ship shakes free once the
    runner is 12 m from its face (`pin_release_gap`). The tower crumbles away, the ship rises back in
    front of the runner in 1.1 s (`release_seconds`), and the face-off goes on. E1c replaces this with
    the stomp windows.

12. **Its sounds** (GDD §11). Placeholder (`tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_bosses.gd`):
    - the eyes' whine (a rising, throbbing charge that ends in a click);
    - the beams' zap and buzz;
    - the jaw's grinding ratchet and clank;
    - a dropped cyborg's landing clank;
    - the tower's crack and groan as the laser clips it;
    - its crash onto the ship.

    The warnings (the whine, the jaw and the crack) sound the same every time.
