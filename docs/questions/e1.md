# Questions from task E1 (the Floating Head)

## From E1c (the stomp windows)

1. **The fallen tower as a ramp** (GDD §10: "run up the fallen tower like a ramp"). As the tower crashes
   onto the ship, a broken slab of it slams down in the weak point's lane nearest the tower's wall: 14 m
   long, its foot on the trucks, its top end resting on the ship's crown just past its face, 0.8 m above
   it, so the runner runs off its end and drops onto the weak point. It looks like a slab of the tower
   (pale concrete, a row of its windows and a white band along its sides, a torn top end with rebar)
   with the City's green ramp chevrons up its middle and white edge lines: green says "go up here" like
   the zone's ramps, though it gives no boost and no wall launch. Does green read right on a tower that
   isn't a ramp pad, or should it be white paint (the tower's own mark)? A jump at its top overshoots the
   weak point: is "run off the end" the skill, or should the ramp end nearer the weak point?
   *Placeholder:* `FloatingHeadRamp`; `data/bosses/city_boss_tuning.tres`, Ramp window: `ramp_length` 14,
   `ramp_lift` 0.8, `ramp_overhang` 0.8, `ramp_slam_seconds` 0.3; its slam sound `ramp_slam`.
2. **The tower breaks as it lands** (GDD §10: "the tower topples onto the ship and pins it"). Lying from
   its stump at the wall across the lanes onto the crown, the whole tower would be in the way of every
   route, and it would cover a weak point at 3 and 6 lanes. So it breaks 2.5 m behind the weak points'
   stomp boxes: the part behind stays on the ship's back and drops away when it shakes free; the part in
   front drops away in dust as it lands (its slab makes the ramp in the first phase). Does that read as
   the same tower? *Placeholder:* `FloatingHeadTower.break_at`, `tower_break_after` 2.5.
3. **The weak points** (GDD §10: "glowing red on top of the head, the same language as the hover truck's
   weak point"). One over each lane whose middle lies within 38% of the ship's width from its centre
   line, so a stomp comes down in a lane: 3 at 3 and 5 lanes, 4 at 6 lanes. Each is a red dome that rises
   out of its socket as its armoured cover swings back, pulsing slowly (a steady glow with Reduced
   flashing), with a hydraulic hiss and a rising four-note "target" arpeggio. A stomp counts over a
   generous box: 2.0 m wide, 3.0 m along the track, from 0.2 m under the socket's top to 0.55 m above it
   (a runner coming down with their feet within the stomp tolerance under its top). Right number, and
   right size? *Placeholder:* Stomp windows: `weak_point_reach` 0.38, `stomp_width` 2.0, `stomp_depth`
   3.0, `stomp_top` 0.55; the sound `head_weak_open`.
4. **Pinned, the ship is the way up's floor** (the owner: collision stays physical on every surface).
   While it's pinned its crown is a floor exactly where its hull is drawn, and its hull stops being
   deadly (the window closes before a runner on the trucks can reach its face). It still rolls 5° toward
   the tower, now about its crown so the weak points stay over their lanes; the tower-side weak point
   sits lower (its socket 1.5 m up at 3 lanes, 2.1 m at 5, 1.7 m at 6, the others 1.9-2.5 m), so that
   side is easier. All right?
5. **When a window closes** (GDD §10: "no time limit, no escalation"; "if the player doesn't land the
   hits, the fight keeps cycling"). There's no timer: it stays pinned until the runner stomps or can't
   any more: still down on the trucks (feet under 0.5 m) within 8 m of its face, where no jump from the
   trucks reaches a weak point, or 1 m past the weak points' stomp boxes. Then it shakes free (it lurches
   8 m further ahead of the runner and 0.4 m up over 0.7 s, so a runner on its crown drops off behind
   it), rises back in front in 1.1 s, and the face-off goes on: towers again, the same window at the same
   pace. It means committing to the wall route before the 8 m line. Right lines, and is it clear enough
   that the chance is gone? *Placeholder:* Stomp windows: `window_release_gap` 8, `window_floor_height`
   0.5, `window_pass_margin` 1, `shake_seconds` 0.7, `shake_ahead` 8, `shake_lift` 0.4; Pinned:
   `release_seconds` 1.1; the sound `head_shake_free`.
6. **After a stomp** (GDD §10: "it shakes free, shrieks and rises"): a distorted, glitching mechanical
   scream through its loudspeakers with a burst of red sparks and its face glitching, the same lurch free
   of the tower, then the rise to the next phase's bombing station within that phase's 2.5 s intro. Is
   the shriek the right sound, or should it carry the propaganda voice (task E1d)? *Placeholder:* the
   sound `head_shriek`.
7. **The wall jump** (the second way up). A wall jump off a fresh wall entry peaks about 2.9 m up, which
   reaches the weak points from either wall at every lane count; at 5 and 6 lanes the outer lanes have
   no weak point, so it takes a second move inward in the air. The wall away from the tower is harder
   (its weak point sits higher): enter the wall within about 15 m of the face and jump 1-3.5 m before it.
   Is that the right difficulty? A wall jump also works in the first and third phases (nothing blocks the
   walls): should the other walls be taken away so each phase's own way is the only one?
8. **The ceiling** (the third way up, "ride a ship's underside via an anti-grav pad and drop onto it
   when the hull ends"). As the tower falls, anti-grav pads light up in every lane 26 m before where the
   ship's face will lie (about 1.6 s to see them, with a sound), and a ceiling section (the City's ship
   underside) lowers in over them from 9 m above once the ship's face is past where it will end (so it
   never reaches into the ship's space), ending 5.5 m before its face: riding it, the runner picks a
   weak point's lane, and the drop off its end lands on the weak points at every lane count. Should the
   pads be in every lane (a runner on the trucks then can't miss them unless they jump)? Should the
   ceiling arrive some other way (a ship flying in overhead)? *Placeholder:* Ceiling window:
   `pad_before_face` 26, `ceiling_end_before_face` 5.5, `ceiling_lower_seconds` 0.5; the sounds
   `pads_light`, `ceiling_lower`.
9. **The order of the ways up** is data (`stomp_routes`: ramp, wall, ceiling, the GDD's order). A missed
   window always comes back with the same way up: never harder, but also never easier. Should a
   struggling player ever be offered an easier way?
10. **Pickups and the pin** (GDD §10: the standard armor rule with 10-15 s). An armor pickup that falls
    due while a marked tower is being lined up or the ship is pinned waits until the ship is back in
    the air (up to about 8 s later), so it never lands where the pin and its way up go; a marked tower
    whose pin would land on a pickup (or on a cyborg it dropped) goes by as scenery; a cyborg still
    under the ship as it crashes down is crushed. All right?
11. **Weapons** (GDD §10: "weapons chip away slowly (tuned so even the best weapon saves at most one
    stomp over the whole fight)"): with 300 health and the framework's cap of 34%, weapons alone can end
    one phase and no more. With the owner's "not very challenging" in mind, should they be slower still?
12. **The fight's length** (GDD §10: 60-120 s): a runner who never misses takes about 134 s from the
    entrance to the last stomp (a 17 s first bombing run, two 9 s later runs, three face-offs until a
    tower, three windows). To tighten it: shorter or fewer bombing runs, fewer attacks before a tower
    (`towers_after` 3), or towers closer together?
13. **Edge cases after a lost window**: at 3 lanes a runner who slides all the way down a wall beside the
    pinned ship lands on the trucks inside its hull for a moment before it shakes free; at 6 lanes a
    runner who drops off the ceiling into the outer lane on the tower's side lands where the rolled hull
    dips under the roofs. Both only happen once the chance is gone, and nothing hurts. Acceptable?
14. **The defeat for now**: after the third stomp the ship bursts and is gone at once and what's left of
    the tower drops away (task E1d builds the crash into the street).
