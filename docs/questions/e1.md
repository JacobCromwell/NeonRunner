# Questions from E1 (the Floating Head)

## From E1a (the ship and its face, the entrance, the bombing run, the reveal)

1. **The ship and its face** (GDD §10: a giant ship whose back is a giant cybernetic propaganda face).
   Placeholder (`scripts/bosses/floating_head/floating_head_model.gd` and
   `floating_head_face.gdshader`, marked DESIGN-TBD): a hull shaped like a huge head seen from behind
   (a rounded crown, cheeks, a narrower chin), dark gunmetal with cold white and blue lights. Its
   stern, the side that faces the player, is the face: a rounded visor screen with a face drawn in
   cold white LED dots (stern eyes whose pupils follow the runner and blink, heavy brows that come
   down in anger, a nose line and circuit traces), soft static and a slow rolling bar. Below the
   screen is a hinged jaw with a lip line that lights up with the face (the mouth for E1b's cyborg
   drop), with slatted engine vents at its corners. Loudspeaker "ears" with glowing rims stand out at
   its sides. Underneath are a searchlight on a gimbal under its chin, a bomb bay behind that, and
   lift pads that throw a glow on the street. Three red weak points sit on its crown under armoured
   covers; they're shown only while it's pinned (E1c). The screen is dark glass until the reveal.
   Does the look fit? Should the face stay in the cult's cold-white screen language, or have a colour
   of its own (never a hazard colour)?

2. **Its size** (GDD §10: a giant ship; the camera sees about 102° across). Placeholder
   (`data/bosses/city_boss_tuning.tres`): it fills the street between the walls, less 0.6 m on each
   side (`street_margin`): 6.6 m wide at 3 lanes, 11.4 m at 5 and 13.8 m at 6. It is 24 m long
   (`hull_length`) and 10.5 to 12 m tall (`head_height_narrow`, `head_height_wide`, by street width).
   Because it fills the street, the arena's walls carry no signs (`FloatingHead._plan_lap`). Is a
   street-filling ship the right scale? A bigger ship would have to fly above the buildings, and
   then its face and weak points are hard to read on a phone.

3. **The entrance** (GDD §10: the ship flies in overhead). Placeholder: the first phase's intro
   (4 s, `BossPhase.intro_seconds` in `data/bosses/city_boss.tres`). It starts 42 m behind the runner
   with its belly 10.5 m up, out of sight, with a jet roar (`head_flyover`). It sweeps over the runner
   (the camera shakes as it passes) and eases into its bombing station, its stern 34 m ahead and its
   belly 12 m up (`enter_behind`, `enter_height`, `station_ahead`, `station_height`). It attacks
   nothing on the way, and the run starts when it gets there. Later phases' intros are 2.5 s.

4. **The searchlight** (GDD §10, proposed: a searchlight sweeps the lanes and the bombs fall where it
   lingers, with a falling whistle, so the light is the visual warning). Placeholder
   (`scripts/bosses/floating_head/floating_head_bombing.gd`, `data/bosses/city_boss_tuning.tres`):
   - The light hunts the runner. Its spot runs on the floor where the runner will be when a bomb
     lands (about 22 m ahead).
   - After each blast it swings away 1 or 2 lanes (a seeded pick) at 10 m/s, sweeps back to the
     runner's lane, and rests there 0.12 s. It sweeps for at least 0.6 s between locks.
   - Then it locks on: the warning. The light turns from white to red, the framework's red target
     circle marks the spot, a clack-and-alarm sound plays (`searchlight_lock`), and a bomb falls from
     the bay with its falling whistle (`bomb_whistle`, 0.9 s). The blast comes 1.1 s after the lock
     (`lock_seconds`).
   - The bomb is aimed where the runner would be 0.1 s after the blast, so a runner who doesn't move
     is hit.
   - Every third lock covers two lanes side by side (`straddle_every`): two bombs, and the free side
     is the way out.

   That makes 9 locks in the 17 s first run, 3 of them over two lanes: 12 bombs for a runner who
   dodges each one (at 3, 5 and 6 lanes alike). Should the light hunt the player like this, or
   sweep a fixed pattern and bomb wherever it happens to linger? Is 1.1 s the right warning? Is it
   fine that the sweeping light is white (no hazard colour) and only the locked light is red?

5. **The blast** (GDD §10). Placeholder: a fireball 1.1 m in radius. Its hitbox burns for 0.35 s and
   is 0.7 of a lane wide, 1.9 m deep and 2.4 m tall, too tall to jump, so switching lanes is the
   dodge (`blast_seconds`, `blast_width_share`, `blast_depth`, `blast_height`). It is an enemy attack
   under the shared damage rules: armor and the shield block it, the dash passes through it, and claws
   don't help. A wall runner beside a blast in an outer lane is safe, because the blast keeps clear of
   the wall. Should a jump, a slide or a wall run ever be a planned way to dodge a bomb?

6. **Where bombs may fall** (fairness). Placeholder (the Fairness group in the tuning). A bomb only
   falls where:
   - the lanes it strikes are clear roof, free of holes and fences from 8 m before the blast to 5 m
     after it;
   - a lane it doesn't strike, at most 2 lanes away, is free of holes and fences from the runner to
     5 m past the blast, along with every lane on the way;
   - it isn't under a ceiling;
   - no pickup waits in that lane within 3 m.

   Otherwise the light keeps hunting, so a runner hemmed in by fences may see no bombs for a moment.
   Are these the right rules?

7. **The later, shorter runs** (GDD §10: once or twice during the fight it rises for another bombing
   run, shorter than the first). Placeholder (`later_runs` 2, `later_run_seconds` 9 s): at the start of
   phases 2 and 3, after it shakes free and rises (E1c). They run at the phase's pace (1.15 and 1.3),
   so the warning shrinks to 0.96 s and then 0.85 s. When should they come (at a phase's start, or in
   the middle of a face-off), how long should they be, and should "the next phase is faster" also
   shorten the warnings?

8. **The reveal** (GDD §10: the ship finishes its flyby directly in front of the player, and its back
   turns out to be the face). Placeholder: after the first run it drops in front of the runner over 3 s,
   to 26 m ahead with its belly 3 m up (`descend_seconds`, `face_ahead`, `face_height`). That is above
   the fences, so the track stays in view under it. Over the last 1.6 s (`boot_seconds`) its screen
   powers on: a thin bright line opens into static, then the face. The sound is a picture tube's thunk
   and whine, static and a loudspeaker blare (`head_reveal`). Until E1b's face-off it hovers there,
   watching the runner. Is the height right? A lower ship hides the track ahead; a higher one puts its
   face near the top of the screen.

9. **The arena** (GDD §10: the fight runs over the trucks; handmade arenas are allowed within the
   generator). Placeholder (`data/bosses/city_boss.tres`): the City's truck roofs with gaps, fences and
   pulsing fences at difficulty 0.3 (seed 1101), in 3 laps of 60 s, opening with 80 m of clear roof. It
   has no signs on its walls (question 2), no ceilings (the ship hovers lower than a ceiling would be;
   E1c's third phase will need one to drop from) and no enemies of its own (E1b's cyborgs come from its
   mouth). Its City look (`data/bosses/city_boss_skin.tres`) hangs none of the towers' big feed screens
   out over the street (`feed_tower_share` 0), since the ship flies through that space; the feed
   still plays on the roof billboards. Is that the right mix, and how hard should the arena itself be
   under the bombs?

10. **Its sounds** (GDD §11; CLAUDE.md: every attack has an audio warning). Placeholders, generated in
    `tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_bosses.gd`:
    - `head_flyover`: an engine drone and turbine whine that swell, pass overhead with a Doppler drop
      and a power chord, and roll away.
    - `searchlight_on`: a relay's clunk, then an arc lamp's rising hum.
    - `searchlight_lock`: a gimbal clack and a two-tone alarm.
    - `bomb_whistle`: a classic falling whistle.
    - `bomb_blast`: a crack, a deep boom and debris.
    - `head_reveal`: a picture tube's thunk and whine, static, and a distorted blare.

    Do they fit the game's sound?
