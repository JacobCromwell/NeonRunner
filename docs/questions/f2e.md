# Questions from F2e: the Beach's outro

The owner's beats (October 15, 2026) are built as `BeachOutro` (`scripts/cinematics/beach_outro/`, numbers in
`data/cinematics/beach_outro_tuning.tres`). These are the staging choices the beats leave open, each built as the
smallest reasonable placeholder (`DESIGN-TBD` in the code); none blocks other work.

1. **Where it happens.** The fight ends on a tiki roof high over the sea; the outro opens back down on the promenade,
   on a stretch in the fight's look (the arena's open shore on both sides, under Sunset Strip's sunset sky,
   `CineStageDef.after_fight`). Should it instead open where the fight ended (up on the roof) and get the runner down?
2. **Mecha Guppy swimming away.** It leaps out of the sea once more (the defeat's breach, smaller: scale 1.1 against the
   fight's 1.5) about 58 m out, dives back in and swims off with its back and dorsal fin cutting the water. Is one leap
   right, or should it only swim off (a fin)? No Captain Cogs: the fight ended with him eaten.
3. **The screech friend.** It is the Gangland outro's passenger (still in Gangland's grimy look), sitting at the
   runner's feet, trotting beside them to the bar, hopping up onto the counter, and running and jumping into the pool
   with them. Its spines keep the screeches' red-orange glow, as in the Gangland outro. Keep its look?
4. **The bar.** The Beach's tiki bar kiosk (the zone's large doodad) built bigger and scaled to the characters, at the
   street's left edge with the sea behind it, dressed with a wordless chalk menu board (cocktail pictograms and lines,
   since the Beach's signs have no words), a small violet neon cocktail glass, two bamboo stools and two paper lanterns.
   Should the menu have words?
5. **The bartender** is the volleyball rival (same look, 1.4 m tall). He asks only with a gesture (no voice line). Does
   the owner want a voice or a speech card for "what'll it be"?
6. **The drinks**: two tall sunset-coloured cocktails with a pineapple wedge, a straw and a paper umbrella, one for the
   runner and one for the screech, set on the counter while the scene is away at the palace.
7. **The Golden Palace room**: a closed room of near-black panels with three walls of the feed's screens (three rows of
   nine, showing the Grand Court's own feed: the calm golden face and the cult's emblem). The Golden Convergence is the
   golden suit at 0.3 of its fight size, its cape gathered in a little so the screens show, the scene's light dimmed to
   0.25. It raises its right arm and points its index finger straight ahead (a finger added to the suit's fist); the
   camera swings to a three-quarter view as it points so the finger reads. Is the suit right here, or should it be The
   Magnate, or a face on a screen?
8. **The ships**: three of the fight's refill ships in a V, 44 m up, at 24 m/s. Their bombs are their racks' missiles,
   dropped nose first. Eight bombs: three out on the beach beyond the bar, then one to each side of the runner in turn
   as they run (the first on the screen's left), one by the pool, and the last on the pool as they go in.
9. **Explosion sizes** are the shared fireball at 3 to 5 m. The fire that fills the picture is a fade to fire-orange
   over 0.3 s (0.75 s and darker with Reduced flashing), then to black over 0.7 s.
10. **Sound.** No music: the fight's track fades out as it opens, and the scene plays on sound effects only (the game's
    own, plus two new ones: `bar_shaker`, the cocktail shaker, and `bar_glass`, the drinks set down). Should the outro
    have music (a calm beach track at the bar, a sting at the palace)?
11. **Length**: 39.6 s, longer than GDD §1's 5–15 s cinematics, because the beats are many. Should any beat be cut?
12. **The hand-off to the Dead Zone.** It ends on the fire then black, and the Dead Zone's intro opens on the runner
    lying in a smoking crater. Is the pool explosion meant to be what makes that crater (the GDD's open question about
    what puts them there)?
