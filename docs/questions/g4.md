## G4: laser tier 1, a shorter range and two more shots

1. **Laser tier 1's exact range** (GDD §8 damage reference, owner's September 30, 2026 playtest: "a
   shorter range... so enemies get close enough to be a threat before they fall"; the brief set the
   approach at about 60% of the old range). **Placeholder:** `PowerupTuning.weapon_range` tier 1 is
   42 m (was 70 m, shared with every tier), about 2.3 s of approach at the 18 m/s base run speed
   (down from about 3.9 s). Tiers 2–4 keep 70 m, since they shared the old value (GDD's rule: "higher
   tiers keep their range unless they share the value"). Is 42 m (60%) the right amount, or should it
   be shorter/longer?

2. **Tiers 2–4 shots to kill can't all stay exactly "unchanged" once tier 1's health rises by a flat
   +2 per enemy** (GDD §8: "higher tiers keep today's shots to kill"). Every weapon tier's damage is
   one shared number across every enemy (`PowerupTuning.weapon_damage`), but the +2 bump changes
   enemies' health by different ratios (15→17 for the hover truck and heli drone, 5→7 for the
   Octodog, 3→5 and 5→7 for the cyborg and window cyborg). No single tier 2, 3 or 4 damage value can
   hold every one of those enemies' old shot counts unchanged at once (worked out: the three
   constraints from the 3 different health deltas don't overlap for any tier). **Placeholder:** only
   raised missile tier 4's damage (3.0 → 3.5 in `data/tuning/powerups.tres`), which keeps the one
   pair the GDD's table actually pins down (the hover truck and heli drone, still 5 missile tier 4
   shots each, since they share the same health). Left tiers 2 and 3 alone (no GDD number to match),
   so most enemies now take one more shot at tiers 2 and 3 than before, and the Octodog and cyborg one
   more at tier 4 too. Is that an acceptable side effect, or should some enemies' health not have
   risen by exactly +2 so the higher tiers line up better (or should tiers 2–4 get their own per-enemy
   numbers instead of one shared curve)?
