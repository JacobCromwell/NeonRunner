# H1: Gilded Sentinels, the warning halved and the live statue and its niche brought forward

The owner's two requests (October 8, 2026, GDD §9.11) fix the warning (0.6 s) and the goal (the live statue
and the back of its niche further forward, out of the arch's shadow, so the player can make out what it is).
How it is done is left to the build; these are the choices made, each a placeholder to confirm in playtest.

- **Is the live niche the lit one, while the decorative alcoves stay dark?** The owner's request is about the
  live Sentinels; decorative wall-base statues were left as they were. Seen from down the street the live
  niche is now a lit bronze chamber and the decorative alcoves (also at wall-run height, 2.0 m wide) are
  still near-black arches, which also tells the live one apart. Should the decorative alcoves be lightened
  too (then the live one is only told by its red eyes and its narrower opening)? (GDD §9.11, §5.)
  **Placeholder:** `GoldenStatue.LIT_BACK`, `LIT_SIDES`, `LIT_CEILING` (`DESIGN-TBD`) for the live niche
  only, `recess(..., lit = true)`; the decorative `recess()` is unchanged.
- **How far forward, and how wide?** Physically the statue can't come out of the wall: a wall runner's body
  lies along the face and the solid body hitbox is behind it (GDD §3: what looks like a hit is a hit). So the
  front of the statue stands almost flush with the face (0.02 m behind it, measured from its real outline)
  and the niche is shallower (0.78 m, from 0.9) and wider (1.8 m, from 1.4: more of the statue shows through
  the opening at a grazing angle). A 3-lane street, where the camera is closest to the wall, still shows only
  a sliver of the statue from far down the street; it reads from about 15 m. Is that enough, or should the
  opening be wider still (the decorative alcoves are 2.0 m)? **Placeholder:** `GildedSentinelTuning`
  `niche_width` 1.8, `niche_depth` 0.78, `statue_inset` 0.02 (`data/enemies/gilded_sentinel.tres`).
- **Decorative alcoves beside a live niche are left out.** A decorative alcove that would touch or crowd a
  live niche (less than 0.84 m of wall between the frames, `GoldenSkin.NICHE_CLEARANCE`) is not built, so
  the live niche is never overlapped by a decorative one (2 of 32 Sentinels in Golden 2 and the Palace
  overlapped one before, and the wider opening would add more) and has clear wall around it. Is losing the
  odd decorative statue next to a live one fine? **Placeholder:** `GoldenSkin.crowds_niche()` (`DESIGN-TBD`
  clearance), used by `GoldenFacades` and `GoldenPalaceWalls`.
- **The eyes glow a little more at rest.** So the live statue is picked out from the decorative ones before it
  warns: idle eye glow 1.6 (from 0.9), still well under the flare's 6. **Placeholder:** `GildedSentinel.EYES_IDLE`
  (`DESIGN-TBD`).
- **The halberd's draw-back takes half of the shorter warning.** The eyes flare through all 0.6 s and the draw-back
  takes the last 0.3 s (it was a fixed 0.55 s of 1.2 s), so the eyes and the grinding come first. **Placeholder:**
  `GildedSentinelTuning.raise_share` 0.5.
- **The generator's keep-out for a Sentinel now starts 0.1 s before its warning** (`escape_lead_seconds` 0.7 s,
  the escape lane's clear lead, is longer than the new warning), so nothing is placed in the lane beside it
  just before. No design change; mentioned because Golden 2 and the Palace lay out slightly differently.
