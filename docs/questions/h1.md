# H1: Gilded Sentinels, the warning halved and the live statue and its niche brought forward

The owner's two requests (October 8, 2026, GDD §9.11) fix the warning (0.6 s) and the goal (the live statue
and the back of its niche further forward, out of the arch's shadow, so the player can make out what it is).
How it is done is left to the build; these are the choices made, each a placeholder to confirm in playtest.

- **The statue moved only about 4 cm. Most of the gain is lighting and a wider opening.** Physically the statue
  can't come out of the wall: a wall runner's body lies along the face and the solid body hitbox is behind it
  (GDD §3: what looks like a hit is a hit), so a statue standing out of the wall would block the wall run at
  every height, which is a design change for the owner, not a build choice. Its front now stands 0.02 m behind
  the face, measured from its real outline (it was 0.063 m behind: the bounding box the old placement used
  overstated its reach by 0.033 m), the niche is shallower (0.78 m, from 0.9) and wider (1.8 m, from 1.4: more
  of the statue shows through the opening at a grazing angle, where whatever stands deep in an opening hides
  behind its near edge), and its inside is lit. A 3-lane street, where the camera is closest to the wall, still
  shows only a sliver of the statue from far down the street; it reads from about 10 to 15 m. Is that enough, or
  should the opening be wider still (the decorative alcoves are 2.0 m)? **Placeholder:** `GildedSentinelTuning`
  `niche_width` 1.8, `niche_depth` 0.78, `statue_inset` 0.02 (`data/enemies/gilded_sentinel.tres`), all
  `DESIGN-TBD`.
- **Every statue niche is lit alike, the live one told by its eyes and its warning.** The owner's earlier request
  (`docs/USER_REQUESTS.md`: the decorative statues belong at the bottom of the walls so that a live Sentinel can
  surprise the player; "too conspicuous and easy to avoid") rules out a niche of its own for the live one, so
  the decorative alcoves, outdoors and in the Palace, get the same warm bronze inside as the live niche
  (non-glowing, no hazard hues) instead of staying near-black. The live statue is then told from the decorative
  ones by its red eyes (idle glow 1.6, from 0.9) and its warning (the eyes flare, the niche tints red, stone
  grinds). Is a lit alcove the right look for the decorative ones too, and is the eye glow at rest enough to
  tell the live one at a glance, or too much? **Placeholder:** `GoldenStatue.LIT_BACK`, `LIT_SIDES`,
  `LIT_CEILING` (`DESIGN-TBD`), `GildedSentinel.EYES_IDLE` (`DESIGN-TBD`).
- **0.6 s changes the wall dodges.** On the floor, a lane change started after the 0.35 s reaction time the boss
  suites assume is still in time (tested at 3 and 6 lanes, both walls, 18 and 25 m/s). On the wall, passing above
  or below the band by timing the wall entry now has to be planned from the statue at rest, before the warning:
  the jump onto the wall that runs above the band takes the whole warning (0.6 s: the jump's apex time, the
  wall entry and 0.08 s), so it can't be a reaction. A runner who stepped onto the wall 0 to 0.55 s before the
  warning sees no warning before the cut, but escapes with two moves (off the wall, then a lane change) started
  within 0.50 s of it; stepping off the wall alone leaves them in the outer lane's cut. Is that the dodge the owner
  wants on the wall, or should the warning start earlier along the wall approach?
- **A decorative alcove that would overlap a live niche is left out** (less than 0.3 m of wall between the gold
  frames, `GoldenSkin.NICHE_CLEARANCE`, `DESIGN-TBD`), hole and statue, so a live niche is never overlapped by a
  decorative one (2 of 32 Sentinels in Golden 2 and the Palace overlapped one before, and the wider opening would
  add more). Nothing more than that: the live niche is not left in a clearing. **Placeholder:**
  `GoldenSkin.crowds_niche()`, used by `GoldenFacades` and `GoldenPalaceWalls`.
- **Facade statues no longer straddle a chunk's end** (about 6% of the outdoor decorative statues are not placed:
  `GoldenFacades.chunk_straddled`). The walls are built a chunk at a time and the part of such an alcove in the
  next chunk was cut open without its recess when a live niche beside it made the first chunk leave it out. No
  design change; mentioned because the outdoor walls have slightly fewer statues.
- **The halberd's draw-back takes half of the shorter warning.** The eyes flare through all 0.6 s and the draw-back
  takes the last 0.3 s (it was a fixed 0.55 s of 1.2 s), so the eyes and the grinding come first. **Placeholder:**
  `GildedSentinelTuning.raise_share` 0.5 (`DESIGN-TBD`).
- **The generator's keep-out for a Sentinel now starts 0.1 s before its warning** (`escape_lead_seconds` 0.7 s,
  the escape lane's clear lead, is longer than the new warning), so nothing is placed in the lane beside it
  just before, and the level's introduction Sentinel is placed from that window (not 0.1 s too far on). No
  design change; mentioned because Golden 2 and the Palace lay out slightly differently.
- **GDD §9.11 no longer matches the build in one place** (it predates H1): "decorative statues never stand at
  wall-run height (safe things look safe)". Since the owner's earlier request, the decorative statues stand in
  alcoves at the bottom of the walls, 0.1 to 3.5 m up, which is wall-run height. They are told from live ones by
  the red eyes and the warning, as above.
