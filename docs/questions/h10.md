# Questions from H10 (the Tithe Collector stays twice as long)

## 1. Should Hostile Takeover's Tithe Collector stay twice as long too?

GDD §9.12 (owner, October 8, 2026): the Collector "stays in the level twice as long as first built".
A level's Collector now closes in at 3.5 m/s instead of 7 m/s (about 10.9 s on screen untouched, was
about 5.4 s). On the boss's 130 m flatcar roof that is not workable: the Collector appears 30 m ahead
of the runner 12% along the roof and meets the runner after 8.6 s, about 150 m of track later, which
is past the roof's end (over the coupling gap the runner jumps, and the armored carriage after it),
and in the build the runner's coupling jump stomped it mid-air and the bot died to a cyborg bolt.

**Placeholder:** the Board spawns its Collectors with the spawn param `approach_speed` =
`HostileTakeoverTuning.tithe_approach_speed` (7 m/s, the first-built stay; `DESIGN-TBD` doc comment,
`@export_range`), so the boss fight is exactly as before. A longer stay in the boss would need a longer
flatcar roof or a Collector that starts further back; the owner's call.

## 2. May two Tithe Collectors be in the level at once?

GDD §9.12. Nothing reserves a window for a Collector (touching it is never a hit, it is no keep-out for
anything after its `at`). With the longer stay, two overlap whenever their patterns are closer than
about 255 m at the Corporate zone's 23.4 m/s (about 195 m at 18 m/s): in the generated levels that
happens in some layouts (Corporate 2 at 6 lanes has gaps of 70, 98, 160 m between its seven; most
other levels have one or two, far apart). They overlapped before too (a gap under about 125 m), just
less often. Both vacuum their own lane and either can rob the runner once.

**Placeholder:** none; allowed. If the owner wants at most one at a time, the generator needs a
reserved window of about `stay_seconds()` of track after each Collector's `at`.
