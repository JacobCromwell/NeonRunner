# Questions from H3 (Buzz Overdrive cuts show the zone below the street)

The owner asked (October 8, 2026, GDD §9.9) that the bottom of a cut show a zone-specific background, the same as
through an ordinary gap: "the canal in the Golden Zone, the trench under the maglev line, and so on, never a dark
box". The build made a cut show exactly what the zone's own gap shows (the same plane, the same sides, the same
shade); the questions below are what that leaves open.

## 1. How bright may the scenery below the street be, in the zones where the Buzz Overdrive appears? (built; the readability trade-off for the owner to confirm)
The first build made a cut show exactly what its zone's gap showed. The coordinator's decision (the owner made the
request after playing Corporate, the Dead Zone, the Golden Zone and the Golden Palace) was that matching was not
enough where the planes below were drawn almost black: the zone's scenery must be **recognisable from the runner's
camera**, through gaps and cuts alike, while a hole still reads as a hole at a glance. Built (kit patterns, all
steady and albedo, so no Reduced flashing case; nothing glows below the street but the orange edges, and nothing
below is orange, red or any other hazard hue: the Dead Zone's is greys and ash, the Golden Zone's blues and a faint
warm white for the lamps' reflections, which is not a hazard colour):
- **Golden Zone:** a stone quay of round arches on piers, the canal's light wavering on the stone and shimmering in
  the arches, and the canal below as moving water (ripples sliding with the current, a sheen of the dusk's sky,
  glints, and the walkways' lamps wavering on it).
- **Golden Palace:** a stairwell (stone steps descending along the sides) over a lower hall of marble slabs with a
  soft pool of light on it, and the well's walls now drawn on both sides (an outer-lane hole used to show the hall's
  outside, a starry sky, under the wall).
- **Corporate:** a carriage's side with a band of dim cold windows; under it the guideway beams on their piers with a
  lighter top edge over a wet concrete trench (joints, puddles, retaining walls); the plaza's deck edge as a slab over
  a lower level of painted bays, now 9 m down (it was 18 m, lost in the dark). The trench's retaining walls
  (`CorporateTrains.below`) and the plaza's lower-level walls were built facing away from the street and so never
  drew; they now face it. Hostile Takeover's arena inherits the Corporate gap look (its train's carriage ends, its
  `guideway_color`; its moving street far below is its own and unchanged).
- **Dead Zone:** the road's broken layers, then ruined basements (formwork courses, a row of dark window slots
  under pale lintels, pilasters, a pipe run, ash on the sills), a slope of rubble blocks at the wall's foot, and
  far down a rubble floor with paler slabs and pale ash in its cracks, all in greys. (The first build had dull
  embers in the cracks; they sat at the gap edge's hue, drew thin red lines in the holes and broke the zone's own
  rule that embers stay high above the play field, so they are gone: ash only.)

**How the readability bar is now guarded.** The first build raised each skin suite's `GAP_CONTRAST` until it
passed (to 1.3 for Corporate and the Dead Zone, 0.65 for the Golden Zone) and counted only the base colours, so the
guard had become a pin that missed the real peaks (Corporate's slab pattern could reach 1.25 times its colour, the
canal's sky and lamp terms were not counted at all, the palace's pool of light was three times its colour). Now
every below-the-street pattern is capped at its colour (plus, for the quay and the water, exactly the sky's and
the lamps' colours, which the Golden suite adds in sRGB as the shader does), and `SkinSuite.hole_share()` compares
the brightest thing drawn below the street, as rendered (a wall's side face is lit 0.70 by the kit, a street or a
hole's floor seen from above 0.92), with the darkest the street is drawn, as rendered, extreme against extreme:
it must stay at most 0.8 (`HOLE_SHARE_MAX`). Each suite prints its share: Corporate 0.70 (trains and plaza), Golden
0.72, Dead Zone 0.75, Golden Palace 0.22 (its floor is white marble). The colours are the levers; one that goes up
fails its suite. Measured on the rendered frames (build/h3, the gameplay camera, the nearest 160 px of the hole,
display luma): a hole's 99th percentile is 31 to 47 against the darkest tenth of the street at 71 to 110, and a
hole's mean went from 18/10/29 to 19/24/30 on Forward+ (Dead Zone / Corporate / Golden; the Dead Zone's gain is in
structure: its walls now show window slots and courses where they were black).

**Compatibility renders every hole darker than Forward+.** The same colours drew as near-black there (a hole's
median luma 0 to 2 against 9 to 29 on Forward+; the street matched within a few points), so on the web and on
low-end Android the scenery was invisible. The solid kit now lifts what lies below the street on the Compatibility
renderer only (`UNDER_COMPAT_GAMMA` in `kit_solid.gdshader`, 0.85, on the Corporate, Dead Zone, Golden and palace
patterns): the holes then sit within a few luma points of Forward+'s (hole means 16/26/28 against 19/24/30). It does
not go past Forward+'s look, and the suites guard Forward+'s peaks.

**Question:** is "dim but recognisable" the right level, or does the owner want the scenery brighter (a stronger
risk that a hole reads like a floor from a distance) or dimmer (back toward a dark slot)? The levers are one
colour each: `gap_inside_color`, `void_floor_color` (Dead Zone), `canal_color`, `canal_sky_color`, `canal_lamp_color`
(Golden), `gap_inside_color`, `well_floor_color` (palace), `guideway_color`, `trench_color` (Corporate), and
`HOLE_SHARE_MAX`.
**DESIGN-TBD inside it:** the palace's stairwell and light well (the older design notes say a break in the palace
floor is "a collapsed floor, an open stairwell, a light well"; the GDD gives nothing more); the Dead Zone's
basements and rubble (the GDD says "rubble, embers, smoke and silence" and keeps fires minimal); the Corporate
plaza's lower level (a painted-bay concrete floor, 9 m down) and the Golden Zone's arched quay and its water's
sky and lamp colours.
**Placeholder:** as built.

## 2. A cut's far end in the City
At a City gap the far side is the next truck's cab (headlights, grille, orange marker lights). A cut's far end is the
lane's own truck running on, so it has no cab: the cut draws a plain dark face as tall as a truck (2.6 m, `truck_height`)
there, with the road far below. A cab with lights at a cut's end would glow in hazard colours that mean something
else there (the cut's orange is the one edge).
**Question:** keep the plain dark face, or should it read as a truck's rear or front?
**Placeholder:** the plain dark face (`CitySkin.floor_cut`).

## 3. A cut's end faces in Gangland are half as bright as the earth in the holes' walls
The strata at the cut's two ends use the zone's `earth_color` at half brightness (`GanglandSkin.CUT_STRATA_SHADE`),
so a cut's inside stays under the dark limit `test_floor_cuts` holds every look to (a cut's inside is the darkest
thing on the street). The holes' own walls (the neighbouring lanes' sides, which the cut shows) are at full
brightness, so the end faces are a little darker than the sides.
**Question:** fine, or should the limit follow the zone's own wall colour (then the test's limit becomes per zone)?
**Placeholder:** half brightness.
