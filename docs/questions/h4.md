# H4: two cyborg-type bursts in the air at once, open questions

- **The crossfire rule** (GDD §9.2: "Bolts are slow enough to dodge by switching lanes"; "Up to two
  bursts in the air at once"). With two bursts in the air, the runner can dodge the first into a lane
  where the second then aims while the first's bolts still come down the lane they left. From the edge
  lane of three, from a wall run (the only way off is the outer lane below), beside a hover truck or on a
  narrow ceiling, their only way out is then the first burst's lane. Without a rule, 3-lane tests had
  the second burst's bolts arrive 0.04–0.33 s from the first's in the lane beside the runner.
  - **What's built** (`DESIGN-TBD` in `scripts/enemies/cyborg_gun.gd`, `_crossfire_fair`; numbers in
    `data/enemies/cyborg.tres`, `window_cyborg.tres`, `barnacle_turret.tres`): bursts whose bolts arrive
    within `crossfire_gap` (0.5 s) of each other must leave the runner a way out, a place one move away
    that none of them is aimed at: a lane beside them, or the outer lane below a wall. A lane a hover
    truck alongside (or any lane blocker) holds is no way out. A burst that would leave none waits before
    its charge-up (task R3's rule), anticipating every place the runner could be by its lock, a wall they
    could step onto included; a burst still charging for more than `reaction_time` (0.25 s) counts as
    aimed where the runner is now. A last check as the aim locks calls a charge-up off only if the runner
    moved while two charge-ups that began together ended.
  - **Measured** (a dodging bot with a 0.25 s reaction, god mode; the campaign's 15 levels up to 160 s
    each, and dense levels with cyborgs, window cyborgs, ceilings and ramps where the bot wall-runs about
    20 times a minute): charge-ups called off as their aim locks went from 5 in 34 min to 0 at 3 lanes
    and stayed at 1 in 34 min at 5 lanes; in the dense levels from 1 in 8 min (a wall runner) to 0 at both
    lane counts. The share of bursts flown alongside another didn't drop: 14% at 3 lanes and 12–13% at 5 in
    the campaign, 20–22% in the dense levels.
  - **The question:** is this the rule and are these the numbers? Its cost: on three lanes a second burst
    begun well after the first waits while the runner is in the middle lane or in an outer lane beside a
    wall they could step onto (on five or six lanes, one lane in from an edge or beside a wall). Should a
    wall count as a way out from an outer lane (it doesn't now: the rule doesn't judge a wall's own
    hazards, signs, wall fences and window cyborgs), and a lane two moves away?
- **Wild fire next to another burst.** A panic cyborg's bolts land anywhere around the runner, so no
  lane is sure to be free of them. Placeholder (same code): a wild burst's bolts never arrive within
  `crossfire_gap` of another burst's, either way round. Should a panic cyborg's spray be allowed to
  overlap an aimed burst?
- **The last-resort call-off.** When two charge-ups begin within `reaction_time` of each other and the
  runner moves between their locks, the later one can still be called off at the end of its charge-up
  (the glow and the charge sound play, no bolts follow); measured once in 34 min at 5 lanes, never at 3.
  Should it instead hold its charge until it can fire (a longer warning)?
- **What "in the air" counts.** As in the build's one-at-a-time rule, a burst holds its place from the
  start of its charge-up until `burst_gap` (0.5 s) after its last bolt is fired, not until its bolts
  land, so a third burst's charge-up may start while the first two's bolts are still flying (the
  crossfire rule still keeps their arrivals apart). Should the limit count bolts until they land?
