# H4: two cyborg-type bursts in the air at once, open questions

- **The crossfire rule** (GDD §9.2: "Bolts are slow enough to dodge by switching lanes"; "Up to two
  bursts in the air at once"). With two bursts in the air, the runner can dodge the first one into a
  lane where the second then aims, while the first's bolts are still arriving in the lane they left.
  On three lanes from the edge lane, from a wall run (the only way off is the outer lane below) or on
  a narrow ceiling, their only way out is then the lane the first burst's bolts are arriving in.
  Measured in 3-lane tests without a rule: the second burst's bolts arrived 0.04–0.33 s from the
  first's in the lane beside the runner, leaving that long to switch back between the two lines of
  bolts (a bot reacting within a frame survives; a person can't count on it). Placeholder
  (`DESIGN-TBD` in `scripts/enemies/cyborg_gun.gd`, `_crossfire_fair`, and
  `CyborgGunTuning.crossfire_gap`, 0.5 s, in `scripts/enemies/cyborg_gun_tuning.gd`): bursts whose
  bolts arrive within 0.5 s of each other must leave the runner a lane one move away (beside them on
  the floor or a ceiling, the outer lane below from a wall) that none of them is aimed at; otherwise the
  later one waits until its bolts arrive at least 0.5 s before or after the others'. On five or six
  lanes the second burst nearly always fires at once. Is this the right rule and gap, and should a
  lane two moves away count as a way out?
- **Wild fire next to another burst.** A panic cyborg's bolts land anywhere around the runner, so no
  lane is sure to be free of them. Placeholder (same code): a wild burst's bolts never arrive within
  `crossfire_gap` of another burst's, either way round. Should a panic cyborg's spray be allowed to
  overlap an aimed burst?
- **A charge-up called off.** When the runner's dodge leaves them no free lane just as a second
  burst's aim would lock, that burst is called off at the end of its charge-up (the glow and the
  charge sound play, no bolts follow) and charges again once it fits, as the gun already does when a
  fence comes into a burst's path. Should it instead hold its charge until it can fire (a longer
  warning)?
- **What "in the air" counts.** As in the build's one-at-a-time rule, a burst holds its place from the
  start of its charge-up until `burst_gap` (0.5 s) after its last bolt is fired, not until its bolts
  land, so a third burst's charge-up may start while the first two's bolts are still flying (the
  crossfire rule still keeps their arrivals apart). Should the limit count bolts until they land?
