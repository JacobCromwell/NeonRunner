# H5: the dash smashes doodads

Raised while building task H5 (GDD §3, "Zone doodads": "The dash smashes a doodad", owner, October 8,
2026). The orchestrator folds these into `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` under "Raised during build".

1. **What a smash looks and sounds like** (GDD §3 says only that the doodad "breaks apart"). The doodad
   vanishes as the runner reaches it and 10 to 28 solid pieces (by its size) fly out of its box in its
   look's own colours. The pieces carry on along the runner's way at half to a little over its speed, fly
   out to the sides and up, tumble, skid off the street and shrink away within 0.9 s. A light camera shake (0.1, under
   a dash kill's 0.32) and a crunch go with it: a crack, a heavy thump, crumbling grit and a few small
   pieces clattering down, heavier than the push's thud. No fireball, sparks, flash or hit-stop. The pieces
   are lit scenery and never glow, so Reduced flashing changes nothing; Screen shake scales the shake. The
   alternatives: a dust cloud as well, more or bigger pieces, or a zone-specific sound.
   *Placeholder:* `SpeedFxTuning`'s "Doodad smashes" group (`smash_shake_*`, `rubble_*`; F6, "Speed
   effects"), `RubbleBurst` (`scripts/run/rubble_burst.gd`), and `doodad_smash` in
   `tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_player.gd` and `data/audio/sfx_library.tres` (-5 dB). The colours come from
   `ZoneSkin.doodad_debris_colors`, which by default reads each look's main lit colours.

2. **A dashing switch into a doodad's side** (GDD §3: "dashing into one breaks it apart"). A lane switch
   into a doodad's side while dashing isn't blocked. The switch goes through, and the doodad breaks where
   the body meets it, with no clank and no bump. Without the dash the switch is blocked as before.
   Any other solid side (a hover truck's, a boss's prop) still bumps a dashing player. The alternative:
   only a head-on dash smashes, and a dashing switch into a side bumps like any other.
   *Placeholder:* `Player._start_switch` and `Player._lane_blocked(target, dash_through)` (DESIGN-TBD).

3. **A dash that ends just short of a doodad** (GDD §3). Whether the dash reaches a doodad is decided
   when the doodad comes within the push's reach: if the dash lasts until the body gets there (counting a
   ramp's or speed pad's boost as it fades), it smashes it. Otherwise the doodad pushes as usual, at the
   push's usual moment, so the body never sinks into it.
   A doodad the dash has reached still breaks on contact up to 0.1 s after the dash's last frame (the
   contact can fall a frame or two after it). The alternative: whatever the dash's timing, a doodad
   touched within a fixed time after the dash ends is smashed.
   *Placeholder:* `Player._dash_claims`, `Player.SMASH_CLAIM_GRACE` (DESIGN-TBD).

4. **Does smashing a doodad score anything?** (GDD §7 lists the score's sources; doodads aren't among
   them). It scores nothing and pays nothing: a doodad is scenery, and the dash is the reward. The
   alternative: a small bonus per smash, which would make dashing into doodads something to chase.
   *Placeholder:* `Player._smash` (DESIGN-TBD), no score.

5. **Attacks near a smashed doodad.** Some enemies hold an attack while a doodad stands where it would
   land: a drone's barrage, a hover truck's cannon, an Octodog charge or a Resonator pulse moved on by a
   wait, cyborg bolts. They read the level's plan (`LevelLayout.doodad_between`), so after a smash they
   still hold off along that stretch, as if the doodad stood. Every attempt plays the same whether or not
   the runner dashed, and the attack only waits a moment longer. The alternative: let them attack once
   it's gone, which is what a level without that doodad would do.
   *Placeholder:* `DashBreakable` (header, DESIGN-TBD); nothing changed in the enemies.

6. **Telling the player** (GDD §8: the dash "barrels through enemies and obstacles"). Nothing new tells
   the player that the dash smashes doodads. The shop's dash text already says "Barrel through enemies
   and obstacles", and there's no first-encounter hint for it. The alternative: a hint the first time a
   runner carrying the dash meets a doodad, or a line in the shop's text.
   *Placeholder:* none built.

7. **A dash started during a doodad's push** (GDD §3). A dash that starts while a doodad's push is
   carrying the player doesn't smash that doodad: the push completes, and the doodad stands. If the player
   then steers back into it with the dash on, the dash takes it, and it breaks where the body meets it
   (the body never sinks into it). The alternative: a dash during the push smashes it at once and the
   push stops.
   *Placeholder:* `Player._check_doodads` (the pushed doodad left alone while its push carries the player,
   DESIGN-TBD).

Measured, not a question: a runner who dashes through a doodad has time to react to whatever it hid. The
doodads' placement keeps the level's spacing after each one clear in every lane. Over every campaign
level at 3, 5 and 6 lanes (270 doodads), the next thing after one comes at least 0.75, 0.73 and 0.72 s
after its front at the dash's speed, and 0.70, 0.68 and 0.68 s after its end (for a dash into its side
near its end). A reaction (0.35 s) and a lane switch take 0.49 s. So the generator is unchanged
(`test_doodads`' `_test_dash_fairness`; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, The generator, Zone doodads).
