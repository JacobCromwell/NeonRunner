# Questions from H6 (the explosions: one shared yellow-and-red fireball)

The owner asked (October 8, 2026, GDD §11) that every explosion be a big, visually impressive yellow and red
fireball, softened by Reduced flashing. The questions below are what the build had to decide without him.

## 1. Red and orange glow in an explosion: an exception to "only hazards glow in hazard colours"?
GDD §11 (colour rule): only hazards glow in hazard colours (pink, yellow and black, red, orange, green, cyan).
A yellow-and-red fireball is red and orange and glows. The placeholder keeps it a look and keeps it short: no
collision, no light, about a second of fire (a bit longer for a boss's), embers, and then dark, non-glowing
smoke, so nothing is left behind that could read as a hazard. The bombs' fireballs (Floating Head, The House)
mark where the blast hazard is, so they are sized to it and gone about when it is (no smoke).
**Question:** is the fireball the one explicit exception to the colour rule (it is in the placeholder), or
should the explosions of things that are not themselves hazards (the player's missiles, a destroyed
generator) keep a cooler look?
**Placeholder:** the shared fireball everywhere (`scripts/run/fireball_pool.gd`).

## 2. The player's missiles: fireballs too?
GDD §11's line lists "missiles", while the weapon section keeps the player's fire cool (cyan, white, violet) so
it never reads like red enemy fire. The build made the heavy missile's blast a fireball (inside the cyan ring that
still shows its splash reach) and the plain missile's hit a small, quick one; lasers stay cool.
**Question:** both missiles, or only the heavy one? (`WeaponFx.missile_pop` is the one call to drop.)
**Placeholder:** both; sizes `WeaponFx.HEAVY_FIRE_SIZE` (2.2) and `MISSILE_FIRE_SIZE` (0.9).

## 3. Which other events count as explosions?
Beyond the list the owner gave, the build also made fireballs of: the hover truck bursting through the wall
(a quick one), the drone's first hit (it bursts into flames before it crashes), the Floating Head's tower landing
on the ship, and Hostile Takeover's rolling blasts (five fireballs, not two). Not changed: the Sleep Taker's
wisps and chunks, the Sewer Swarm, and plain deaths.
**Question:** keep these, or leave any of them as they were?
**Placeholder:** all in (`DroneScript.FIRE_HIT_SIZE`, `HoverTruck.FIRE_BURST_SIZE`, `FloatingHead.IMPACT_FIRE_SIZE`,
`HostileTakeover.BLAST_SIZES`).

## 4. How big and how long?
