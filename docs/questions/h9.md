All the numbers below are in `data/bosses/dead_zone_boss_tuning.tres` (`SleepTakerTuning`, F6 in the
fight) or `data/bosses/dead_zone_boss_wall_gaps.tres` (`WallGapTuning`), marked DESIGN-TBD in
`scripts/bosses/sleep_taker/sleep_taker_tuning.gd`.

- **How dark lights out goes on the web / low-end renderer** (GDD §10, Sleep Taker, lights out "half as
  bright again as first built"). Placeholder: `dark_level` 0.225 (exactly half the first build's 0.45) for
  the ambient and sky light, the fog's light and the sun, and the scenery's own light (0.162 of the zone's
  light in its arena, was 0.324), above floors of its own (`light_floor` 0.2, `scenery_floor` 0.15; every
  other boss keeps 0.3). Measured on screen (`tools/showcase/sleep_taker_showcase.tscn --scenario=measure`,
  `--first-dark` for the first build): the street goes from 50 to 32 (of 255) at the darkest on both
  renderers; the glows hold (generator 218 to 217, pad 182 to 181, mist 106 to 105 on Forward+). On the
  Compatibility renderer the walls go nearly black (6, was 15; they are darker there than on Forward+ in
  every light), though the street, the bridges, the wall gaps' orange edges and every glow stay readable.
  Is that dark enough, or too dark, there?
- **Translucent warnings over a darker street.** The slash's red lane marks are drawn over the street, so
  they lose some brightness with it (62 to 52 on Forward+, 48 to 35 on Compatibility) while standing out
  from it more than before (96 to 110). Should they brighten during lights out instead?
- **The hands' rounds** (GDD §10, "the hands spread out"). Placeholder: a round has 2 rows, then a row more
  each round up to 4 (`hand_rows_first`, `hand_rows_max`), kept across phases, 0.85 s of run apart
  (`hand_row_seconds`, divided by the phase's pace: 0.74 s and 0.65 s later); each row leaves one floor
  lane open (`hand_row_open`), one lane over from the last, and puts a hand in every other floor lane whose
  floor is clear (4 hands a row at 5 lanes, 5 at 6), so every row is a lane switch. Should the wider
  streets leave two lanes open? Are four rows and these gaps between them right?
- **All of a round's mists at once.** Placeholder: as a round starts, every hand's mist pools at once
  (its later rows' warnings show longer than the first's), with one whisper for the round and one burst
  sound for each row as it rises. Should the rows' mists appear one after another instead?
- **Wall hands** ("the hands attack the walls much more often"). Placeholder: from the first round, one
  wall hand on every row, alternating walls (`wall_hands_per_row`), never beside a door in an outer lane,
  never at a wall gap, and only over an outer lane that has its own floor hand, so no floor runner ever
  passes under one (a jump there would reach it).
- **When a round can't fit.** Placeholder: a round takes the rows that are over before the next refuge's
  slash or lure, but never fewer than 2 (`hand_rows_min`); otherwise it waits and the next attack in the
  phase's list may go first. A clean win now sees 3 or 4 rounds (about 78 s, still inside the three-star
  par of 86 s).
- **The fairness margins the rounds are planned with.** Placeholder: a runner moving 0.4 s after the mists
  show (`route_reaction`), a lane switch taking the real 0.14 s times 1.5 (`route_switch_margin`), and the
  body 0.55 m past a hand either way (`route_body_margin`), checked by The House's lane router.
- **How many wall gaps** ("lots more gaps on the side walls"). Placeholder: 1.6 to 1.3 s of run from one
  gap's end to the next (a level: 32 to 24 s), 30% on both walls, 0.6 to 1.1 s long: about 15 a minute
  (a level's median is 1.7). Both walls stay whole from where each refuge's slash warns to the end of its
  bridge (and 0.5 s either side), so a runner who takes to a wall as the slash warns is never dropped
  into it, and a bridge never hangs over a missing wall. Is that frequency right, and should the walls
  stay whole there?
- **"Double the floor gaps."** Placeholder: once a lap's refuges are in, the generator's additive gap pass
  doubles the rows of holes the fight actually has (`floor_gap_increase` 1), keeping the lap's average
  width, which also doubles the lane-gaps: over its three laps 11 to 22 rows at 3 and 5 lanes and 8 to 16
  at 6 (lane-gaps 15 to 30, 26 to 53 and 24 to 50). The new rows keep 1.3 s of run from everything else
  (`floor_gap_spacing`): the arena's own 1.9 s leaves room for only 1.6 to 1.8 times as many.
