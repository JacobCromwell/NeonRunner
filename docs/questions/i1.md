# I1, the Buzz Overdrive shares the field: open questions

The owner's calls (October 10, 2026; GDD §3 and §9.9) are built as given: the Buzz Overdrive's recency boost capped
at 1 like the other big enemies', its pattern 10 m long instead of 200 m, obstacles and lower-tier enemies on the
field with it (its lane, GDD §9.9's limits and the big attacks' turns kept), and zone doodads beside the bigger
enemies. These are the choices made to fill them in, and what they cost elsewhere.

- **Which enemies count as "lower tier"** (GDD §9, "Big attacks take turns"). Placeholder: every type whose tuning
  says it makes no big attack (`EnemyTuning.big_attack` false: the cyborg, the window cyborg, the screech, the fence
  generator, the Tithe Collector, the Barnacle Turret); a host counts as big (its Bad Dream's chase). They may stand
  anywhere during a cut but in its lane, and never in the lane its way out needs (`LevelGenerator.cut_problem`,
  `cut_escape_clear`). The big ones (`big_attack` true in `data/enemies/*.tres`) still keep off its rev and charge.
  The same list as `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` item 93's. OK?
- **Which bigger enemies doodads may stand beside.** Placeholder: the three named, a Buzz Overdrive (off its cut's
  lane and its way out), an Enforcer Truck (in its showing window, wherever it can still show itself:
  `EnforcerTruckRules.doodad_guard`) and a hover truck (its whole stay, off its lane; its cannon already holds fire
  while a doodad is in reach, and it lurches only at a runner who can leave its lane). The others keep doodads off
  their planned attacks as before: an Octodog's run, a Resonator's visit, a Gilded Sentinel's strike, a drone wave
  until its first pad, a Bad Dream's chase. Their attacks are planned around a clear floor and don't wait for a
  doodad (a drone's barrage does, so a doodad in a wave would quiet its first barrages). Should doodads come beside
  those too (each would need its attack to wait for a doodad, or its planner to count them)?

  never takes one out (it takes out plain cyborgs, window cyborgs and screeches). Placeholder: the planner as it
  was. Should a window take out a plain Tithe Collector too, or should a Buzz Overdrive that a truck's chase baits
  keep a calm stretch before it?
