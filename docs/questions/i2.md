# Questions from I2 (the cyborgs drawn 20% bigger)

## 1. Should the other zones draw their cyborgs bigger too?

**The question** (GDD §9.2, "Drawn 20% bigger", owner, October 14, 2026): the cyborgs were hard to see in the
first zone, so the Neon City draws them 20% bigger with their hitboxes unchanged. The GDD records it for the
Neon City only, where the problem was seen. Do the other zones' variants (the Brute, the Casino Mob Enforcer,
the VR Runner, the burned base, the ceremonial enforcer) need the same, and by how much?

**The placeholder:** the City only. `ZoneSkin.cyborg_draw_scale` (`@export_range`, default 1.0) is 1.2 in
`data/skins/city_skin.tres` and 1.0 in every other skin, marked `DESIGN-TBD`. Extending it is one number in
each zone's skin resource. It scales the model only; the hitboxes, the bolts' start and the floor-use spans
are what they were.
