# Questions from I6 (one hit from death)

## 1. Does the cue play when a level or a fight begins with the player unprotected?

**The question** (GDD §4, "One hit from death", owner, October 14, 2026): the cue marks the moment the player has run
out of all armor and shields. The game's own runs always begin with the free armor, so a run can only begin
unprotected in a test or a tool (a bare loadout), or if a later task lets a boss or a level take the armor away.
Should the cue then play once at the start, as if the protection had just run out, or wait until the player has had
protection and lost it?

**The placeholder:** it plays once at the start. `LastHitWarningTuning.warn_at_start` (default on, marked
`DESIGN-TBD`, `data/ui/last_hit_warning.tres`); off, the cue waits until the player has been protected once. After a
revive that brings no protection back (no armor carried) it plays again, since the player is one hit from death again.

## 2. How strong is the cue, and does it need a marker that doesn't rely on colour?

**The question** (GDD §4): the GDD says "an audio and visual cue" and "a two-tone siren", nothing more. The
placeholder is a red band along the screen's edges (three pulses with the siren, then a dim steady edge; steady with
Reduced flashing), and the siren once, mixed a couple of dB under the quietest attack warning so it can't mask one.
Is that the right strength on a phone in daylight, and should the HUD also carry a steady marker that isn't a colour
(a warning icon by the protections) for players who can't tell the red edge from the scenery?

**The placeholder:** the edge only. Every number (`pulse_count`, `pulse_seconds`, `pulse_alpha`, `steady_alpha`,
`reduced_alpha`, `edge_width`, the fades and `onset_delay`) is in `data/ui/last_hit_warning.tres` and F6, "One hit from
death", marked `DESIGN-TBD`; the siren's level is `last_hit_siren` in `data/audio/sfx_library.tres`. The siren is made
for `pulse_count` and `pulse_seconds`, so regenerate it (`tools/godot.sh sfx --only=last_hit_siren`) after changing
either.

## 3. Should a first-encounter hint tell the player what the siren means?

**The question** (GDD §4): a player who has never lost all their protection hears a siren and sees red edges with
no words. The hints (`data/hints/hints.json`) teach each new thing once. Should the first time the cue shows, a hint
say that the next hit ends the run (and that the armor comes back after 30 seconds)?

**The placeholder:** no hint. The cue is the whole of it, as the owner described it.
