# Questions from I6 (one hit from death)

## 1. Does the cue play when a level or a fight begins with the player unprotected?

**The question** (GDD §4, "One hit from death", owner, October 14, 2026): the cue marks the moment the player has run
out of all armor and shields. The game's own runs always begin with the free armor, so a run can only begin
unprotected in a test or a tool (a bare loadout), or if a later task lets a boss or a level take the armor away.
Should the cue then play once at the start, as if the protection had just run out, or wait until the player has had
protection and lost it?

**The placeholder:** it plays once at the start. `LastHitWarningTuning.warn_at_start` (default on, marked
`DESIGN-TBD`, `data/ui/last_hit_warning.tres`); off, the cue waits until the player has been protected once. After a
revive that brings no protection back (no armor carried) it plays again, since the player is one hit from death again.

## 2. How strong is the cue, and does it need a marker that doesn't rely on colour?

**The question** (GDD §4): the GDD says "an audio and visual cue" and "a two-tone siren", nothing more. The
placeholder is a red band along the screen's edges (two pulses with the siren, then a dim steady edge; steady with
Reduced flashing), drawn under the attack markers, and the siren once. Is that the right strength on a phone in
daylight, and should the HUD also carry a steady marker that isn't a colour (a warning icon by the protections) for
players who can't tell the red edge from the scenery?

The siren's level is the part to judge by ear. It is mixed (`last_hit_siren` -6.5 dB in `data/audio/sfx_library.tres`)
so that on the general loudness weighting (headphones, PC speakers) it is 2.4 dB under the quietest of the 43 attack
warnings in the library (-21.0 dB heard against `cogs_aim`'s -18.6 dB), so it can't mask one. **On the phone
weighting** (a small speaker, little below 450 Hz) the figures are different, because a phone drops the low parts of
many warnings but not the siren's (it is made of 494 and 698 Hz): the siren is -23.8 dB heard, **louder than one
warning, `truck_rev` (-25.8 dB, an engine rumble), and within 3 dB over it of nineteen more**: `guppy_floor_warn` +0.2,
`tower_crack` +0.6, `gilded_sentinel_grind` +1.1, `truck_cannon_charge` +1.2, `wave_swell` +1.3, `sleep_taker_inhale`
+1.3, `house_cherry` +1.4, `house_lightning` +1.4, `drone_windup` +1.6, `gc_hatch` +1.6, `head_mouth_open` +1.9,
`magnate_crackle` +2.1, `host_roar` +2.1, `gc_grind` +2.2, `truck_bang` +2.6, `octodog_windup` +2.6, `magnate_glitch`
+2.7, `cogs_aim` +2.9, `cogs_charge` +3.0 (each: how much louder than the siren that warning is on a phone). Clearing
every warning but `truck_rev` by 2 dB on a phone would mean -8.3 dB (4.2 dB under the quietest warning on the general
weighting), and clearing `truck_rev` too -10.5 dB (6.4 dB under). Lower it that far, or leave it where a phone player
hears it among the loud sounds?

**The placeholder:** the edge only, and -6.5 dB. Every number (`pulse_count`, `pulse_seconds`, `pulse_alpha`,
`steady_alpha`, `reduced_alpha`, `edge_width`, the fades and `onset_delay`) is in `data/ui/last_hit_warning.tres` and
F6, "One hit from death", marked `DESIGN-TBD`; the siren's level is in `data/audio/sfx_library.tres`. The siren is made
for `pulse_count` and `pulse_seconds`, so regenerate it (`tools/godot.sh sfx --only=last_hit_siren`) after changing
either. `test_last_hit_warning` prints the phone figures and fails if the siren is as loud as or louder than more
than two warnings there.

## 3. Should a first-encounter hint tell the player what the siren means?

**The question** (GDD §4): a player who has never lost all their protection hears a siren and sees red edges with
no words. The hints (`data/hints/hints.json`) teach each new thing once. Should the first time the cue shows, a hint
say that the next hit ends the run (and that the armor comes back after 30 seconds)?

**The placeholder:** no hint. The cue is the whole of it, as the owner described it.

## 4. Is the siren different enough from The House's jackpot siren and the searchlight's alarm?

**The question** (GDD §4, §10): two sounds the player can hear in a boss fight are also two-tone alarms. The House's
jackpot (`house_jackpot`, a reward: sirens at 880 and 660 Hz, about 0.23 s a tone, a square wave over a fanfare of bells)
and the Floating Head's searchlight lock (`searchlight_lock`, a bomb's warning: 1320 then 990 Hz in two 0.14 s beeps).
The one-hit-from-death cue can fire in both fights, and a two-tone siren near the jackpot's figure could read as a
reward. This one is lower, slower and softer than both: 698 and 494 Hz (a tritone where the jackpot's is a fourth), 0.34 s a tone, a pair of slightly detuned triangle
waves through a low-pass, with only two pulses so the siren is 1.44 s. Are the three clearly different when heard
together, in the House fight and the Floating Head's? (The measured tones and the spectrograms differ; nobody has
heard them side by side.)

**The placeholder:** the siren as described, `last_hit_siren`, generated by `tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_power.gd`. Its
register, interval and speed are constants there (`F5`, `B4`, and the tuning's `pulse_seconds`).
