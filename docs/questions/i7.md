# Questions from I7 (the economy rebalanced for eight zones)

Measure with `tools/measure/economy.gd --lanes=5 --seeds=0` (the model is `tools/measure/economy_model.gd`; the
pins are `FIRST_FIT` in `tests/suites/test_economy.gd`); prices are in `data/shop/catalog.json`. "Level" means the
campaign's play order with the Beach's volleyball match counted (City 1-3 are levels 1-3, Gangland 4-6, Marketplace
7-8, Casino 9-10, Corporate 11-12, Beach 13-15, Dead Zone 16-17, Golden 18-20). "First fits" is the first level
whose running wallet (a typical player: 70% of each level's credits, no deaths, no boss payouts, nothing bought)
covers a tier's line total, its price plus every tier before it.

1. **When should each upgrade come into reach?** (GDD §7 "Rebalance for the full campaign", §8, §5's schedule.) The
   GDD fixes none of it except what the owner has said: Armor I fits City 1 (October 3), Laser I costs 1,800 and
   first fits at the campaign's 4th level (October 11), and the dash's upgrades cost 800 / 1,000 / 1,200. The rest
   is placed by what each thing answers: claws at the Octodog (level 5, its tentacles); Laser II before the first
   ceiling hazard (level 7); Armor III before the Casino's mixed crowd (level 8); the dash line complete (level 8)
   before the Corporate dash walls (level 11); Missile at Buzz Overdrive's debut (level 11); Armor IV before the
   Dead Zone, whose Bad Dreams ignore weapons (level 15); Heavy missile at the Golden Zone, where the Sentinels
   (17 shots at tier 1) and peak difficulty arrive (level 18).

     1,800, the dash's four prices, claws 2,600, magnet 700 / 1,600 / 3,200, Armor II 1,700, Armor III 3,400,
     Laser II 2,200 and the breakables (shield 400, grapple 250, revive 600). Moved: **Armor IV 6,000 to 5,500**

2. **Should a first playthrough reach the capstones?** (GDD §6, replay value; §7 "key balancing value".) The whole
   permanent catalog costs 43,650; a typical player's playthrough pays 16,343 from the levels and the match
   (20,343 with the bosses), so the catalog is 2.7 playthroughs (2.1 with the bosses), and a player who buys as they
   go owns about half of it by the end. "First fits" says when a tier is in reach if saved for, not that a player
   who also bought Armor I, Laser I and the dash can afford the Heavy missile at level 18. Every completion pays
   again, so the rest comes from replays: the best single level pays about 1,000, so the whole catalog is about
   43 clean replays on top of the playthrough. Is that the intended grind, or should the capstones (Armor IV, Heavy
   missile, Slow time) be cheaper so a first playthrough can own them?
   - **Placeholder:** the prices above. The alternative is to halve the late tiers (Armor III and IV, Magnet III,
     Missile, Heavy missile, Slow time), which puts the Heavy missile at about level 12 and Armor IV at about 9.

3. **The payouts.** (GDD §7.) The owner asked for the completion payouts to be rebalanced too. Measured, the
   completion bonus (100 + 25 a level, so 100 at City 1 and 500 at Golden 3; the Beach's levels share Corporate 2's
   place, item 572, so the plateau is 375 for three levels) and the boss payouts (500 each, 1,000 for the last) fit
   the placements above, and City 1-3 are the approved early revision, so they are unchanged. Raising the slope
   moves only the late tiers: at 35 a level a typical player's Heavy missile comes at level 17 (Dead Zone 2) and
   Armor IV at 14; at 15 a level, Heavy at 19 and Armor IV at 16 (`--bonus-per-level=35`). A boss pays 500
   whatever the zone: the City boss pays 77% of a City 3 finish, the Dead Zone's about half of a Dead Zone 1 finish.
   - **Placeholder:** unchanged (`data/tuning/game_rules.tres`, `data/bosses/*.tres`). Should the late levels, or the
     bosses, pay more?

4. **The dash's base price.** (GDD §8.) The owner approved the three upgrades (800 / 1,000 / 1,200, test_economy's
   dash checks) against "the existing base price", 1,800. At 1,800 the dash first fits at level 4 and all four
   tiers by level 8, well before the dash walls start (level 11, GDD §9.14). A base of about 5,000 would put the
   whole line at levels 8 to 11, "as its use appears", at the cost of the dash arriving four levels later than
   today for everything else it does (fences, doodads, enemies).
   - **Placeholder:** 1,800, unchanged.
