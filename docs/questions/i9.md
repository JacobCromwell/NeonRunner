   its 30 s, or the final phase's pickup are their next chance). Should a phase begun with the armor
   down count as unprotected even with a shield held?

   (`BossEncounter.player_protected()`, `scripts/bosses/boss_encounter.gd`), so such a phase schedules
   nothing, and its shield's break brings nothing either. Marked `DESIGN-TBD` there and on
