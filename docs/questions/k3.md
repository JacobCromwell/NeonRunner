# K3: Casino roof and named signs

Questions raised while building the whole glass roof and the two named casinos' lettering (the owner's
answers of October 9, 2026, open questions 371, 372 and 374; GDD §5, Zone 4). The owner left the lettering's
case and styling to the art agent (Exo 2 in capitals, warm white, on dark boards in brass frames), so only
what the answers did not settle is asked here. Each is marked `DESIGN-TBD` in
`scripts/world/skins/casino/casino_lettering.gd` and `casino_facades.gd`.

1. **Which casino carries which name.** The owner named two casinos but not how often each appears. Placeholder:
   each casino building with a big sign carries one of the two by hash, half and half (`CasinoLettering.pick()`),
   so a stretch of street can have a few of the same name in a row. Should one name be the common one (the
   reference's Gasket's House of Chance is the larger sign, the Brass Lotus the one with the tall blade), or
   should they alternate more evenly?

2. **Casinos whose big sign plays the cult's feed (35% of them).** The feed's screen stays a screen with no
   words on it (the cult's feed is wordless). Placeholder: a Gasket's casino puts its name on a one-line strip
   over the screen in its own brass frame, and a Brass Lotus casino carries its name on its tall blade sign only.
   Every casino with a big sign also has a blade sign at one end of its building (GASKET'S, or THE BRASS LOTUS,
   stacked down it): a board on a wall is seen along its face from the street, so only the blades can be read
   from a distance while running. Is that right, or should a feed casino go without a name, or without a blade?

3. **Where else the names might appear.** Only the casino buildings' big signs and their blades carry words.
   Every other sign (the lounges' blade signs, the arcade halls' name boards, the sign gantries over the street)
   keeps its rows of glyphs, as the owner asked. Should the sign gantries (a ceiling piece, seen face-on from far
   down the street) carry one of the names too?
