# L10, no feed TVs in the shop windows: ad screens and posters

**How many of the windows, and how are they split between ad screens and posters?** (GDD §5, "Cyborg Viewing Devices",
owner, October 17, 2026: "some windows get screens playing random ads, with the cult emblem flashing on them every once in
a while; others get billboards that fit the world", roughly the TVs' share.) Placeholder: `MarketplaceSkin.window_display_share`
0.22 (the old TVs' share, and the very same windows) and `window_screen_share` 0.5 (half of them ad screens, half posters),
in `data/skins` through the skin's exports; the Casino reads the same two numbers.

**What do the ads and posters show, and how often does the emblem flash?** Placeholder, all wordless and in the market's
own marks: six ad scenes (the market's brand card, a bowl of noodles, a drink, a delivery drone, a sale, a cup of tea), four of
them cycling on each screen in a seeded order, each up 5 to 7.5 s (`ad_scene_time`); the cult's emblem flashing onto a screen
every 14 to 26 s (`emblem_flash_period`) for under two seconds, in over 0.22 s, held 1.1 s, out over 0.55 s
(`emblem_flash_times`; with Reduced flashing 0.9 s, 2 s, 0.9 s, `emblem_flash_times_reduced`); four posters (a shop's own sign,
a sale, a menu, a product) in blues, violets, slate and cream. All in `kit_window.gdshaderinc` and the skin's exports, so
changing a scene, a rate or a colour never touches code that places anything.

**Are posters allowed to have citizens in front of them?** Placeholder: no. The citizens skip every window holding an ad
screen or a poster (`shop_windows()`'s `screen`), which keeps their numbers and the window cyborgs' apart exactly as with the
TVs. A window a window cyborg stands in gets no display either, so nothing beside a screen-headed cyborg competes with it.

**Gangland's TV and the Beach's bars.** The task named "every zone whose windows hold these TVs": only the Marketplace and
the Casino (which reuses its windows) hold them in *shop* windows. Gangland (D9) also plays the feed on a TV glowing in an
upper window of some ruins (`GanglandSkin.feed_windows()`, `feed_window_share` 0.3), and the Beach (D10) on TVs behind some
upper-deck bars. Neither is a shop window, and the owner's decision speaks of shop windows, so both are left as they were. If the
owner meant every TV in every zone, those two are the next place to change (a ruin's window would get a dead screen or an ad
screen of its own).

**The panels are turned toward the runner.** A flat panel parallel to a wall is seen almost edge-on from the lanes, so every panel
(screens and posters) is turned `window_screen_turn` (28 degrees) toward the end of the street the runner comes from, as the
citizens already turn; the window's glass and display stay as they were. 0 puts them flat against the wall.
