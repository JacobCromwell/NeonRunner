# Task L3: doodads in rows, and the lane by the side wall

The owner's requests: "occasionally ... rows of similar doodads: for example three plant doodads one right after the
other, or three market stalls, one followed by a space, followed by another stall" (October 17, 2026), and "doodads
prefer the lane next to the side wall when it's open, without costing gameplay: not where the generator already has
something on that part of the wall or needs it open" (October 9, 2026, held until now). Both in
`LevelGenerator._place_doodads` (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, The generator, Zone doodads). Every number below is a
`LevelConfig` field in its "Doodads" group (F6 "Level pacing"), the same default for every level, unless said
otherwise.

1. **How often rows come, how long, how spaced.** (GDD §3, Zone doodads.)
   Placeholder: a doodad starts a row with `doodad_row_chance` 0.5; the row holds `doodad_row_min` to
   `doodad_row_max` (2 to 4, each count as likely) of the same size class, in its lane, pushing to its side; half of
   the rows (`doodad_row_spaced_share` 0.5) with a doodad's length between each two (the owner's stalls), the others
   one right after the other, a sliver apart (`LevelGenerator.DOODAD_ROW_TOUCH`, 5 cm, so the pictures on two boxes'
   faces never lie in one plane). A row is as long as fits where it's drawn, else shorter, else one doodad: rows come
   where a level leaves long stretches free, so most are two long and few are large ones. Measured over the campaign
   (each level's own seed and four others, 3, 5 and 6 lanes) in the task's notes (`docs/architecture/generator-track-features.md`,
   Zone doodads). Should rows come more often, be longer (the owner's examples are three), or wait for a level of their
   own (City 1 introduces doodads gently: it has rows too, of small and medium ones)?

2. **A wall runner meeting a doodad by their wall.** (GDD §3, Side walls and Zone doodads.) A wall runner's body
   reaches into the lane by the wall, and a free entry or the slide down leaves every runner low on the wall, so the
   generator alone can't keep them apart (the wall hop keeps a runner on a wall as long as they like). Today a runner
   who meets one would pass through it.
   Placeholder: it breaks as a runner low on that wall reaches it, as the dash smashes one (its pieces, the crunch, the
   light shake; no push, no damage, no score), and the wall run goes on; a runner high enough on the wall (a ramp's
   launch, a jump onto the wall) passes over it, and it stands (`Player._check_wall_doodads`, DESIGN-TBD).
   Alternatives: the wall run ends in front of it (the runner drops into the outer lane, as at a wall gap, and the
   doodad pushes them aside), or doodads by a wall stand clear of the wall runner's strip (narrower, or set off the
   lane's middle, as a dash wall leaves its strip open).

3. **What "open" means, and how strongly doodads prefer that lane.** (GDD §3, Zone doodads and Side walls.)
   Placeholder: a doodad (or a row) takes the outermost lane by an open wall with `doodad_wall_lane_share` 0.6 (either
   wall, seeded, when both are open; the only choice where no inner lane fits), pushing inward. Open
   (`LevelGenerator.wall_lane_keep_outs`): nothing on that wall within `doodad_wall_clear_seconds` (1 s at run speed)
   of it (a sign, a window cyborg's window, a wall vent, a Gilded Sentinel's wall section, a hover truck's wall, a ramp
   to the end of the longest wall run it launches, a wall-run gap's lit wall); no dash wall's wall route, no wall
   fence's drop window and no side wall gap beside it or dropping a wall runner onto it (`doodad_wall_drop`: the fall
   from the highest wall run and the push's lead); and every lane clear around it, as for any doodad. Wall fences and
   side wall gaps come after the doodads and keep off them in turn, so they move a little elsewhere; no wall credit
   lies within 3 m of one (`DOODAD_WALL_CREDIT_MARGIN`), so the future wall-hop credits keep off them too. Is 0.6 the
   right strength (about 4 in 10 of the campaign's doodads stand by a wall), and should it differ by zone (the
   Marketplace's stalls by the walls, the Dead Zone's rubble anywhere)?

4. **A row's look.** (GDD §3, Zone doodads, their look; `docs/architecture/skins.md`, Zone doodads' looks.)
   Placeholder: a row is one kind: every doodad of it shows its first one's picture (`look`, its seed), so three
   potted palms, never a palm, a bush and a palm; with one picture per size class in most zones every doodad of a
   class looks the same anyway. Should neighbours of one kind vary (mirrored, or the zone's two small pictures
   alternating in a row of planters)?

5. **The Beach's open walls.** (GDD §5, the Beach; task D10b.)
   Placeholder: a side wall gap keeps off a doodad by its wall like any other wall piece, so on the Beach, whose walls
   stand only about half the time, the walls stand a little more around the doodads by them (measured in the
   generator notes). Alternative: let a doodad stand by an open stretch of wall, away from where its start drops a
   wall runner.

6. **Free entry beside one.** (GDD §3, Side walls: "move past the outermost floor lane to jump onto the wall".)
   Placeholder: along a doodad by a wall, no one gets onto that wall from the floor (its front pushes a runner in its
   lane inward, its side blocks a switch from the next lane); the generator puts one only where nothing else goes on
   in any lane, from its push's lead to the level's spacing after it, so nothing there needs the wall.
