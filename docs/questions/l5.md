# Task L5: the wall-run gap's guide, only where the mechanic is introduced

The owner's request (October 17, 2026, `docs/USER_REQUESTS.md`): "For the wall run that is required to get over gaps that
span all floor lanes, there is a green ramp clearly showing the player what they need to do. This is fine for the first
zone where this mechanic is introduced, but I feel it is too easy in later zones, and the ramp should be removed, making
the player use their brain a little bit more. However, if the zone was providing the player with a speed boost that was
required for the player to get over the gap, then you can keep the ramp."

Built as `ZoneSkin.show_wall_run_guide` (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, The generator, Wall-run gaps on the track; Zone skins):
true in the Marketplace's skin (the zone that brings wall-run gaps in) and the grey box, false in every later zone's skin
(`data/skins/`: Casino, Corporate and its plaza floor, the Beach, the Dead Zone, the Golden Zone and its palace). No gap
needs a speed boost, so no zone keeps the guide for one: a gap is a standard wall run at the zone's own run speed
(`WallRunGapPlacement.shape_for`; Marketplace 22.6 m/s up to the Golden Zone's 25), with no speed pad, ramp or other
boost allowed in its clear stretch, and `test_wall_run_gaps` checks that no jump crosses it even with every boost.

1. **Is the stretch to step on readable without the guide?** Without the green chevrons the cue is the hole itself: the
   street ending ahead, in every lane, with its glowing edge. The runner has to be in the outer lane (the clear run-in,
   `approach_seconds` 0.8 s, is there for the lane changes) and step onto the wall in the stretch before the edge
   (`entry_window_seconds`, 0.5 s: 11.3 m in the Marketplace, 11.7 m in Corporate, 12.5 m in the Golden Zone). The
   real tolerance is a little wider than that stretch: a step up to `exit_margin_seconds` (0.25 s) earlier still
   crosses (0.75 s, 17 to 19 m before the edge), and one up to the jump grace (`coyote_time`, 0.08 s, about 2 m) past it;
   a step earlier than that drops the runner in (the wall run ends over the hole; the grapple's save puts them back on
   the wall), which is the mistake a player who runs at the wall as soon as they see the hole will make, and what
   "using their brain" costs. The other wall may carry a sign, a wall fence or a wall enemy where the gap's wall is
   guaranteed clear, so the runner now also picks the wall. Is 0.5 s (0.75 s with the margin) a fair window to hit by
   eye with no mark on the wall, at the later zones' speeds? It can only grow a little: the gap gets shorter by as
   much and must stay longer than any jump (about 1.2 s of run with every boost and the dash, against 1.41 s now). Or
   should the later zones show something else short of the green arrows (a sound at the edge, a sign far ahead)? The
   timing was not changed. (GDD §3, Side walls; task J2's item 1 in `docs/questions/j2.md`.)
