# L6: doodads more often

The owner, after playtesting task L3 (October 17, 2026): "The doodads are too rare and I don't end up seeing them in
my playtesting. Instead of three in a row, could it be two in a row so it doesn't take up as much room? Why do the
doodads require so much space? Is there a rule around it? To me, they do not add much extra difficulty, if any at all,
so we shouldn't have a rule there for fairness' sake." GDD §3, Zone doodads.

What changed: a doodad no longer needs every lane clear from its push to the level's spacing past it. It keeps what
fairness needs lane by lane (`LevelGenerator._place_doodads`; `docs/architecture/generator-track-features.md`, Zone
doodads), rows start at two, and the levels draw them spot by spot. The numbers are in the architecture note.

1. **What a doodad keeps clear, lane by lane.** GDD §3: "a push never lands the player on a gap or a hazard" and
   "it costs nothing else" (proposed). Placeholder (`LevelGenerator._doodad_lane_fits`, `_doodad_push_fits`,
   `LevelConfig.doodad_reaction_seconds` 0.5 s, `LevelGenerator.DOODAD_LANE_MARGIN` 3 m, DESIGN-TBD): its own lane
   holds nothing from a lane switch's room (the switch at run speed and 3 m) before its push to the level's spacing
   past its end (what it hides from the chase camera); the lanes beside it hold nothing from that room before its push
   to that room past its end, and the lane it pushes into nothing until 0.5 s past the push; lanes further out may
   hold anything (a hole or a fence two lanes over). So the push lands on clear floor, a runner beside it never has to
   dodge into its lane, and a runner further out keeps a free lane on their side. Is a free lane beside it on both
   sides right, or may a hole stand right beside one when it's jumpable (more of them at 3 lanes, where the middle
   lane's neighbours are every other lane)?
2. **How often.** Placeholder: the level is walked in spots `LevelConfig.doodad_spot_seconds` (1 s) apart; each spot
   with room for one gets one with the level's `doodad_share`, its front at a seeded place in the spot (it was: each
   free stretch got one, and the next spot in a long one another, which left a level's long free stretches nearly
   empty). The shares in `data/levels/*.tres`: the City's as they were (City 1 0.15, City 2 0.35, City 3 0.45: the
   room lane by lane is what grew there), Gangland 0.6 (Gangland 3 0.7, from 0.6), the Marketplace 1.0 (from 0.8), the
   Casino 0.85 (0.8), and 1.0 from Corporate on (0.6 to 1.0), where the room left (below, 4) is what limits them.
   Measured over each level's own seed and four others at 3, 5 and 6 lanes, doodads a minute went from 0.8 to 5.2 to
   1.7 to 9.9 (the City 5.9 at 3 lanes and about 9.8 at 5 and 6, Gangland 5.8 to 8.7, the Marketplace 6 to 7.2, the
   Casino 4.4 to 6.3, Corporate 4 to 5.6, the Beach 4.9 to 5.4, the Dead Zone 1.7 to 2.6, the Golden Zone 3.6 to 4.7;
   `docs/architecture/generator-track-features.md`, Zone doodads, has the table). City 1 went from 2.8 to 4.9 a
   minute. Is that the right amount, should the City at 5 and 6 lanes have fewer (it has the most room), and should
   City 1 stay gentler?
3. **The gap between doodads, and no slalom.** Placeholder (`LevelConfig.doodad_gap_seconds` 0.6 s, it was 2.5 s;
   `LevelGenerator._doodad_lane_fits`): the next doodad comes 0.6 s past the last one's end (it clears the last one's
   push), and closer than the level's spacing it stands two lanes away at least, never in the lane beside, where the
   last one's push lands (no slalom), nor in its lane, which it hides. At 3 lanes two doodads that close stand by the
   two side walls. Is 0.6 s right, and may two close ones stand in one lane (only rows do now)?
4. **Doodads stay off enemies' attacks in every lane.** The enemies' own fairness checks hold an attack back while a
   doodad stands in any lane along it (cyborg and window cyborg bolts, a drone's barrage, a hover truck's cannon, an
   Octodog's charge, a Resonator's pulse; item 274), and the generator plans those attacks on floor that's clear in
   every lane. So a doodad still keeps off what the fill pass keeps for every enemy, in every lane (its attack as the
   runner closes in), and off every floor cut's window (GDD §9.9: nothing else goes on meanwhile), every ceiling to
   the end of its landing zone and the level's quiet stretches (The Hush). That's what most of the later zones'
   levels hold (about 80 to 90% of a Dead Zone or Golden Zone level), so they get fewer doodads than the earlier
   zones even at a share of 1. Placeholder: `LevelGenerator.doodad_keep_outs`. Should doodads stand beside an
   enemy's attack in a far lane? That needs each of those enemies' checks made lane-aware first (an enemy task), so
   attacks aren't held back more often. Or beside a Buzz Overdrive's cut, two lanes from its lane and off its escape
   lane (as its company is, task L2)? Or in The Hush's quiet stretches, as silent wreckage (item 273's alternative)?
5. **Rows of two.** The owner asked for them; `LevelConfig.doodad_row_min` is 2 (`doodad_row_max` 4). GDD §3's rows
   note still says "three or four" (proposed, task L3): it should read "two to four" once the owner confirms. The
   chance a doodad starts a row: 0.5 by default, 0.25 in City 1 (gentle), 0.7 from the Beach on (the Beach, the Dead
   Zone, the Golden Zone), where room is short and rows carry more of the doodads (`data/levels/*.tres`). Rows now come
   1.7 to 3.9 a level from Gangland on (1 to 1.6 in the Dead Zone), and about half the doodads stand in one (43 to 72%
   by zone and lane count).
6. **Dash walls take out the doodads in their way.** With doodads in far more of a level's room, a level they leave
   short of its dash walls takes out the ones in the way of the rest (`DashWallRules._clear_doodads`: the face that
   fits with them out of the way and the fewest in its way; a row goes whole), before any feature gives way (a
   wall-run gap, task J2; an enemy, the last resort). A doodad also keeps a wall's footprint off its push's lead before
   it and a lane switch's room past it (`DashWallRules.doodad_span`; it was the level's spacing past it), as the
   doodads keep off a wall placed before them: a wall stands across every lane, taller than any jump, so a doodad never
   hides it. Placeholder, DESIGN-TBD. Should scenery always give way to a wall, or should a level keep its doodads and
   have fewer walls?
7. **The Beach's doodads by its walls.** The Beach's side walls stand about half the level (task D10b, the owner: "much
   longer sections where there aren't sidewalls"; `test_beach_levels` aims the median wall at 45 to 55%), and a doodad
   by a wall keeps the wall gaps off it (task L3). With more doodads the median wall stood on 55.0% (54.6% before), so
   the Beach's levels take the lane by a wall less often: `doodad_wall_lane_share` 0.15 in `data/levels/beach_1.tres`
   and `beach_3.tres` (0.6 elsewhere; L3's rule: "not where the generator ... needs it open"), the median then about
   54%. Placeholder, DESIGN-TBD. Should the Beach's doodads keep off its walls altogether, or its walls open less?
