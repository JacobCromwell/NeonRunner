# Questions from task M3 (the Centurion and Party Wolf outfits; the owner, October 18, 2026)

The briefs (`docs/art/BRIEF_OUTFIT_CENTURION.md`, `docs/art/BRIEF_OUTFIT_PARTY_WOLF.md`) and the owner's concept sheets left these
details open. Each has a placeholder, marked `DESIGN-TBD (docs/questions/m3.md, N)` where it lives
(`scripts/player/outfits/centurion.gd`, `party_wolf.gd`) and built so it's cheap to change. Nothing here blocks anything.

The frames mentioned come from the review tools (`CLAUDE.md`, Commands): `tools/showcase/avatar_showcase.tscn -- --shot=sheet|sheet-close|poses-back|equipment-back
--outfit=<id> [--look=armor|all]` and `tools/showcase/avatar_run_review.tscn -- --outfit=<id> --skin=<zone> --equip=armor`, rendered with
`godot --path . --fixed-fps 10 --write-movie build/f.png --quit-after 60 <scene> -- <args>` (add `--rendering-method gl_compatibility`
for the web renderer; the Forward+ frames need lavapipe under `xvfb-run`).

6. **"PARTY WOLF" is unreadable from the gameplay camera, and sits on the torso where the sheet has it on the skirt.** From the
   game camera each cell of the lettering is about one pixel, so there it shows as a band of pink and cyan under the neon wolf; it
   reads only in close-ups and the avatar showcase (about 9 mm a cell at design scale). The sheet sprays "PARTY WOLF" across the
   coat's skirt below the belt; here it is built in two lines of block letters (PARTY in pink over WOLF in cyan) on the lower back
   of the torso above the belt, because the skirt's panels swing apart as the legs move and would break the lettering. The colours
   (the sheet has pink with cyan splashes) and the size are mine. Would the owner rather have it bigger, say across the whole back
   with the neon wolf behind it, or on the skirt anyway, or the second line a different colour? A texture on a rigid plate would
   read better close-up but costs a draw call and doesn't follow the glow effects, so the flat strokes were kept.
   Placeholder: as built (`GLYPHS`, `_sign` in `party_wolf.gd`).
