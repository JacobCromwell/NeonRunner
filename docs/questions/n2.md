# N2: stars that can be earned

The owner's request (October 18, 2026): some levels may not allow three stars, at least the volleyball match (Beach 2) and
perhaps the Beach's boss; the volleyball's stars should be "based on how well you did in the volleyball match, not how
quickly you were able to get through". GDD §4, §5 (the Beach), §10 (bosses: stars and leaderboards).

What the audit found: the volleyball's stars were already by points won, never by time (a 4-0 match through the real flow
gave three stars and the profile recorded them), but a 4-3 win gave three too; every campaign level's three-star line is
within reach; every boss's three-star par is above the fight a clean bot wins at the shipped 5 lanes (the Floating Head's was
below it at 3 and 6 lanes). The table is in `docs/architecture/bosses.md`, Stars, and `docs/architecture/economy-platforms.md`,
Level stars.

1. **Does a lost match with 2 or 3 points earn two stars?** The owner's rule: "4 points to the rival's 0 is three stars, one
   point or none is one star, anything in between two stars". Read literally, a 4-1, 4-2 or 4-3 win is two stars, and so is a
   2-4 or 3-4 loss (the runner finishes the level whoever wins the match, and is paid by the points won). Is a lost match
   meant to be two stars, or one? Placeholder: the literal reading, as data on `VolleyballTuning` (`two_star_points` 2,
   `three_star_points` 4, `three_star_rival_points` 0: the most points the rival may have for three stars);
   `data/minigames/volleyball_tuning.tres` uses the defaults. A loss would be one star by making the rule "won the match" for
   two stars, a one-line change in `VolleyballTuning.stars_for`.
2. **Boss stars: by time (as built) or by hits?** GDD §10's proposal (never confirmed by the owner) is par times. Four bosses'
   fights are scripted so tightly that their clean time is the same to the tenth of a second at every lane count and speed
   (Hostile Takeover 68.6 s, Sleep Taker 139.0 s, Sewer Swarm 104.3-104.7 s, The House 80.4-81.9 s): their time can't be
   beaten by playing better, only matched, so their "three stars under 1:14" means "let no chance go by" (a missed chance costs
   14.6 s at the Sewer Swarm, 26.7 s at the Sleep Taker). Every one of them is earnable that way, so nothing is changed. Placeholder
   (built, used by no boss): `BossDef.star_rule` `HITS` stars the fight by the protection hits taken (the armor and shield blocks:
   `three_star_max_hits` 0 for three stars, `two_star_max_hits` 2 for two, else one), shown on the results screen as "3 stars: no
   hits taken · 2 stars: 2 hits or fewer"; a boss switches by setting `star_rule = 1` in its `data/bosses/<id>.tres`. Should any
   boss use it, and are those numbers right? A no-hit fight is stricter than an error-free one: with time, a boss's armor can
   absorb a mistake and still earn three stars. The Golden Convergence's checkpoint would also need the hits so far carried
   with the time (`RunContext.boss_resume`, in `BossEncounter`, a core file: not touched here).
3. **The Floating Head's three-star par is 123 s** (it was 110 s). A clean fight against it takes 100.6 s at 5 lanes (the
   shipped count) but 113.9 s at 3 and 114.0 s at 6, so three stars were out of reach at those counts; 123 s is the slowest
   clean fight with Hostile Takeover's margin (1.079) that every other boss's par uses. Placeholder: `data/bosses/city_boss.tres`
   (`three_star_seconds`); the two-star par stays 150 s. At 5 lanes the first boss now allows 22 s of slack. Tighten it to about
   108 s if the first boss's bar should stay close to a flawless fight at 5 lanes and 3 and 6 lanes are never shipped.
4. **The level stars' line is mostly cleared by kills from Gangland 1 on.** Three stars are 75% of the level's credits
   (`GameRules.three_star_share`), but the score also counts every kill (a stomped Cyborg 250, a Hover Truck 800), which dwarfs a
   credit's 1, 5 or 25. A runner who takes only the floor line reaches the three-star line in City 1 and 2, then 25-98% of it
   later; one or two stomped Cyborgs close the gap in every level (and every build has two or more), so no line is out of
   reach, but the later levels' stars are as much a kill count as a credit count. Is that wanted, or should the line count
   kills (a share of credits plus kills), or the credits alone? Placeholder: unchanged (`data/tuning/game_rules.tres`).
