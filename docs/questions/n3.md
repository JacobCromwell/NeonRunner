# Questions from N3 (shields stack)

## A boss fight's granted shield when the player brings a stack

**The question.** A boss may grant a shield (BossDef.granted_items, GDD §8: "never costs the player's own
stock"). Shields now stack, so a player can bring several of their own plus the grant. Does the grant add
one more shield on top of the stack, or is it one free charge inside it?

**The placeholder.** `Loadout.grant` still never adds a charge to a breakable the player already brings
(a stack of four stays four), and the granted item is one free break: `Loadout.costs_stock` lets the
picked-up charges, then the granted charge, break free, and every further break costs the player's own
stock. (Before this, a granted breakable's breaks were all free, which was one break.) Marked
`DESIGN-TBD` in `scripts/run/loadout.gd`.
