# Task O1: The Hush's extended wall runs

The owner's request (October 19, 2026, GDD §3 "Extended wall runs in The Hush"): three sections in The Hush (Dead
Zone 2) where an extended wall run is required, double and triple ones, with small platforms by the wall to recharge
the wall run on; a wall on both sides in one, on one side only in the others. Built as `LevelConfig.extended_wall_runs`
(`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, The generator, Extended wall runs). Numbers in `data/tuning/wall_run_gaps.tres` unless said
otherwise.

1. **"As long as the default wall run time."** The request says the double "will be as long as the default wall run
   time is" and the triple "as long as three default wall runs". Read as two and three standard wall runs (a double
   with one platform needs two runs), from the lit stretch to the far edge: 2.2 and 3.3 standard wall runs (4.7 s and
   7.1 s of run), the rest being the platforms' room to land. Is that what was meant? (GDD §3.)
   Placeholder: each hole an ordinary wall-run gap's (1.41 s of run), each platform between two.

2. **The platforms' size.** How small? Placeholder: one lane wide (the outer lane by the lit wall), and as long as the
   latest standard wall run over the hole before it takes to drop the runner on it plus `platform_slack_seconds`
   (0.2 s): about 1.2 s of run (29 m at The Hush's 24.2 m/s). Its last `entry_window_seconds` (0.5 s) is the stretch to
   step back onto the wall from; a step a little earlier still crosses (the drop off the wall carries the runner past
   the next hole's edge), a step much earlier comes down in it. Shorter would leave a runner who stepped on late with
   nowhere to land.
