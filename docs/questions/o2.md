# Questions from task O2 (the debris maze; the owner, October 20, 2026)

The owner's design (GDD §5, Zone 8, "The debris maze", "Shorter doodads" and "Ceilings") left these details open.
Each has a placeholder, marked DESIGN-TBD where it lives and built so it's cheap to change. Numbers are at the
Terminal's run speed, 24.6 m/s.

1. **"Stars stay time-based."** (GDD §5, Zone 8: "Stars stay time-based, so a stop and a wait for the dash cost
   stars"; but §6, "Level score", and the game have a level's stars come from its score: the credits collected as a
   share of the best score, `GameRules.stars_for`, with no time in them.) Placeholder: stars as built, so a stop and a
   wait cost time on the level clock but cost stars only through credits a runner misses. Should the Terminal's
   levels (or every level) get time-based stars, with par times per level the way GDD §10 proposes for bosses?

2. **How a stop feels.** (GDD §5: "a complete stop, facing it".) Placeholder: the runner stops at once at the
   doodad's front (no slowing down) and sets off at once at the run speed; the stop plays the push's thud and its
   small shake (`doodad_stop` in `scripts/run/run_world.gd` EVENT_SOUNDS, `scripts/run/run_effects.gd`;
   `SpeedFxTuning.push_shake_*`). Should a stop have its own sound or a stumble?

3. **Lane switches while stopped.** (GDD §5: "the runner sets off again by switching into an open lane or by
   dashing through".) Placeholder (`scripts/player/player.gd`, `_lane_blocked`, `_stop_motion`): a switch into a lane
   that's open beside the body sets the runner off; one into a lane with debris beside them is a blocked move (the
   clank and bump of a solid side) and they stay stopped; against a wall of debris whose piece in the next lane has
   its front level with this one's, the switch slides them along the wall's face, still stopped, so they can work
   along a wall to its opening. Is that right?

4. **Jump and slide while stopped.** Placeholder: they play out where the runner stands (no forward motion), and a
   jump into debris stops in the air at its front and comes down there (debris stands 1.9 m, out of a jump's 1.6 m
   reach). Should a jump or a slide do anything else?

5. **A switch that would meet debris's side.** Placeholder: a switch toward a lane whose debris starts within the
   switch's run ahead is refused like a blocked move (`Player._stop_switch_ahead`); one further ahead lets the
   switch go and the runner stops at that debris's front; a front corner caught by an odd frame's timing moves the
   body back out the way it came (`Player._start_bounce`, with the clank). Is that the right call?

6. **What one dash breaks.** Placeholder: the dash breaks every piece in its lane whose front it reaches while it
   lasts (`Player._dash_claims`, as everywhere), so a dash through a wall also breaks the start of a divider right
   behind it, and the runner runs on to the next piece's front. Should a dash break only the first piece it meets?

7. **The maze's shape.** ("Doodads stand so thickly that the empty space between them forms a maze.") Placeholder
   (`scripts/world/debris_maze.gd`, header): blocks of debris, each a row of walls (debris across every lane but
   its openings, medium or large pieces, fronts level) with rooms between them (lanes open from one wall to the next
   but its dividers: debris along a lane the room's whole length), so the open floor is cells joined through the
   walls' openings; a block has open floor in every lane before it (its run-in) and after it (its breather), and
   normal track between blocks. Is this the maze the owner pictured, or should it be freer (debris scattered with
   no rows)?

8. **How much maze, and how hard.** Every number in `DebrisMazeTuning` (`scripts/world/debris_maze_tuning.gd`) is a
   placeholder. Rubble Line (`data/tuning/terminal_maze_1.tres`): eight blocks over about two thirds of the level,
   blocks up to 14 s long, a 1 s run-in, a 3.8 s breather after each (so a wider gap fits between blocks), rooms
   1.4 s apart, a quarter of the walls large, a lane of a room a divider with chance 0.35, a lane of a wall open with
   chance 0.3. Last Stop (`terminal_maze_2.tres`): four blocks of up to 12 s over about a third of the level, 2.4 s
   breathers. Is that the right amount of maze for "the debris maze" and "a normal run with stretches of maze"?

9. **How many breaks a block needs.** ("Can't get through without dashing ... choose where to break through.")
   Placeholder: the least dashes a block takes is drawn per block in `breaks_min`..`breaks_max`: 1 or 2 in Rubble
   Line, 1 in Last Stop; with the dash at its slowest recharge (8 s) a runner may wait for it. Should blocks need more
   breaks further into a level, or with the difficulty?

10. **The Terminal's doodad height.** ("Lower than standard doodads ... still too tall to jump.") Placeholder: 1.9 m
    (standard 2.6 m; a jump's feet reach 1.6 m), `doodad_height` in `data/levels/terminal_1.tres` and
    `terminal_2.tres`; the Terminal's doodad pictures are drawn for it (`tools/godot.sh doodads --only=terminal`).
    Is 1.9 m low enough to read the maze over, and still read as too tall to jump? (The chase camera, 4.2 m up and
    7.5 m behind, sees the floor behind a 1.9 m wall about 20 m ahead again some 23 m past it.)

11. **The maze's ceilings.** ("Little shortcuts over part of the maze.") Placeholder (`DebrisMaze._add_ceiling`): a
    block gets one with `ceiling_share` (0.35 in Rubble Line, 0.5 in Last Stop): a one-lane ceiling over its last
    wall, its pad in a cell of the last room the runner can reach, a dead end where there is one (so it saves a
    dash), the cell two lanes wide or more (a runner who'd rather stay on the floor passes beside the pad), far
    enough past the wall before it to be seen after a dash through it; the rider comes down in the breather, clear
    of debris. About one or two a level. Is a ceiling that skips the last wall the shortcut the owner meant?

12. **A runner without the dash.** (GDD §5: "a player without it can't finish a maze level and has to go back and
    buy it. The owner accepts this.") Placeholder: nothing tells them; they stand at a wall with no open way until
    they quit from the pause menu. Should the game say so (a note on the level's intro, or a message when a runner
    without the dash stops where no lane is open)?

13. **The maze's first-encounter hint.** Placeholder (`data/hints/hints.json`, `debris_maze`, shown on Rubble Line's
    intro): "Debris here stops you dead instead of pushing you aside. Look ahead for the way through: switch into an
    open lane ({move_left} / {move_right}) or dash through the debris ({dash}). If the dash isn't back yet, you wait
    for it." The wording is open.

14. **The Terminal's other doodads.** Placeholder: every doodad of a Terminal level stops the runner
    (`LevelConfig.doodads_stop`), the zone doodads in the normal track between blocks too (most of Last Stop), placed
    by the same rules as a pushing doodad's (both lanes beside one clear). Should those push as elsewhere, so only the
    maze's debris stops the runner?
