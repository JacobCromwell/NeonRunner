# Questions from task O5 (the Terminal: more centipedes, and a maze section escaped by the anti-grav pad)

The owner's request (October 22, 2026): Rubble Line "about 20 to 30% more" Cybernetic Centipedes, Last Stop "about
double", and "a section of maze that will make it so the player needs to escape using the anti-grav pad", drawn as a
sketch (a key: `*` debris, `A` anti-grav pad, `O` anti-grav / ceiling landing, `.` open space, `|` the playfield's edge,
`C` a centipede; read bottom to top, the runner's way):

```
|..O..|   the ceiling landing past the barrier
|*****|
|*****|   the barrier: four rows of debris
|*****|
|*****|
|..A..|   the room with the pad
|.****|
|*****|
|*****|
|.....|
|*****|
|**C**|   a centipede in the maze before it
|.....|
|*****|
```

The placeholders are numbers in each Terminal level's maze tuning (`data/tuning/terminal_maze_1.tres`,
`terminal_maze_2.tres`: `escape_blocks`, `escape_from`, `escape_pieces`, `centipede_scale`) and the model in
`scripts/world/debris_maze.gd` (`_plan_escape_block`, `_add_escape_ceiling`), marked DESIGN-TBD, built so they're cheap
to change.

1. **A runner who misses the pad isn't stuck for good.** Placeholder: the barrier is debris like the rest of the maze,
   only too deep for one dash (four large pieces in every lane, about 26 m: one dash smashes three, at the Terminal's
   run speed). A runner who misses the pad stops at the barrier and needs a second dash, waiting up to 8 s for it, to
   break out, with any centipede that's awake coming for them meanwhile. An unbreakable barrier would leave a runner who
   missed the pad with no way on at all (the runner can't turn back). Is a long wait and a second dash the right cost of
   missing the pad, or should the barrier be something else (for example a solid train car that can't be broken, with a
   second pad as a fallback)?

2. **How many escape sections, and where.** Placeholder: one in each Terminal level (`escape_blocks` 1), the first maze
   block that starts at 55% of Rubble Line or later (`escape_from` 0.55, after the centipedes come in at 35%), and the
   first at 50% or later in Last Stop. Endless mode's copy of Last Stop (20 minutes) has one too, near its middle. More,
   or somewhere else?

3. **The section's shape.** Placeholder: an ordinary maze block (walls, rooms, dashes, centipedes as before) whose last
   wall opens onto an open room (no dividers) with the pad in it, then the barrier, then the block's usual breather. The
   pad's lane is drawn from the level's seed (the sketch has it in the middle lane); the pad lies as late as lets its lift
   carry the rider over the barrier, and the room is long enough before it for a lane switch and to see the pad after a
   dash through the wall before it. The sketch's row `.****` (one lane open beside debris just before the pad's room) and
   its double wall aren't built as drawn: the maze before the room is the usual random maze. Should the room have
   dividers, or the walls before it a fixed shape?

4. **A longer one-lane ceiling.** The Terminal's one-lane ceilings last at most 1.6 s from their pad
   (`LevelConfig.one_lane_ceiling_seconds`); the escape's has to reach over the barrier, so it lasts what that takes
   (about 1.6 to 1.7 s at the Terminal's speed, from its pad to just past the barrier's back, which a later speed change
   could lengthen). OK for the escape only?

5. **Centipedes in the escape section.** Placeholder: they may wait in its maze before the pad's room (as in the sketch),
   never in the pad's room itself; the proof that every block stays escapable takes the pad as the way out (the runner
   is safe once lifted, and a centipede gives up on a runner on a ceiling).

6. **The counts.** Placeholder: `centipede_scale` 1.35 in Rubble Line and 2.3 in Last Stop, which multiplies each
   block's drawn count (and the most a block holds: 3 and 5). At 5 lanes over 12 seeds: Rubble Line about 6.75 a level
   before and 8.4 after (about +25%), Last Stop about 4.4 before and 8.8 after (about double). A block only takes one
   where the proof finds the block still escapable, so the counts vary with the seed and are lower at 3 lanes, where
   there's less room to pass one. Close enough?
