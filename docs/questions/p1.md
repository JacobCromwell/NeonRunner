# P1: Razor Echo, the new player model

Questions from building Razor Echo (GDD §11, brief `docs/art/BRIEF_RAZOR_ECHO.md`). Each placeholder is
marked `DESIGN-TBD` in code. Frames to look at are listed in the task report.

1. **How each power-up looks on Razor Echo** (GDD §8; brief "Power-up looks", all *proposed*; supersedes "From
   the full build" item 35). Placeholders (`PlayerSuit._claws`, `_armor`, `_weapon`, `_magnet`): each in steel,
   white or its own colour, never the copper, so it reads as added on top.
   - **Weapon line:** a gunmetal emitter on a folding mount over the gold arm's (left) shoulder, growing by
     tier: a laser with a cyan light (1), a heavier laser with a cooling fin and a violet light (2), a two-tube
     missile pod (3), a heavy four-tube pod (4), white lights for the missiles; a light on its back shows the tier
     from behind. The tier colours are the shots' own (cyan, violet, white). Shots now leave the left shoulder
     (`PlayerSuit.WEAPON_MUZZLE`, `WeaponPowerup.MOUNT`); aiming and damage are unchanged.
   - **Claws:** three steel blades with a cool white glow out of a knuckle housing on each hand.
   - **Armor:** steel plates with pale edges over both shoulders (they widen the back-view silhouette), a chest
     plate over the vest and a yoke across the upper back. When the armor breaks, the plates burst off in 16 steel
     shards that tumble away and fade in 0.75 s (`PlayerAvatar._shatter`); nothing flashes.
   - **Magnet:** a gunmetal coil across the back of the belt with two azure windings (the magnet's pull already
     glows azure).
   - **Shield:** the existing bubble, unchanged, still pale cyan. It never used the old suit's trim colour, so it
     didn't follow the copper. Should it?
2. **The pattern across the back** (GDD §11: "a distinctive pattern across the back"). Placeholder
   (`PlayerSuit._back`, `_skirt_panel`): the sheet's pipes simplified for about 30 pixels on screen: two conduits on
   a rusted plate (a hook and a cross, after the sheet's "Lf" shape), joined and dropping into a forked Y to the
   belt, framed by two long conduits from the shoulders to the belt, and a loop down each back panel of the skirt.
   The right shapes?
3. **How bright and how deep the copper is** (GDD §11: "soft copper"). Placeholder: `PlayerSuit.GLOW`
   (0.96, 0.64, 0.46) at glow 0.34, shining by its own light (`GLOW_ALBEDO`). It renders as a pale copper (about
   RGB 250, 195, 140) on both renderers, well apart from the gap edges (253, 147, 33 and 253, 80, 17). A deeper
   copper moves toward the gap edges and enemy fire; `test_avatar` keeps it at least 0.3 from every hazard
   colour in hue and saturation. Keep it, or deeper?
4. **The rim light for dark tracks** (brief: "a subtle rim light must carry it there"). Placeholder: a faint
   pale steel-blue glow on faces seen edge-on (`PlayerSuit.RIM_COLOR`, `RIM_STRENGTH` 0.3), below the bloom
   threshold. Right colour and strength?
5. **Invulnerability and death in the new look** ("From the full build" items 36 and 37). Placeholders
   (`PlayerAvatar`): the invulnerability tint is now a pale copper white (it was pale cyan); on death the red
   flash stays (item 37 is still open) and the conduits and the implant go dark (glow 6%).
6. **The dash in the runner's copper** (brief: effects follow the new copper). Placeholder (`DashPowerup`): the
   shell, the speed lines and the enemy-smash burst use a pale copper (`PlayerSuit.GLOW_PALE`), and the shell is a
   little dimmer, so the additive shell never reads as a hazard's orange.
7. **How the coat moves** (brief: "a few stiff panels hinged at the waist"). Placeholder: four panels, knee to
   mid-calf (two behind the legs split by a vent, two at the open coat's front edges), following their thigh by
   half (front ones by 60%) through a spring that lags a little and overshoots slightly, trailing in the wind of
   the run, flaring when falling, trailing behind a slide, hanging toward the feet on the ceiling, and folding
   along the legs in a death. On a wall they also sag 12° toward real gravity (the brief only says how the
   ceiling looks). The numbers are F6 tunables ("Runner animation", Coat panels group:
   `data/tuning/avatar_animation.tres`). Right amount of motion, and should the wall sag stay?
8. **Details read off the sheet** (GDD §11). Placeholders (`PlayerSuit`): the scar runs across the right cheek, the
   right hand's fingerless glove is dark leather, the vest carries two small copper lights where the sheet had
   cyan and orange ones, the coat's lower hem is a dusty band (the weathering), and the high collar is open at
   the front with a copper conduit round its back. Anything to change?
