# P2: the ragged screen-head cyborg base

Questions from building the cyborgs' base look (GDD §9.2, brief `docs/art/BRIEF_CYBORG_GANGSTER.md`, Variant 1,
the "Static TV Head" Infiltrator). Each placeholder is marked `DESIGN-TBD` in code. Frames to look at are listed in
the task report.

1. **ERR before the screen goes dark** (brief: *proposed*). Placeholder (`CyborgBody.die`, `ERR_TIME`,
   `SCREEN_OFF_TIME`): a defeated cyborg's screen shows "ERR" in cold white (not the sheet's red, which means
   enemy fire) for 0.2 s, then switches off like an old CRT: the picture collapses into a bright line and goes
   dark over 0.14 s (with Reduced flashing it only fades). Both fit inside the quickest death animation (the
   claws' and the dash's, 0.34 s); a window cyborg's husk keeps its dark screen. The sparks of a cyborg's death
   are cold white now (they were orange, close to the player's copper). Keep the ERR, and is it long enough?
2. **The faces' pixel art** (GDD §9.2: "expressions readable on small screens"). Placeholder (`cyborg_kit.gd`,
   `FACES`, 13 × 9 LEDs): calm (square eyes, a flat mouth), aiming (brows running in from the corners into
   narrowed eyes, a long hard mouth), the panic variant's shocked "O" (wide ring eyes over a big round mouth), ERR,
   and the hosts' two corrupted faces (a wide grin, and broken eyes over a zigzag mouth). The features are drawn
   big and bold so each face keeps its own shape when the screen is only about 7 × 5 pixels, as it is 14 m ahead
   of the player at 720p (`test_cyborg_body` checks this); far away the shader averages the LEDs instead of
   point-sampling them. Right expressions?
3. **How much static on the screen** (brief: the sheet's static, recoloured). Placeholder (`cyborg_body.gdshader`,
   `static_amount`): a faint cold-white static (about 8% brightness on average) under scanlines, on dark glass
   that darkens toward its rounded corners. The sheet's screen is mostly static; here it stays faint so the face
   reads. More?
4. **How a host looks** (GDD §9.7; brief: purple glitch static on the screen, purple glowing veins along the neck
   and arms). Placeholder (`CyborgSuit._host_veins`, `cyborg_body.gdshader`): the screen keeps its white face,
   tinged purple, over a dim purple wash with blocks of purple static and rows that jump sideways (the corrupted
   faces as before); veins are thin purple lines up the front of the neck, branching down the front of the
   hoodie's sleeve onto the back of the hand, and along the front of the cyber arm and its cannon, pulsing slowly
   (steady with Reduced flashing). They stay a true purple: brighter, they drift toward the fences' pink. Right
   amount of purple, and the right places for the veins?
5. **How strung out it moves** (brief: gaunt, hunched and twitchy; a jittery idle and a shambling walk).
   Placeholder (`CyborgPoses`: `HUNCH`, `TWITCH_*`, `TREMOR*`, `LIMP*`): the chest hunched 11° with the screen
   raised to look ahead and the arms hanging slack; now and then (in about half of every 1.9 s) the head jerks by
   up to 8° / 13° / 11° (pitch, yaw, roll) in 0.05 s and settles over 0.3 s, with a hitch of the chest; a fine
   tremor in the free hand; and a shamble that drags the left leg (it lifts half as high, the body dips 4° onto it)
   while the heavy cannon arm swings less than half as much. Timings and reach are unchanged. Too much, too little?
6. **The window cyborg's window** (GDD §9.2; brief: only glows are the face, the charge-up and a host's purple).
   Placeholder (`WindowCyborg.WINDOW_LIGHT`): the light inside the window is now a dim, cold screen light (the
   feed on a TV in the room), below the glow threshold; it was a warm orange that read like the player's copper
   and the gap edges. Right?
7. **Details read off the sheet** (GDD §9.2). Placeholders (`CyborgSuit`): the TV is a grimy grey casing with a
   dented bezel (the top bar knocked askew, one side pushed in), vents in the sides and the chin, two knobs, rust
   and grime, and a darker tapered back; the vest is dark worn leather with turned-back lapels and flap pockets,
   open over an olive-khaki hoodie with a torn, frayed hem, its hood bunched round a ribbed black neck and its
   drawstrings hanging; the cargo pants are olive drab with a cargo pocket on each thigh, a faded patch on the
   right thigh, a knee patch on the left and a strap with a buckle round the right thigh; the boots are scuffed
   brown leather, laced. The backpack is an olive-grey metal unit (vents, a dial, rust, a side cell, webbing
   straps over the shoulders) with two black cables looping up into the back of the TV and a brown rubber hose
   (the sheet's is copper-coloured) over the right shoulder into the cyber arm. The cyber arm is rusted steel and
   gunmetal (a domed shoulder, plates, a piston, the hose) and its forearm is the cannon (a breech, a barrel with
   cooling fins, a rusted patch plate, a feed cable). The left hand is bare, pale and bony. Anything to change?
8. **The cult feed's face matches the cyborgs' now** (GDD §5, "Cyborg Viewing Devices"; OPEN_QUESTIONS "What the
   cult's feed shows"). The feed's placeholder picture (task D2, `cult_feed.gdshaderinc`) showed a smaller face
   with tall, close-set eyes; it now draws the cyborgs' calm face (the same proportions, smooth instead of LED
   dots), in the same cold white, so the billboards and the screen heads show one face. Keep them the same?
