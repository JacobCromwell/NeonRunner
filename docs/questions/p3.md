# Questions from P3, the cyborg zone variants

Brief: `docs/art/BRIEF_CYBORG_GANGSTER.md`, "Zone variants (task P3)"; GDD §9.2, "Zone variants". Review with
`tools/showcase/enemy_showcase.tscn`: `--view=lineup` (every look side by side; `--back`, `--host`,
`--face=aiming`), `--view=lineup_far` (at gameplay distance), and `--variant=brute|casino|vr_runner|burned|golden`
with the views `poses`, `faces`, `turn`, `window`, `far`, `charge` (ui_left / ui_right switch the look live).
Each placeholder is marked `DESIGN-TBD` with its number here.

1. **The Broadcast Brute's details** (GDD §9.2, Gangland; the sheet's Variant 3). Placeholder
   (`scripts/enemies/cyborg_looks/brute.gd`): the base's TV (the face in the same place) in a heavy, dark steel
   casing inside a cage of dull brass bars, clear of the screen; two small side monitors bolted to the cage, whose
   screens show a dim X in the face's cold white over faint static (the sheet's orange X screens; about a fifth of
   the face's brightness, dark when it dies, purple static on a host); a short antenna raked back off the TV (the
   sheet's rises behind the head; on the head it can't swing into the TV when the head turns, and it stays short so
   the Brute is no taller than the base); a brown work jumpsuit with a turned-up collar and a belt; dull, scuffed
   brass armour (a chest plate and a belly plate with rivets, pauldrons on both shoulders, knee pads); both arms
   heavy steel prosthetics with big fists (the sheet's); heavy boots with steel toe caps; a radio pack on the back.
   Its one weapon: the sheet's pipe as a crude pipe gun, a steel pipe held in the right fist and running out along
   the arm to the red emitter ring, with a valve, a gas canister and tape. Should the side monitors be dim screens
   like this or unlit dark glass? Is brass right for its armour (the brief allows it), or should it be rusted steel
   to keep brass further from Razor Echo's gold arm?
2. **The Casino Mob Enforcer's details** (GDD §9.2, the Marketplace; `cyborg_casino_enforcer.jpg`). Placeholder
   (`scripts/enemies/cyborg_looks/casino.gd`): the base's TV gilded (unlit, polished gold) with a gold bezel and a
   thin black lip round the screen, card suits engraved on it (a spade and a heart on the sides, a diamond and a
   club on top); a black suit with gold pinstripes open over a black shirt and a gold breastplate engraved with a
   diamond; black satin lapels edged in gold; a bandolier with brass cartridges; gold pauldrons and knee plates;
   gunmetal mechanical forearms and hands with gold rings; gunmetal shin guards; black shoes with gold toe caps; a
   black backpack with gold side panels and lid, braided gold cables into the TV. The drum-fed gun is compact and
   held one-handed along the right forearm (its stock under the forearm, the drum below with a spade and a heart
   on it, a perforated barrel shroud), since the cyborgs aim with the weapon arm alone (the sheet holds a rifle in
   both hands); the "SPADE" arm cannon is left out (brief). The screen's zone flavour: its LEDs are small diamonds
   (a card suit) instead of round dots, the same light far away; the faces themselves stay the shared ones. Is the
   one-handed gun right? Are diamond LEDs enough of a casino touch on the screen, or should the screen also show
   dice or card glyphs (which would change the faces' shapes, or add a moment the face isn't showing)?
3. **The Golden Zone's ceremonial enforcer** (GDD §9.2: "derived from the Casino Mob Enforcer by the art agent";
   §5: white, cream, red and gold, the cult shown openly). Placeholder (`scripts/enemies/cyborg_looks/golden.gd`,
   built from the Casino Mob Enforcer with its own palette): a cream suit with pale gold pinstripes over a red shirt,
   red lapels, the gold breastplate, a red sash from the right shoulder to the left hip (in place of the
   bandolier) and, on it, the Convergent Triad on a medallion: CultEmblem's drawing of the owner's choice in the
   look's unlit, polished gold meeting at CultEmblem's small red centre stone, on ivory enamel inside a gold rim
   (GDD §5). The shader fades the mark out while it spans fewer than about 24 pixels, as the skins do (the
   radiation-trefoil rule), so at gameplay distance the medallion is plain; the Triad shows as a cyborg comes
   close. Also gold fringes hanging from the pauldrons (epaulettes), ivory gauntlets and hands like white gloves,
   gold filigree on the TV's sides instead of card suits, and the gun in gold with an ivory stock and a red drum.
   The reds are deep and unlit, kept 0.3 from the charge-up's red on the colour wheel. Right palette? A cream
   cyborg may not stand out on the Golden Zone's white and cream walkways (D6a, not built yet): would a red
   tunic with cream trousers be better? Should the Triad also be worn larger (on the back, or the TV's top) so it
   shows from further away?
4. **The Wide-Aspect VR Runner and its visor** (GDD §9.2, Corporate; the sheet's Variant 2). Placeholder
   (`scripts/enemies/cyborg_looks/vr_runner.gd`; the faces in `cyborg_kit.gd`'s `VISOR_FACES`): a wide headset
   (0.42 m) strapped round a head in a dark mask, its visor the screen: 0.38 × 0.14 m, 19 × 7 square LEDs, flat
   and evenly lit (a visor, not a tube) with a little less static. The same expressions redrawn for the wide
   shape: the calm face's eyes far apart over a short mouth, the aiming face's brows slanting into narrowed eyes
   over a long mouth, the shocked "O" between two staring eyes, ERR in the middle, and the hosts' two corrupted
   faces. 14 m ahead the visor is about 8 × 3 pixels (the TV's screen 7 × 5); the test checks the three faces still
   differ there. The jacket's high collar covers the lower face; two cables run from the back of the head to a slim
   module on the back (the sheet's leave the visor's side; from the back of the head they stay plugged in when the
   head turns). A charcoal bomber jacket with a ribbed waistband and cuffs, a zip, pale unlit piping where the
   sheet has cyan trim, a few worn patches and a chest harness; olive-grey military trousers with knee pads and a
   thigh strap; dark trainers with pale midsoles; chrome hands; the sleek chrome arm cannon on the right; an unlit
   tablet (dark glass) in the left hand, face up in front of the chest when it aims. Are the visor's faces right,
   and do they read well enough at their smaller size?

6. **How burned the Dead Zone's cyborg is** (GDD §9.2: "the base, burned out: soot, ash, a flickering screen").
   Placeholder (`scripts/enemies/cyborg_looks/burned.gd`): every piece of the base under soot (68% of its
   brightness, half its colour, a little warmer), the TV's casing charred but lighter so the head still reads; pale
   ash on the TV's top, the shoulders, the pack's lid and the boots (the lightest thing on it, which keeps its
   outline on the zone's dark ground); scorch marks on the casing, the vest, a shin and a thigh; torn further: the
   hoodie's hem shredded into long strips, the left sleeve ripped open, a burn hole in the vest's back, a torn
   knee. The screen flickers (P2's flicker at three quarters strength: it drops to 40% for a ninth of a second
   about two and a half times a second, so the face never quite goes out; a steady 85% with Reduced flashing), is
   cracked across (a thin line through the face), and shows a little more static. No embers (they would glow a
   hazard orange). More or less burned? Keep the crack?
