# Task Q2: the Ice Lab's skin

The owner's request (October 21, 2026, `docs/USER_REQUESTS.md`; GDD §3 Zone doodads, §5 Zone 9): a frozen industrial laboratory, icy and
crystalline, brain jars (some holding a curled-up Octodog), level 1 leaning into the snow and the industry, level 3 into the laboratory.
Built as `IceLabSkin` (`scripts/world/skins/ice_lab_skin.gd`, `docs/architecture/skins-marketplace-corporate-deadzone-casino.md`, The Ice
Lab), in three leans (`data/skins/ice_lab_frost_skin.tres`, `ice_lab_skin.tres`, `ice_lab_lab_skin.tres`). Every number below is an export on
the skin (inspector, "Lean", "Hall", "Light", ...) unless said otherwise. All of it is DESIGN-TBD: the GDD gives the mood, the palette and the
reference image, not the pieces.

1. **The hall's shape.** How tall are the walls, how far is the far ice wall, and how high do the cranes ride? (GDD §5, Zone 9: "an indoor
   frozen hall with no sky, like the Terminal's vault: cold fog, ice walls behind the machinery".)
   Placeholder: the machinery wall is 12 m tall in bays 5 m long (`wall_height`, `IceLabWalls.SLOT`), the hall's far ice wall stands 14 m
   beyond it (`hall_extra`), crane runways ride at 16 m with a cross girder every 20 m (`crane_height`, `girder_pitch`), the frozen floor
   is 7 m under the street (`pit_depth`, deeper than a fall that ends a run). There is no sky: the horizon is the fog's colour, so the
   hall's far end runs out into a cold haze.

2. **The palette and the haze.** The GDD says "ice blue and white snow, frost-crusted black steel, cold fog, small amber machine lamps, the
   pale glow of the specimen jars" and gives no values. Placeholder: steel `(0.16, 0.19, 0.24)`, packed snow `(0.8, 0.86, 0.92)`, ice
   `(0.3, 0.5, 0.66)`, crystals `(0.62, 0.82, 0.95)`; the haze `(0.12, 0.18, 0.26)` in level 1's lean and `(0.085, 0.14, 0.19)` in level 3's
   (`fog_color_frost`, `fog_color_lab`: level 1 a little brighter and bluer, level 3 darker and greyer-green).

3. **The wall bays and their weights.** The GDD names the pieces ("lab machinery, consoles, jar racks and ice") but not how many kinds or how
   often. Placeholder: ten bays of 5 m (a run of pipes, a lattice pillar, a pressure tank, a wall of ice; a bank of consoles, a row of
   server racks, clean-room tile, a service door; an alcove of brain jars, an alcove with an Octodog among them), weighted by the lean
   (`bay_weights_frost`, `bay_weights_lab`: the industrial four are 78% of level 1's walls, 23% of level 3's). Everything below the
   wall-run band (`calm_top`, 6.6 m) is flush (nothing out of the face more than 12 cm: wall running), the jars stand in alcoves recessed
   0.8 m, and no alcove opens beside a window cyborg (a window cyborg's window must be the only one).

4. **The machine lamps.** How many, how bright, how high? (GDD §5: "small amber machine lamps"; the colour rule, looser from the Terminal on:
   `docs/art/reference/README.md`.) Placeholder: the Terminal's amber, a short bar light in a cage on 45-75% of the bays and on one cross girder in
   two, **never lower than 6.7 m** (above the wall-run band, so nothing glows at floor level where a gap edge's orange is, and nothing on the
   lane side of the wall in the band), glowing 9% of an albedo times the emissive scale (a fifth of a gap edge's, under the bloom threshold which the
   edge is over; 16% on the Compatibility renderer, which scales emissive colours in sRGB space), with a faint halo card; a hashed half
   of them breathe slowly (a smooth swell every 4-7 s), holding still with Reduced flashing. The reference image has orange lamps down on
   the pillars at head height; they are not at that height here because the colour rule keeps the band free of glows.

5. **The jars' and screens' light.** How pale and how dim? Placeholder: the jars' liquid `(0.42, 0.7, 0.78)` and the screens and status LEDs
   `(0.5, 0.74, 0.84)`, glowing 7% and 6% of an albedo times the emissive scale (11% and 10% on the Compatibility renderer), so each is far under
   the anti-grav pad's saturated cyan and under the bloom threshold; the screens show dull static readouts (bars, a waveform, rings, rows of
   dashes: never a word, a symbol or a face). A brain is a dull pinkish grey `(0.5, 0.43, 0.46)`, not the electric fence's pink.

6. **The Octodog in its jar.** On the walls, in the jar doodads (`octodog_jar`): how should a sleeping Octodog look, and how many jars hold
   one? (GDD §5, §9.4: "some jars hold a curled-up Octodog instead of a brain"; the colour rule: only hazards glow in hazard colours.)
   Placeholder: a squashed mass of dim green in OctodogModel's own tentacle greens (darkened) with tentacles spiralling round it, its robot
   chassis folded under it, **its three eyes shut** (dark red-brown slits, matte), **no glow at all** (the live one's toxic green and red are
   hazards' colours), in the jar's pale liquid, so it is recognisably an Octodog and never an attack. On the walls about 6% of the lab level's
   bays and 1% of the frost level's hold one (`bay_weights_*` BAY_OCTODOG, `octodog_in_jar_share`); in the lanes the small doodad class has the
   look `octodog_jar` among five, by the lean (affinity 0.85: about one small doodad in six at level 3, one in twelve at level 1). Question for the
   owner: should the doodad Octodog jars be rarer than that, and should the live one's three red eyes show, dimly, on the sleeping one?

7. **The ceilings.** GDD §5 names "crane gantries and overhead walkways". Placeholder: two kinds (`IceLabCeilings`): a GANTRY (a girder with
   its web and ribs, a rail, a hoist trolley and its cable drum on top, rods up to the crane runways) and a WALKWAY (an overhead grated
   walkway: grating seen from below, a railing with snow on it, lab cabinets and a lamp on the deck), weighted 3:1 in level 1's lean and 1:3
   in level 3's (`gantry_weight_*`, `walkway_weight_*`). Task Q3's falling gantryways build their own look; the gantry here is only a ceiling.

8. **The lean for each level.** The owner: level 1 "heavily" snow and industry, level 3 "much more heavily" the laboratory, level 2 in
   between (proposed). Placeholder: `lab_share` 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9 (the three skin files). It weighs the wall bays, the ceilings and the
   doodad looks, thins the snow on the floor (`snow_frost` 0.9 to `snow_lab` 0.14) and makes more of its floor panels clean tread plate, and
   tints the haze. Is 0.1/0.5/0.9 the right spread, and should level 2 be nearer one end?

9. **The doodads.** Which pieces, and how many looks a size? (GDD §3: "brain jars and other lab equipment", "ice crystal clusters and crates
   of lab supplies among them" (proposed).) Placeholder: five looks a size (small: a brain jar, **the Octodog jar**, a cryo tank, lab supply
   crates, a small crystal cluster; medium: a pair of jars on one base, a server rack bank, a lab bench, a big crystal cluster, a pallet of
   crates; large: a row of jars, a console bank, a mound of crystals, stacks of crates, a machine frozen in ice), each with a laboratory affinity
   (`IceLabDoodads.LOOKS`) that the lean's weights follow. **The Octodog jar** has its own look name (`IceLabDoodads.OCTODOG_JAR`, the manifest's
   `octodog_jar`), a way to ask for it (`IceLabDoodads.look_seed_for(&"small", &"octodog_jar")`), a way to test a seed
   (`is_octodog_jar`) and a metadata key on a built doodad's body (`IceLabDoodads.META_LOOK`), for task Q5; it pushes like any doodad (it is a card
   look on its box). The pictures are painted for the standard 2.6 m boxes (`DoodadArtSet.zone_tuning` reads the Ice Lab's levels' own
   `doodad_height` once Q1 adds them): **rerun `tools/godot.sh doodads --only=ice_lab` if Q1 sets a lower one**, or `test_doodads` fails.

10. **Motion, enemies and music.** The still catwalk gets the owner's motion cues as snow drifting down (thinning toward the lab end), puffs
    of cold mist and speed streaks (as every still floor). Its cyborgs wear the Dead Zone's burned base until the owner asks for one of their
    own (GDD §5: "an existing zone's look"); the zone has no dash walls (the skin keeps the base's default look, `test_dash_walls` lists the three
    skins) and no song of its own (task Q1).
