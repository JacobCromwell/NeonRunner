the golden zone"; proposed: "in all three of its levels, fixed and blinking alike with no introduction of their own (the
Ice Lab taught them), in the teleporter's own look so the mechanic reads the same in every zone". Built as proposed; the
falling gantryways and the Octodog jars stay in the Ice Lab (the owner: "For now, the Falling Gantryways should only be in

`tools/measure/teleporters.gd --rules` over 234 builds: the Golden Zone's three levels at 3, 5 and 6 lanes on their own
seeds and on the 25 other seeds the suites build them on (8101-8103, 609, 610, 9001-9008, 7101, 7102, 9101-9103, 9201,
9202, 7301, 7302, 7001, 7002, 9019).
