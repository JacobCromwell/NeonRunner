# R1: ramps' fading speed boost and the blocked-wall bump

Questions raised while building the owner's two decisions of September 26, 2026 (GDD §3): a ramp's
speed boost fades away the same way a speed pad's does, and a blocked wall entry plays the clank and
a small sideways bump. The behaviour is decided; these are the numbers and looks the GDD leaves open.

1. **How big a ramp's speed boost is** (GDD §3). Placeholder: a speed pad's, 6 m/s
   (`ramp_speed_boost` in `data/tuning/movement.tres`, marked `DESIGN-TBD`), fading like a speed pad's
   at their shared 4 m/s every second (`boost_decay_per_second`), so it's gone after 1.5 s. A ramp's
   wall run then covers about 43 m instead of 39 m, and the runner is faster through its first half.
   Bigger, smaller, or a speed pad's? (To tune after playtesting, as the owner said.)
2. **How far and how fast the blocked-entry bump goes, and how it looks on Razor Echo** (GDD §3: "a
   small sideways bump"). Placeholder: out toward the wall and back over 0.16 s (`wall_bump_time`), at
   most 0.35 m (`wall_bump_distance`), both in `data/tuning/movement.tres` and marked `DESIGN-TBD`. A
   sign that reaches down to the runner stops the bump at its face (about 0.28 m out), so it never
   takes the runner into it. The runner stays upright on the way out and leans away from the wall on
   the way back, as if pushed off it (`Player._switch_dir`, `DESIGN-TBD`). Right size, speed and look?
3. **Credits along a ramp's wall run when the runner has claws** (GDD §7, §8; unchanged by this task).
   The credits follow the path of a runner without claws. Claws make wall runs 1.5 times longer, so a
   runner with claws slides down more slowly and passes the last three credits (worth 35 of the 42)
   too high to take them. The level is generated before the run and doesn't depend on the loadout.
   Keep it that way (claws trade those credits for a longer run), or place the line so both paths can
   reach it (for example, fewer credits, all in the high first half)?
