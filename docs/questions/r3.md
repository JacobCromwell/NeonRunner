# R3: big attacks of different enemy types take turns

GDD §9 (decided September 26, 2026): the major attacks of different enemy types never overlap, so the player
never has to dodge two big attacks at once. The owner may revert it after playtesting, so it sits behind one
switch, `big_attacks_take_turns` in `data/tuning/game_rules.tres` (on; F6 panel, "Game rules"). Switched off,
the game plays exactly as before the rule. These are the choices the GDD doesn't settle.

1. **Which attacks count as big** (GDD §9; its examples are a drone barrage and a hover truck's lurch or cannon
   shot, and §9.7 names the Octodog's charge sequence and the drone barrage). Placeholder (each enemy's
   `is_major_attack_active()`, marked `DESIGN-TBD`):
   - **Big:** the Octodog's whole charge sequence (from its first wind-up until it gives up, so no other type
     attacks between its charges either); the drone's wind-up and barrage; the hover truck's rev and forward
     lurch; its cannon's charge and volley (the window shooters' bolts included); the Bad Dream's whole chase
     (see 3).
   - **Small, not taking turns:** a cyborg's burst, a window cyborg's shots, a screech's swipe.
   - **Not counted:** the hover truck's entrance (the banging, then the burst that hurts only a player on that
     wall section). The generator plans where it bursts out, so it can't wait for a turn. With the rule on, a
     big attack was open during 13 s of trucks' entrances over the measured runs (the same as without the
     rule). Should the entrance count? The others would then have to be kept from starting a few seconds
     before each one.
   Right list?

2. **When a big attack is over.** Placeholder: when nothing of it can reach the player any more: the lunge has
   passed them, the lurch has ended, and the last bullet, shell or bolt is behind them (0.5 m behind their
   hitbox, `EnemyDirector.SHOT_PASS_MARGIN`, watched where it really is) or gone. The next one's warning may
   start from then. A warning never waits once it has started, so the player's read of a warning always stays
   true. OK?

3. **The Bad Dream's chase holds every other type's big attack for its 20–30 s.** GDD §9.7 already kept
   Octodog charges and drone barrages out of a chase; with the rule, a hover truck's lurch and cannon shot
   wait too (the brief counts the whole chase as one big attack). A truck that bursts in during a chase holds
   back or paces until the chase is over. In Golden 3 at 5 lanes, one arrives during a 25 s chase and gets
   one lurch in before its stay ends, and no cannon shot (3 shots and a lurch without the rule). The chase
   itself can't wait: it starts when the player kills a host, and an attack already on when it starts
   finishes first (the Bad Dream holds its slash until then). The other way would be to count only its
   slashes: other types would attack between them, and it would hold a slash while theirs are on. Which?

4. **Who goes first, and how long an Octodog waits.** Placeholder (`EnemyDirector.major_attack_blocked`,
   `DESIGN-TBD`): of the enemies waiting for their turn, the one that has waited longest goes next (on a tie,
   the one in play longest), so none is kept waiting for ever by others that keep attacking. While they wait,
   they carry on: a drone keeps following, a truck holds back behind the player (for its lurch) or keeps
   pacing ahead (for its cannon; a cannon shot that doesn't get its turn before the truck's pacing ends is
   skipped), and an Octodog paces in position in front of the player. An Octodog's charges are planned for
   particular stretches, so while it waits its whole planned sequence moves on with it, for up to **4 s**
   (`turn_wait_max` in `data/enemies/octodog.tres`), keeping the planner's margins (a clear stretch, no other
   enemy near it, never into the level's last 40 m); after that its usual 40 m of slack runs out and it runs
   off. Kept from charging by a Bad Dream's chase (GDD §9.7), it runs off as before. OK?

5. **Keep the rule after playtesting?** (The owner found early playtests not very challenging.) Measured over
   every campaign level at 3, 5 and 6 lanes (`tools/measure/big_attacks.gd`: 45 simulated runs, 6,298 s of
   play, a god-mode runner in the middle lane who stomps every host it passes):
   - **Without the rule**, big attacks of different types overlap for **60.5 s** in all (61 times; 1.3 s per
     run on average, 7.3 s at worst, in Dead Zone 2 at 3 lanes), mostly a drone barrage with a hover truck's
     lurch or cannon shot (40 s); then the Octodog with the drone (7 s) or a truck (7 s), the Bad Dream's
     slash with a truck (5 s), and all three at once (2 s).
   - **With it**, never (0 s).
   - **What it costs:** 13% of the trucks' cannon shots (94 against 108), 6% of their lurches (78 against 83)
     and 5% of the drone barrages (221 against 232); none of the Octodogs' charges (77) or the Bad Dream's
     slashes (98). No enemy goes a whole level without a big attack. About one attack in eight waits for its
     turn: 2.2 s on average for a drone barrage (up to 20 s during a Bad Dream chase), 2.7 s for a truck's
     lurch (up to 9 s, the chase in 3), 1.7 s for a cannon shot and 2.3 s for an Octodog's charges.

6. **The enemies still to come** (their tasks decide; proposals): the **Buzz Overdrive**'s rev and charge
   (big; but the generator plans its cut in advance, GDD §9.9, so like the truck's entrance it can't wait:
   the others would have to be held off before its rev); the **Resonator**'s pulse, chime to shockwave (big);
   the **Gilded Sentinel**'s halberd swing (its wall section and the outer lane only: big or small?); the
   **Barnacle Turret**'s burst (small, like the cyborg's; "only one fires at a time" stays its own rule); the
   **Tithe Collector** (not an attack: touching it costs credits, not the run).
