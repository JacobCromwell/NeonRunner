# R7 — credits and shop prices after the playtest changes

Measured with `tools/godot.sh test` then `tools/measure/economy.gd` (default options: 3/5/6 lanes,
each level's own seed plus 2 others, a good run standing in for 70% of a level's credits — GDD §7
names no number, so this is reported, not a design decision). All of the below is one clean
playthrough, in campaign order, with nothing bought along the way (the floor for what's in reach;
real play mixes in deaths, which pay less, GDD §4).

## The curve (per zone; 5-lane average, rounded)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Gangland | 3 | 2,598 | 1,819 | 2,419 | — (placeholder) | 4,561 |

| Golden Zone | 3 | 2,305 | 1,613 | 2,888 | — (placeholder) | 13,281 |

A level's own finish payout runs 473 (city/1) to 999 (golden/2); a death or quit pays 74–137 (20% of
the good run's credits, never more than finishing, GDD §4 — this is structural: the catalog can't
break it).

Everything else already landed close to where it matters once G1's and G4's numbers were in: Claws is
in reach exactly at Octodog's debut (gangland/2, "claws beat tentacles"); the Dash, Slow time and
Weapon II land together at the top of Gangland; Weapon IV is a Golden Zone capstone, not needed
anywhere (every boss is beatable with only what it grants, GDD §8). Only Armor I needed a change: at
800 it took two finished levels (or about 11 death attempts at ~74 credits each) to reach; the owner
died a lot in the playtest, and GDD §4 asks for the first armor tier to come early, so it's now priced
to be in reach after a single clean run of City 1, or about 5 death attempts.

**Mobile packs** (`StubBackend.FAKE_PRODUCTS`, placeholder, OPEN_QUESTIONS §7): the $0.99 pack (1,000
credits) and the $4.99 crate (6,000 credits) are both already reachable for free within the campaign
(by city/2 and corporate/1), so they read as a convenience, not a requirement (GDD §7, GDD §2: PC has
no purchases at all). Left unchanged; noted here since they weren't ours to price.

**Not touched:** the per-credit denominations (1/5/25/100) and their placement are in
`scripts/world/level_generator.gd` (core, out of this task's scope) — this task only adjusted
*prices and payouts*, not where credits sit. Boss payouts (`data/bosses/*.tres`,
`BossDef.payout_credits`) are bundled with the rest of the boss numbers under OPEN_QUESTIONS item 27
(health, defeat score, par times); only two bosses are built (City, Dead Zone) and both already pay
the same flat 500, so this task left that question to whoever resolves item 27, rather than picking at
one number in it. The star thresholds (`GameRules.two_star_share`/`three_star_share`) still fit: they're
a share of a level's own credit total, so they're scale-invariant to G1's pace changes, and a clean run
collecting well above the 70% stand-in clears three stars on credits alone, before any kill or stomp
bonus. `armor_tier_hits`/`armor_tier_recharge` (the alternating hit/wait curve) weren't changed either:
they already line up with the zone timing above.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Armor I's new price (GDD §4, §8).** 350, reachable after one clean run of City 1 (or ~5 deaths).
   **Alternative:** cheaper still (so even a first attempt's partial credits cover it), or a flat
   starting discount instead of a price cut.
2. **Weapon IV only comes into reach in the Golden Zone (level 13 of 15)** under a single clean
   playthrough. Nothing needs it (every boss is beatable with what it grants), so it reads as an
   end-game capstone purchase. **Is that the intended feel, or should Heavy missile be reachable
   earlier** (e.g. by Corporate, where Buzz Overdrive first makes a weapon's damage matter)?
3. **The armor tiers' price curve after Armor I's cut**: Armor I is now a cheap starter (350) and
   Armor II is 4.9x that (1,700), versus 2.1x before. **Leave II–IV as the existing escalating sink
   they already were, or pull them down too to keep a smoother step?**
