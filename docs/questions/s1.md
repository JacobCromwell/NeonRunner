# S1: the Heavy Cyborg (GDD §9.16)

Questions raised building the Heavy Cyborg (owner, October 23, 2026; the GDD's *proposed* parts built as written). Every
number is in `data/enemies/heavy_cyborg.tres` (HeavyCyborgTuning, in the F6 panel) unless said otherwise.

1. **How many, and where they come from** (§9.16 "Where", §5's level schedule). Placeholder (`heavy_cyborg_rules.gd`,
   DESIGN-TBD): Heavy Cyborgs have no pattern of their own; they take the places of some of the level's plain cyborgs
   (never a host or a cyborg a rule planted), after the zone doodads, where one fits (its stream's zone clear, off dash
   walls, wider gaps, wall-run gaps and calm stretches, spaced `spacing_seconds` = 3 s, never the level's first cyborg
   nor its last plain one, nor one the danger density pass added).
   The first that fits past the start always becomes one; each later one with the chance `share` (0.2) times the
   campaign's recency factor (0.8 in Relay Station, 0.5 in Specimen Vault and Golden 1, 0.35 in Golden 2, 0.25 in
   Golden 3). So the rest of every level is built exactly as before. On the campaign's own seeds at 5 lanes that gives
   Relay Station 1, Specimen Vault 2, Golden 1 and Golden 3 one each and Golden 2 none (its one cyborg of its own stands
   just past a wider gap, where a stream would hardly fire; it has some at 3 and 6 lanes), and a build whose cyborgs leave
   no place has none: it is never forced (`OCCASIONAL`; Relay Station at 6 lanes has none). The rules that let a
   cyborg stand nearer than any other enemy (a dash wall's, a wider gap's, a wall-run gap's, an Enforcer Truck's
   showing) keep a Heavy Cyborg to the rest's rule, which leaves it fewer places; treating it there as the cyborg it is
   (its stream is held by all of them as a cyborg's burst is) would give it more, a change to those rules. Built first
   with a pattern of its own, it reshuffled every Ice Lab and Golden level (each picks differently from its first Heavy
   Cyborg on), which cost some of their own builds a teleporter, a dash wall or a wider gap and the generator about half
   a build more a level. Is "a few cyborgs are Heavy Cyborgs" the right amount, or should they be more common (their own
   picks, and the levels' layouts change)?
2. **When Relay Station brings it in** (§5: "then the Heavy Cyborg", proposed level). Placeholder
   (`data/levels/ice_lab_2.tres`, `feature_starts`): from 0.3 of the level, after the teleporters' start (0.15) and
   their introduction; the first Heavy Cyborg is the first cyborg that fits past it, so how soon it comes depends on
   where the level's cyborgs stand.
3. **The Golden Zone's share** (§5: "in all three levels with no introduction of their own"). Placeholder: the Golden
   Zone dates it from the Ice Lab, as it does the teleporters (a level old in Golden 1, so `share` × 2.5 there, not the
   introduced × 4), DESIGN-TBD in `tests/suites/test_campaign.gd` (`_ages_but_ice_lab`).
4. **The stream's numbers** (§9.16 "Attack", §9.6). Placeholders: a 1 s wind-up (`charge_time`); 6 rounds
   (`bullets_*`), reaching a runner on the line 0.18 s apart early and 0.16 s late (`bullet_gap_*`, the drone's), all
   within 0.9 of the hit invulnerability window (`invulnerability_share`); rounds at 18 to 22 m/s (`bolt_speed_*`),
   2.4 to 1.8 s to reload, engaging from 70 m (`engage_distance`, so a whole stream fits before the runner reaches it);
   the first round flying at least `min_warning_time` (0.75 s, a cyborg's) and every later one at least half of it
   (`later_round_warning_share`), or the stream waits.
5. **Its walk** (§9.16 "Movement", proposed: "a cyborg's, heavier"). Placeholder: 0.9 m/s toward the runner
   (`walk_speed`), up to 6 m (`walk_max`), dropping back at 6 m/s once passed, keeping the cyborgs' 10 m margin from
   obstacles.
6. **Its build** (§9.16 "Look", proposed: about 1.4 times a cyborg's build). Placeholder (`heavy_cyborg_body.gd`,
   `cyborg_looks/heavy.gd`, `model_scale`): drawn 1.15 times its zone's cyborg, each segment 1.3 to 1.5 times wider and
   deeper (never longer, so the poses and walk keep their reach), shoulders and hips 1.3 times wider apart, and armour
   plates over the clothes (breastplate, belly bands, tasset, pauldrons, upper-arm plates, bracers, thigh plates, knee
   and shin guards) in each look's own dull metal. The gatling (six barrels, a drum of ammunition on its left, a carry
   handle) hangs at the hip from the chest, the left hand on the handle and the cyber arm's cannon plugged into a
   coupling at its back, which hides the cannon's emitter ring (on a Heavy Cyborg only the face and the gatling glow).
7. **Its hitboxes.** Placeholder (`heavy_cyborg.gd`): a solid body 0.7 × 1.12 × 0.45 m (below the stomp line) and a
   stompable top 0.9 m wide from 1.18 to 1.6 m (above a standing runner, its top at a jump's highest feet): smaller than
   its drawn body, as every hitbox is.
8. **The warning's look and sound** (§9.16 "Attack", proposed: the barrels spin up and glow red, a red aim line on the

   steady on the locked line while it fires; a short muzzle flash with each round (none with Reduced flashing). The
   wind-up's sound plays on the way from the gatling to the runner, no further than 24 m from them

9. **Its sounds** (`tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_heavy_cyborg.gd`). Placeholders: `heavy_cyborg_windup` (a motor grinding
