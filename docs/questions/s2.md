# Questions from task S2 (the Terminal's intro, fixes; the owner's four requests, October 23, 2026)

The owner watched the Terminal's intro cinematic (task F2k) and asked for four fixes: no walking through the grey block on the way to
the sign, a better spark sound for the sign (a longer electrical zap, or none), a hands-and-knees crawl up the rubble, and floor under
the landing inside. The requests themselves are settled; these details of how they were filled in are not in the design document, and
each has a placeholder built so it is cheap to change (`scripts/cinematics/terminal_intro/`, `data/cinematics/terminal_intro_tuning.tres`,
`scripts/cinematics/cine_poses.gd`).

1. **How long the intro may now run.** GDD §6 (Cinematics, "Terminal intro") sets no length; F2k's brief suggested about 25 to 32 s. A crawl
   at a believable pace up the 6 m of rubble takes about 4.6 s, where the old scramble took 2.8 s, so everything after the climb moved
   1.2 s later. Placeholder: 33.2 s in all (`duration`, `fade_at`, `card_at`, `inside_at`, `jump_at`, `climb_from`, `crest_at`,

   1.5 s to 0.9 s to give some of it back. The alternative is to shorten an earlier beat (the facade shot is 4.4 s) by that much.

2. **The crawl's pace and look.** GDD §6 says only that the runner climbs the rubble; the owner asked for hands and knees, "naturally".
   Placeholder (`CinePoses.crawl`, constants `CRAWL_*`): the body pitched along the slope, the diagonal hand and knee reaching together,

   to look ahead at the crack; the camera rides with them from beside the heap (`climb_cam`). A slower, more labouring crawl would need more time
