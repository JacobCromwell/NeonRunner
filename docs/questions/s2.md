# Questions from task S2 (the Hunter; GDD §9.17, the owner, October 23, 2026)

The owner decided what the Hunter is, how it looks, that it comes up alongside and slashes, stays at the runner's side,
doesn't wait for other big enemies, takes 25 laser hits, dies under a falling gantryway the runner triggers, follows about
as long as the Enforcer Truck (25 s), can be escaped with teleports and ceilings, and hovers over gaps. Everything else is
GDD §9.17's proposal or task S2's placeholder, marked DESIGN-TBD in `scripts/enemies/hunter.gd`, `hunter_rules.gd`,
`hunter_tuning.gd` and `tools/asset_gen/sfx_bank_hunter.gd`, its numbers in `data/enemies/hunter.tres` (F6 "Enemy:
hunter"). The architecture notes are `docs/architecture/enemy-hunter.md`.

1. **The laser beside the runner.** The owner: it "stays on the player's side when it does its slashing attacks, making
   it difficult for the player to damage it with dashes or with laser attacks". As built, auto-fire picks it like any
   target (`Enemy.targetable`; `EnemyDirector.targets_ahead` takes anything from 1 m behind the muzzle), and its shots hit
   it beside the runner, so a runner with laser tier 1 (one shot every 0.32 s) destroys it in about 8 s of its 25 s chase,
   one with heavy missiles in about 5 s. Should being beside the runner keep it out of auto-fire's reach (it would then be
   hittable almost never: it comes from behind and rides level with them), slow the weapon down against it, or is 25 hits
   the whole answer? Placeholder: a target like any other.

2. **The dash and its solid side.** GDD §9.17 (proposed): "the dash passes through it safely ... and does it no harm". As
   built, its body's hitbox is harmless (`Hazard.harmless`: the dash, the claws and a stomp do nothing to it, and it does
   nothing to the runner), but its solid side (a lane blocker, as the hover truck's) bumps back a lane change into it
   whether or not the runner is dashing (`Player._lane_blocked` lets a dash through only a breakable doodad). Should a
   dashing runner pass through its side into its lane? Placeholder: bumped back, unharmed.

3. **Arriving and riding beside the runner.** Placeholder: it arrives where its entry's `at` says, from `arrive_gap` (30 m)
   behind, closing at up to `approach_speed` (11 m/s faster than the runner), into a lane beside theirs, its middle
   `alongside_ahead` (0.6 m) ahead of theirs (its front level with them, on screen: test_hunter checks every lane and side
   at 3, 5 and 6 lanes through the chase camera's resting view), its torso turned back toward them. Its hover whine
   (`hunter_whine`) rises as it arrives and each time it comes up again.

4. **Following a moment later.** Placeholder: it takes the lane beside the runner's on its side `follow_delay_seconds`
   (0.25 s) after they change lanes, its own lane change taking `switch_seconds` (0.25 s). The Enforcer Truck's delay is
   0.8 s; the Hunter's must be shorter for the bait: a walkway lands 1 s after its pillar breaks, and the runner steps out
   a reaction (about 0.35 s) after that, so the Hunter has to be in the walkway's lane within about 0.5 s of their step.

5. **Which side it rides on, and lining up the bait.** Placeholder (`Hunter._pick_side`, `_bait_side`, `_run_up_bait`):
   it comes up on the runner's far side from the next planned bait's walkway lane (so they reach that lane stepping away
   from it), where they could then step out of the walkway's lane away from it onto open floor (the pillar's push side,
   kept clear by the gantryways' rules, or the other side where it's open); otherwise, and with the runner already in the
   walkway's lane, on the side away from the push; with no bait ahead, toward the nearer edge (the runner keeps the most
   lanes on their other side); otherwise seeded. Over a bait's run-up (`bait_hold_seconds`, 2.5 s before its pillar) it
   keeps out of the walkway's lane, riding one lane further out, so a runner it followed past that lane can step back in.
   Left open: a runner two or more lanes on the push side with it between them and the walkway's lane can't line up that
   bait (it never lets them past). Should it drop back and come up on their other side then?

6. **The flurry.** Placeholder: its warning `warning_seconds` (0.85 s): both blades raised over its head, its eyes and
   blades brightening, a steel scrape (`hunter_scrape`, as long as the warning) and a red mark on the runner's lane from 2 m
   behind them to 6 m ahead (`mark_behind`, `mark_ahead`), widening and pulsing (only widening with Reduced flashing); then
   `cuts` (4) quick cuts over `flurry_seconds` (0.5 s) into that lane (`hunter_slash`; the cuts' box is the lane less
   `side_margin`, 0.25 m, each side, `slash_height` 3.0 m high, over a whole jump and under any ceiling rider, and
   `slash_depth` 2.2 m long around the runner); then `recover_seconds` (0.4 s). Its first flurry `first_slash_seconds`
   (1.4 s) after it comes up beside the runner, each next one a seeded 2.4 to 3.2 s after the last one's warning. One
   flurry is one hit: armor or the shield takes the first contact and the 1 s invulnerability covers the rest. It holds
   its lane through a warning and its cuts.

7. **When it may slash.** Placeholder (`Hunter.may_slash`): only at a runner on the floor in the middle of their lane, with
   it settled beside them and the lane on their other side open to step into (no hole, floor cut, doodad, live fence,
   floor enemy or solid side) from `escape_reaction_seconds` (0.3 s) after its warning to its last cut; never in the
   level's calm start, from `bait_hold_seconds` (2.5 s) before a standing gantryway's pillar in any lane until its calm
   stretch is over (the bait stays clean), in a teleporter's calm stretch in the runner's lane or a wall-run gap's
   crossing, nor a flurry that wouldn't end before it gives up. A runner who takes a pad onto a ceiling, steps onto a
   wall or teleports during a warning or the cuts calls them off.

8. **Steering and making room.** Placeholder: it looks `steer_ahead_seconds` (0.6 s) ahead in its lane for what its body
   must go round (a doodad, a gantryway's pillar, a live fence, a teleporter's pad, a ramp, a standing dash wall, another
   enemy on the floor): it takes the lane the runner has just left at once, else the lane further out, else it makes room
   (drops back to `yield_gap`, 9 m, behind the camera, at up to `drop_speed`, 16 m/s, and comes up again no sooner than
   `regain_seconds`, 1.2 s). It also makes room where the runner's lane holds what they must leave within
   `make_room_seconds` (1.2 s: a doodad, a floor enemy, a walkway falling on them, another enemy's lane attack a jump
   doesn't dodge) and the lane on their other side isn't open, and before a wall-run gap's crossing. It hovers over holes,
   gaps and floor cuts.

9. **Ceilings, walls and teleports.** Placeholder: below a runner on a ceiling or a wall it waits beside their lane (the
   outer lane under a wall), changing sides at an edge, never cutting. A teleport leaves it behind: it catches up from
   where the jump left it, but from no further back than `catch_up_gap_max` (70 m) and no nearer than `arrive_gap`, its
   time running on (GDD §9.17, proposed). One still waiting for the runner whose spot the jump skipped never comes.

10. **Giving up.** The owner: about 25 s, as the Enforcer Truck. Placeholder: counted from its arrival whatever happens
    (teleports, ceilings, waiting behind), a warning or cuts on called off; it drops back at `leave_speed` (9 m/s slower
    than the runner), its turbine winding down (`hunter_give_up`), and leaves play `gone_gap` (40 m) behind.

11. **Turns and other enemies.** The owner: it doesn't wait for other big enemies. Placeholder (GDD §9.17, proposed): nor
    do they wait for it (`is_major_attack_active` stays false), and other enemies' charges pass it by
    (`charge_can_hurt`).

12. **Where it comes in.** GDD §9.17 (proposed): only where at least one falling gantryway comes during its chase; never
    two at once. Placeholder (`hunter_rules.gd`, the numbers in the tuning's Placement group): at most `per_level_max` (2)
    a level, each chase's end and drop back `spacing_seconds` (6 s) before the next arrival; its bait's pillar from
    `bait_after_seconds` (6 s) after its arrival to `bait_before_seconds` (4 s) before it gives up, a seeded 8 to 14 s
    after where it can; the bait's Hunter lane (beside the walkway away from the push) clear of doodads, live fences,
    teleporter pads, ramps and floor enemies from the pillar's lead to `bait_lane_after_seconds` (0.9 s) past it at the
    fastest, plain fences there taken out; a calm arrival (the brief): no other attack planned in the `arrival_calm_seconds`
    (3 s) from its arrival, the busy stretches being floor cuts, wall-run gaps, dash walls, the debris maze, picks, Octodog
    charges and every rule's every-lane calm keeps. A level with no calm arrival for any bait still gets one, arriving
    where only the calm is missing (on Specimen Vault's own builds it never comes to that).

13. **Its introduction.** The brief: Specimen Vault, the feature's start after the blinking teleporters' (0.15).
    Placeholder: `feature_starts` "hunter": 0.4 in `data/levels/ice_lab_3.tres`, and the gantryways' rules hang the first
    Hunter's bait `intro_bait_min_seconds` to `intro_bait_max_seconds` (12 to 18 s) of run past that start (task S2's hook,
    `GantrywayRules.wanted`, both lanes beside it clear where it can; else anywhere 6 to 21 s past it), so the first one
    arrives about as the feature starts and shows its flurry before the bait. Specimen Vault keeps 3 gantryways (3 or 4,
    starts of 0.33 or 0.4 measured alike). Over its own builds at 3, 5 and 6 lanes and 8 other seeds each, and the
    weekly challenge's Ice Lab weeks: 46 Hunters in 31 builds; one build (6 lanes, seed 9002) has none (its only usable
    bait has no arrival free of attacks), 4 introductions come later than test_campaign's introduction reach.

14. **Specimen Vault's decoy jars.** Its Hunters' calm arrivals (every lane, 3 s of run each) and the gantryway hung for
    the first one's bait take room the zone doodads used: on its own 5-lane build only two decoy jars found room, where
    task Q5 asks for all of the level's count at five lanes (test_octodog_jars). Placeholder: Specimen Vault's
    `octodog_jar_decoys` 3 → 2 (`data/levels/ice_lab_3.tres`; Relay Station keeps 3, Frost Foundry 2). Or should the
    decoys take precedence (a shorter calm arrival: 1.5 s leaves room for 3)?

15. **Endless mode and the weekly challenge.** Endless is one 20-minute level with Specimen Vault's features and no
    feature starts, so it gets at most `per_level_max` (2) Hunters, wherever its rules find a bait, the first possibly
    early. The weekly challenge's Ice Lab weeks use Specimen Vault's features with Sentinel Row's counts (3 gantryways):
    1 or 2 Hunters a week. Should endless allow more (a count per minute)?

16. **Its health and weapons.** The owner: 25 laser hits. Placeholder: `health_early` / `health_late` 23, which laser
    tier 1 stretches to 25 shots (`PowerupTuning.tier1_extra_shots`); 17, 12 and 8 shots at tiers 2 to 4, the proportion
    every other enemy keeps (GDD §8). A falling gantryway destroys it whatever its health (the crush sees its chest,
    `aim_point`, with `hit_radius` 0.85 m: from under its disc to over its head).

17. **Its look.** Placeholder (`hunter_model.gd`, from the reference image): grey skin, a riveted black vest, a gas mask
    with three orange-red eyes, hoses, a thick riveted dark-steel disc with frost, worn patches and a thruster pod each
    side, four blue-white jets under it, and long serrated blades in the hazard red on bracers, from past the elbow to well
    past the fist. Only the eyes, blades and jets glow. Its death: sparks, steel debris and a small fireball.

18. **Its sounds** (`sfx_bank_hunter.gd`): the hover whine rising (arrival), the steel scrape (the warning), the flurry's
    swishes, the turbine winding down (giving up) and a crunch with a failing turbine and a small blast (death).

19. **The hint** (`hunter` in `data/hints/hints.json`): "The Hunter rides up beside you on its hover disc and stays
    there. When it raises its red blades with a scrape and your lane lights red, step one lane away from it: a jump won't
    clear its cuts. It follows into the lane you leave. Lead it under a gantryway: dash the pillar, step out away from it,
    and the walkway lands on it. Teleporters and ceilings shake it off until it tires."
