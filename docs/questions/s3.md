# S3, the armor's wait from any hit; coming off a wall moves one lane

The owner's two bug reports of October 23, 2026 (GDD §4, Free armor, as amended that day; GDD §3, Side walls and
"Extended wall runs in The Hush").

1. **A hit while the armor's wait runs** (GDD §4, Free armor; §8, Armor). GDD §4 now says any hit starts the wait and
   at its end the armor is back whole, and still says "after it breaks it comes back 30 seconds later". Placeholder: a
   hit the armor survives while its wait runs leaves the wait running (it doesn't start again), but the hit that
   breaks it starts the wait again, so broken armor is always back the tier's whole wait after it broke. Armor III
   (3 hits, 25 s): hits at 0 s and 10 s, back whole at 25 s; hits at 0, 10 and 20 s (broken), back at 45 s.
   `DamageRules.Armor.block()` in `scripts/core/damage_rules.gd`, `DESIGN-TBD`. Alternatives: (a) the wait keeps
   running through the break too (back at 25 s in the second example: 5 s after it broke, against "after it breaks it
   comes back 30 seconds later"); (b) every hit starts the wait again (back 25 s after the last hit). Each is a
   one-line change.
2. **A pickup's extra hit** (GDD §10, the standard armor rule; item 245's placeholder adds one hit over the armor's
   count to whole armor). Placeholder: the extra hit isn't part of the armor's own count. It goes first, and losing it
   leaves the armor whole, so no wait starts; the wait brings back the armor's own count, never the extra hit
   (`DamageRules.Armor.block()` and `tick()`, `DESIGN-TBD`). Alternative: losing it starts the wait, and the wait
   brings it back too.
3. **The HUD's worn armor** (GDD §4; the HUD's contents are open, OPEN_QUESTIONS §5). Placeholder: from any hit the
   armor's icon shows the ring filling in the kit's calm accent until it's back whole (then it flashes and
   `armor_back` plays, as for broken armor). While it still has hits left the icon stays lit, with its hits left on
   the badge and no shading over it (`CooldownIcon.lit_while_cooling`, set by `RunHud`); broken, it dims and shades as
   before. The shop's armor texts now say "whole again {seconds} s after a hit" (`data/shop/catalog.json`). Review:
   `screens_showcase.tscn -- --screen=hud_armor --tier=3 --worn`.
4. **Coming off a wall the game took the runner off** (GDD §3, Side walls; the owner: coming off the wall "must
   behave exactly as it does everywhere else in the game: one lane"). The cause: The Hush's notch at each platform's
   near end (item 1050) drops every standard wall run off onto the platform just as it reaches it, where the move to
   come off the wall is due, and that move, a moment after the drop, was a lane switch from the outer lane into the
   hole beside the platform. Placeholder: when the game takes the runner off a wall itself (the wall run's end, a wall
   gap, the notch, the Sewer Swarm's repel), the first move away from that wall made before they land, and within
   `wall_release_grace` (0.4 s, `data/tuning/movement.tres`, F6 Movement, Side walls; `Player._on_move`,
   `DESIGN-TBD`), is the dismount they meant and moves them no further: they're already coming off into the outer
   lane. A second move, or one after landing, switches lanes as it always did; a move back toward the wall still
   steps back on; a wall jump of the runner's own (the jump, or the move while on the wall) is unchanged. The drop
   from a notch takes about 0.2 to 0.35 s, so a move made once the runner has landed on the platform still takes them
   into the hole. Alternatives: a fixed window from the release, past the landing (it would cover a later move at the
   notch, but swallow a quick move inward just after an ordinary wall run's end); or a move in the window as a late
   wall jump, with its leap, rather than nothing.
