# S3: the Golden Zone's danger back to the approved rise

Questions raised by task S3 (the owner's answer to item 1207, October 23, 2026: "Raise the difficulty of the golden
zone"; GDD §3, Pace and busier levels, and §9.16). Every number is `test_danger_density`'s count (the enemies but the
cyborgs planted in charge paths; the obstacles: floor pieces and wall fences, with the dial over without it) over its
final band (Dead Zone 1, Sentinel Row and the Golden Palace) on each level's own seed and 9001-9012, as the suite now
samples it (item 5).

1. **The danger density pass is short of fair room in the Golden Zone, not short of its dial** (GDD §3: about 35%
   more by the final zones). Before this task, at 5 lanes the pass added half of what Sentinel Row's dial asked for
   and stopped where no fair spot was left, so a higher dial added nothing (Golden 1-3 at 0.40, the suite's MAX_DIAL,
   built the same band); 65% to 80% of a Golden build's playable track was closed to its encounters (a Resonator's
   whole visit, ceiling pads, drones, landing zones, a Buzz Overdrive, wall-run gaps, teleporters, the ends of the fill
   pass's stretches), and in what was left an added enemy's open lane over its window and clearance turned most spots
   away. The final band had x1.248, x1.267 and x1.259 for enemies at 3, 5 and 6 lanes (x1.242 at 5 lanes built
   without the Heavy Cyborg; three seeds had shown x1.33 before task S1, by luck). **Answered (owner, October 24,
   2026):** "relax the fairness rules in the golden zone" (GDD §9.16), never the open lane nor a warning. Built as
   items 2 to 4, in Golden 1-3's data only (`LevelConfig`; every other level's layout is byte for byte as before): the band
   then has x1.332, x1.320 and x1.335 for enemies (x1.266, x1.338 and x1.377 for obstacles, from x1.261, x1.331 and
   x1.374), and its 5-lane floor needs no allowance for the Heavy Cyborg (`HEAVY_CYBORG_ROOM` is gone). Any one of
   items 2 to 4 alone moves the 5-lane band little (x1.264 to x1.274); all three get it there, and less of any falls
   short or lands at the floor's edge (measured before the rows kept their whole margin, item 3: item 4 at half the
   clearance x1.303, at a quarter x1.317, at none x1.337; without item 2, or with item 3 at half, x1.294).
