# S3: the Golden Zone's danger back to the approved rise

Questions raised by task S3 (the owner's answer to item 1207, October 23, 2026: "Raise the difficulty of the golden
zone"; GDD §3, Pace and busier levels, and §9.16). Every number is `test_danger_density`'s count (the enemies but the
cyborgs planted in charge paths; the obstacles: floor pieces and wall fences, with the dial over without it) over its
final band (Dead Zone 1, Sentinel Row and the Golden Palace) on each level's own seed and 9001-9012, as the suite now
samples it (item 5).

1. **The danger density pass is short of fair room in the Golden Zone, not short of its dial** (GDD §3: about 35%
   more by the final zones). Before this task, at 5 lanes the pass added half of what Sentinel Row's dial asked for
   and stopped where no fair spot was left, so a higher dial added nothing (Golden 1-3 at 0.40, the suite's MAX_DIAL,
   built the same band); 65% to 80% of a Golden build's playable track was closed to its encounters (a Resonator's
   whole visit, ceiling pads, drones, landing zones, a Buzz Overdrive, wall-run gaps, teleporters, the ends of the fill
   pass's stretches), and in what was left an added enemy's open lane over its window and clearance turned most spots
   away. The final band had x1.248, x1.267 and x1.259 for enemies at 3, 5 and 6 lanes (x1.242 at 5 lanes built
   without the Heavy Cyborg; three seeds had shown x1.33 before task S1, by luck). **Answered (owner, October 24,
   2026):** "relax the fairness rules in the golden zone" (GDD §9.16), never the open lane, and no attack loses its
   warning. Built as items 2 to 4, in Golden 1-3's data only (`LevelConfig`; every other level's layout is byte for
   byte as before): the band then has x1.317, x1.309 and x1.332 for enemies (x1.263, x1.339 and x1.376 for
   obstacles, from x1.261, x1.331 and x1.374), and its 5-lane floor needs no allowance for the Heavy Cyborg
   (`HEAVY_CYBORG_ROOM` is gone). Any one of items 2 to 4 alone moves the 5-lane band little (x1.264 to x1.274); all
   three get it there, and less of any falls short or lands at the floor's edge (measured before the rows kept their
   whole margin and before item 3's 0.1 s: item 4 at half the clearance x1.303, at a quarter x1.317, at none x1.337;
   without item 2, or with item 3 at half, x1.294). Every counted feature keeps its count on the levels' own seeds
   but one, accepted in the task's review: Sentinel Row at 3 lanes has one Heavy Cyborg where it had two (a zone
   doodad reaches into the second one's stream zone, which gives it back), and the suite's minimum of one holds.
2. **A Resonator pulse by pulse** (item 1's answer). Placeholder (`danger_resonator_per_pulse`, on in Golden 1-3,
   DESIGN-TBD): the pass counts a planned Resonator's attack pulse by pulse there (each pulse from its warning until
   its last wave has passed the runner, with its plan's margin; `DangerDensity.resonator_pulse_windows`), so it may add
   between two pulses, where the Resonator only hovers, as `DangerDensityTuning.resonator_per_pulse` would everywhere
   (off there since its pulses shift at run time with the turns and floor waits it makes). Every pulse still waits for
   clear floor at run time (`Resonator.pulse_clear`: no gap, fence, floor enemy or doodad where its wave meets the
   runner), so each wave meets clear floor where it is jumped; it checks the floor only, not a window cyborg or bolts
   landing. A pulse that waited may come far from its plan, onto what the pass added, as one past a whole visit could
   under the old rules (item 8). Should the pass keep off a whole visit after all, in the Golden Zone too?
3. **Almost no margin from a big attack** (item 1's answer). Placeholder (`danger_big_attack_margin` 0 in Golden 1-3,
   DESIGN-TBD; 1 elsewhere): an enemy the pass adds there keeps only `DangerDensityTuning.big_attack_min_margin_seconds`
   (0.1 s of run, 2.5 m at the zone's 25 m/s), not the clearance (the level's spacing between two patterns, about
   0.9 s), between its attack window and a big attack's (a drone's barrage, a Resonator's pulse, a Buzz Overdrive's
   charge, a Gilded Sentinel's strike, a host's attack). An Enforcer Truck's volley has no attack window (its showing
   windows are calm stretches, where the pass adds nothing) and a host's chase is exempt everywhere (the tuning's
   `keep_out_exempt_features`), so neither is kept clear here or elsewhere. Its rows keep the whole clearance (a row
   at a Heavy Cyborg's keep-out made Sentinel Row give two of its Heavy Cyborgs back). What holds is the plan: an
   attack window is where the attack lands (a cyborg's from 2 s of run before it to its margin past it), and no window
   the pass adds overlaps a big attack's (`test_danger_density` checks every build): an encounter keeps the 0.1 s on
   both sides, an offset twin after itself (it starts no earlier than its anchor, the level's own enemy), and over
   the final band's Golden builds (each level's own seed and 9001-9012 at 3, 5 and 6 lanes) none of the 884 enemies
   the pass added came within it of a big attack's planned window (the least 2.50 m before one, 2.55 m after). The
   0.1 s (the task's review) keeps that in play too where a big attack runs a little late: a Gilded Sentinel's strike
   ended about 0.04 s past its plan in all 102 cases the review measured. Not the warnings: a cyborg starts charging
   at its engage distance, about 2 s before its window, so its charge and its bolts in flight may overlap a big
   attack, and at 25 m/s its bolts land about half a second or more from the big attack's window. Measured in play
   (Golden 3 at 6 lanes on its own seed): before the 0.1 s two window cyborgs at 2365.9 and 2368.0 m began charging
   1.1 s before a Gilded Sentinel's warning and fired during its strike, their bolts landing 0.48 s after it ended
   (the review); with it one at 2372.5 m begins charging 0.86 s before the warning and fires during the strike, its
   bolts landing 0.76 s after it. The two dodges don't conflict. Should a small threat keep more time after a big
   attack, or its warning off one?

8. **A Resonator's pulses drift far from their plan in play** (the task's review; not this task's, under the old rules
   and the new alike). The pass keeps what it adds off a Resonator's planned pulses (the Golden Zone, item 2) or its
   whole planned visit (everywhere else), but at run time a pulse a turn held waits for clear floor
   (`Resonator.pulse_clear`) and may then come far from its plan: in 36 Golden builds (Golden 1-3 at 3, 5 and 6 lanes
   on their own seeds and 9001-9003, played through), 59 of 160 pulses fell outside their planned windows with these
   rules and 57 of 162 with the old ones, so the pass's keep-out doesn't keep an added cyborg's bolts from landing
   during a pulse: with these rules (and item 3's 0.1 s) 8 bolts landed during one and 5 within half a second after
   it (6 and 5 with the old rules), every one beside a pulse outside its plan, and none within half a second of a
   Gilded Sentinel's strike or a Buzz Overdrive's charge. Each was dodgeable in the probes (a jump over the wave and
   a lane switch away from the bolts). The play-throughs showed the same for a drone's barrage far from its planned
   window (one that came in at 64 m still firing at 2,318 m in the Golden Palace at 6 lanes), under both rules.
   Options: leave it, or make a cyborg's gun (`CyborgGun`) hold a burst whose bolts would land during a live pulse.
   Which?
